## Monthly commitment reminders

Send them once a month, on the same day, with the goal name each person chose in their one-page plan. Evidence shows monthly reminders that mention the goal help people save more; an extra reminder after missing a deposit didn't help. On a broadcast channel, use the general version ("your goal"). Always include: "Reply STOP to stop receiving messages."

| Month | Message |
|---|---|
| 1 | Your goal "[goal]" starts today. Set aside [amount] on payday, before you spend. You can do this. |
| 2 | One month with your goal "[goal]". Did you set money aside this month? Every dollar counts. |
| 3 | Month three of "[goal]". If you missed a month, that's OK: start again on your next paycheck. |
| 4 | Check how much you have in "[goal]" and write it in your one-page plan. Seeing progress helps. |
| 5 | Five months with "[goal]". Is your transfer automatic? If not, set it up today. |
| 6 | Six months with "[goal]". Tell your trusted person how it's going. |
| 7 | Month 7 of "[goal]". If you got extra income, consider giving part of it to your goal. |
| 8 | Review your debts and your fund: is "[goal]" still the right priority? |
| 9 | Month 9 of "[goal]". Your consistency counts: the habit is yours now. |
| 10 | Two months left to close the year of "[goal]". How much is left? |
| 11 | Month 11 of "[goal]". Celebrate your progress, even if it's small. |
| 12 | One year with "[goal]". Update your one-page plan and name your next goal. |
