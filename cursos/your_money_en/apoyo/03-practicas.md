# Calculation practice

Solve each exercise showing the procedure, the result, the period and the limits of the calculation. The answers are at the end.

| Code | Lesson | Exercise |
|---|---|---|

| P02 | M1 U02 | A rate goes from 2% to 3%. Tell apart percentage points and relative increase. |

| P04 | M1 U02 | Principal 1,000 at 5% a year, compounded yearly, three years, no other activity. Calculate the balance. |

| P21 | M3 U09 | A tanda of 10 people, 80 a week, turn 4. How much do you receive and how much did you contribute before receiving? |
| P22 | M5 U10 | You sell 900 a month, spend 380 on supplies and work 26 hours. Profit and profit per hour. |

## Answers

- **P01:** 42. 7% = 0.07; 600 × 0.07 = 42.
- **P02:** it went up 1 percentage point; the relative increase is 50%.
- **P03:** interest 90; balance 1,590 (1,500 × 0.08 × 9/12).
- **P04:** 1,157.63 (1,000 × 1.05³). With simple interest it would be 1,150.
- **P05:** 1% monthly; 12.68% effective yearly.
- **P06:** 6% ((265 − 250) ÷ 250).
- **P07:** no. Prices keep going up, just more slowly.
- **P08:** tax 200; effective rate 13.33%; marginal rate of the last bracket 20%.
- **P09:** 200 for the transfer plus a 5 fee = 205 dollars.
- **P10:** 20%. It doesn't prove you can pay after your basic expenses or that you'll be approved.
- **P11:** interest 16; principal paid 84; balance 716.
- **P12:** 0.5 months. Don't include money you can't access.
- **P13:** gain 80; return 8% (3% from the payment and 5% from the price change).
- **P14:** net gain 50; net return 5%.
- **P15:** 1.05 ÷ 1.04 − 1 = 0.96%.
- **P16:** 990; a 1% loss even though the average is zero.
- **P17:** −5%. Receiving cash didn't prevent the loss.
- **P18:** gain 50. You need to know when the contribution was made to calculate a comparable rate.
- **P19:** 44% cumulative and 20% compound yearly (1.2 × 1.2 = 1.44).
- **P20:** 4,800 contributed. There's no interest: contributions aren't a return.
- **P21:** you receive 800; before receiving you contributed 240 (3 weeks × 80), and in total you'll contribute 800.
- **P22:** profit 520; 20 an hour before taxes.
