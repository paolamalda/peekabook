# Welcome

**Your Money, Your Family, Your Future** is a no-cost personal finance program for immigrants living in California. Here you'll learn to organize your money, send remittances for less, build your credit, protect yourself from fraud and prepare your future, here and in your home country.

You don't need to know about finance to start. Each lesson starts from an everyday situation, explains technical words when they come up and ends with an action you can take this week.

Version 3 · English edition · September 2026.

## How each module is built

| Part | What you'll find | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Lessons book** | Each lesson starts with a cover page where you choose your path. | 5 or 10 minutes per lesson |
| **What would you do?** | One activity per lesson: three situations where you choose the best decision. | 3 minutes |
| **Module self-assessment** | Questions from all the lessons in the module. You pass with 70% and can try as many times as you want. | 15 to 20 minutes |

## Choose your path in each lesson

- **Quick path (5 min):** read *The essentials* and go to *Practice*. Ideal if you're short on time or already know the topic.
- **Full path (10 min):** add *Go deeper*, with cases, common mistakes and more facts.

**Colored words** show their meaning when you hover over them or tap them on your phone. Answers are hidden: try first, then tap to see them.

## Three program rules

1. **You can use made-up data.** We will never ask for your Social Security number (SSN), your ITIN, your immigration status, passwords or account numbers.
2. **You don't have to buy anything.** You don't need to open accounts, sign up for products or leave a review to learn or get your certificate.
3. **A good decision can be to wait.** Comparing and deciding not to sign up is also learning.

## Points, levels and badges

- You earn **experience points** by completing activities and self-assessments. Points move you up levels: Getting started, Getting organized, Using my account, Caring for my credit, Protecting my family and Building my future.
- When you pass each module's self-assessment you get its **badge**. There are special badges for people who spot scams and compare by total cost.
- Nobody sees your name on a leaderboard. Points reward moving forward, not competing.

## Certificate of completion

When you pass all five self-assessments you get the **Complete plan** badge and your **Certificate of Completion**, which you can download as a PDF. It has a code to verify it online.

It's an educational recognition from the program: it isn't a professional license or an official accreditation.

## Information changes

Fees, requirements and rules change. Before you act, confirm the current information with the institution. Examples with numbers are simulations for learning; they aren't offers or personal recommendations.

## When to ask for professional help

This program teaches you to understand, compare and prepare questions. For a tax, legal, immigration, insurance or investment case that needs a personal recommendation, look for a licensed professional. **Where to find help** has no-cost and low-cost options.

> **Important:** this program **never** gives immigration advice. Only an attorney or a representative accredited by the Department of Justice can give it.

## Meet the characters

Throughout the program you'll follow six people. Their decisions, mistakes and successes will help you practice. They're made-up characters: any resemblance to real people is a coincidence.

- **Alex and Mar** live in Los Angeles with their two children. Alex is paid through payroll every two weeks and does some freelance work on weekends. Mar is paid every week and gets most of her tips in cash. They send money to Alex's mother in Michoacán.
- **Rubén** is 24, came from Oaxaca two years ago and works in a warehouse. He sends money to his mother and is starting his credit history.
- **Daniela** is from San Salvador and lives in San Diego with her son. She works by the hour and has a custom cake business.
- **Rosa** is 61, worked in Guadalajara and lives in Fresno with her daughter and grandchildren. She thinks about her health and her retirement.
- **Andrés** is from León, Guanajuato, and lives in Sacramento with his wife and daughter. He wants to buy a house.
