## Who can really help me with an immigration case?

**To read and check**

- **Official list of accredited representatives** (DOJ EOIR · English): [justice.gov](https://www.justice.gov/eoir/recognized-organizations-and-accredited-representatives-roster-state-and-city)  
  **What to look for:** the list for California and your city to find recognized organizations near you.
- **How to avoid immigration scams** (FTC · English and Spanish): [consumer.ftc.gov](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-immigration-scams-and-get-real-help)  
  **What to look for:** the list of fraud signs and where to get real help.
- **Resources for immigrant communities** (California Attorney General · English and Spanish): [oag.ca.gov](https://oag.ca.gov/immigrant)  
  **What to look for:** how to report a fraudulent notario or consultant in California.

**Sources for this lesson:** State Bar of California · California Secretary of State, immigration consultants · DOJ EOIR · California Attorney General · USCIS, no-cost forms.

## Does having insurance mean I won't pay anything?

**To read and check**

- **California Department of Insurance** (CDI · English and Spanish): [insurance.ca.gov](https://www.insurance.ca.gov)  
  **What to look for:** "Check a License" for an agent or insurer, and the guides to car and renters insurance.
- **Covered California** (Covered California · English and Spanish): [coveredca.com](https://www.coveredca.com)  
  **What to look for:** the "Shop and Compare" calculator to see plans and help paying based on your income.
- **Car insurance requirements** (California DMV · English and Spanish): [dmv.ca.gov](https://www.dmv.ca.gov)  
  **What to look for:** search for "insurance" to confirm the current minimum amounts.

**Sources for this lesson:** CDI · DMV, insurance requirements · FTB, health mandate · Covered California.

## Is this medical document asking me to pay?

**To read and check**

- **Hospital Fair Billing Program** (California HCAI · English): [hcai.ca.gov](https://hcai.ca.gov/affordability/hospital-fair-billing-program/)  
  **What to look for:** who qualifies for discounts and how to ask for them.
- **File a complaint about your plan** (DMHC · English and Spanish): [dmhc.ca.gov](https://www.dmhc.ca.gov/FileaComplaint.aspx)  
  **What to look for:** how to ask for an independent medical review if you're denied treatment.
- **Surprise medical bills** (CMS · English and Spanish): [cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/nosurprises)  
  **What to look for:** your rights in the emergency room and how to ask for a Good Faith Estimate.

**Sources for this lesson:** DMHC · HCAI, hospital fair pricing · CMS, No Surprises Act · California law on medical debt in credit reports (SB 1061).
