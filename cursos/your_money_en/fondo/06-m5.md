## Does having more money mean my investment made money?

**To read and check**

- **Investor.gov** (SEC · English and Spanish): [investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov)  
  **What to look for:** "Investing basics" and the explanation of risk and diversification.

**Sources for this lesson:** SEC Investor.gov · SEC, fees.

## Does opening an account mean I already invested?

**To read and check**

- **Check a broker or adviser** (FINRA BrokerCheck · English): [brokercheck.finra.org](https://brokercheck.finra.org)  
  **What to look for:** type the person's or company's name and check their registration and complaints.

**Sources for this lesson:** SEC, investment products · SEC, diversification · SEC, bonds · FTC, cryptocurrency fraud.

## Rent or buy? What rights do I have as a tenant?

**To read and check**

- **2026 Landlord-Tenant Guide** (California Department of Real Estate · English): [dre.ca.gov](https://www.dre.ca.gov/publications/ResourceGuidebook/2026_Landlord_Tenant_Guide.pdf)  
  **What to look for:** the chapter on rent caps, deposits and evictions.
- **Find a housing counselor** (CFPB and HUD · English): [consumerfinance.gov](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/find-a-housing-counselor/)  
  **What to look for:** type your ZIP code to see certified counselors near you and whether they offer help in Spanish.
- **Buying a house** (CFPB · English and Spanish): [consumerfinance.gov](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/)  
  **What to look for:** the step-by-step guide and the tool to compare mortgage rates.
- **CalHFA** (California Housing Finance Agency · English and Spanish): [calhfa.ca.gov](https://www.calhfa.ca.gov)  
  **What to look for:** the first-time homebuyer programs and their requirements.

**Sources for this lesson:** California, tenants' rights guide · CFPB, housing counselors · AB 12 (deposits, 2024) · AB 2747 (rent reporting) · CalHFA · AB 1840 (veto, 2024).

## What will I live on when I work less or stop working?

**To read and check**

- **my Social Security** (SSA · English and Spanish): [ssa.gov](https://www.ssa.gov/myaccount/)  
  **What to look for:** create your account to see your earnings record and an estimate of your benefits (you need an SSN).
- **CalSavers** (State of California · English and Spanish): [calsavers.com](https://www.calsavers.com)  
  **What to look for:** "For savers" to learn how it works, how much is deducted and how to change it.

**Sources for this lesson:** CalSavers · SSA, my Social Security · IRS, retirement plans.

## What happens to what I saved in Mexico?

**To read and check**

- **AforeMóvil** (CONSAR · Spanish): [gob.mx](https://www.gob.mx/consar)  
  **What to look for:** "AforeMóvil" to download the app and the steps to sign up from abroad.
- **e-SAR: find your AFORE** (SAR · Spanish): [e-sar.com.mx](https://www.e-sar.com.mx)  
  **What to look for:** "Localiza tu AFORE" and type your CURP.
- **Look up your CURP** (Government of Mexico · Spanish): [gob.mx](https://www.gob.mx/curp/)  
  **What to look for:** download your CURP if you don't have it handy.
- **International agreements** (SSA · English): [ssa.gov](https://www.ssa.gov/international/agreements_overview.html)  
  **What to look for:** the list of countries with an agreement; check whether Mexico appears.

**Sources for this lesson:** CONSAR, AforeMóvil · e-SAR · IMSS, Modalidades 40 and 33 · SSA, international agreements.

## What happens to my family if something happens to me?

**To read and check**

- **Caregiver's Authorization Affidavit** (California Courts · English and Spanish): [selfhelp.courts.ca.gov](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/guardianship/caregiver-affidavit)  
  **What to look for:** the form and the instructions to fill it out.
- **Advance health care directive** (California Attorney General · English and Spanish): [oag.ca.gov](https://oag.ca.gov/consumers/general/adv_hc_dir)  
  **What to look for:** the advance directive form.
- **Family preparedness plan** (Immigrant Legal Resource Center · English and Spanish): [ilrc.org](https://www.ilrc.org/family-preparedness-plan)  
  **What to look for:** the guide to prepare documents and contacts.
- **Mexican consulates** (SRE · Spanish): [directorio.sre.gob.mx](https://directorio.sre.gob.mx)  
  **What to look for:** your consulate and its notarial services.

**Sources for this lesson:** California Courts · California Attorney General · ILRC · SRE.

## How do I formalize my business?

**To read and check**

- **Start a business in California** (CalGold · English): [calgold.ca.gov](https://www.calgold.ca.gov)  
  **What to look for:** type your city and business type to see the permits you need.
- **LLC franchise tax** (FTB · English): [ftb.ca.gov](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html)  
  **What to look for:** the 800 minimum yearly tax and when it's due.
- **Small Business Development Centers** (SBA · English and Spanish): [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance)  
  **What to look for:** type your ZIP code to find no-cost advice near you.
- **Cottage food operations** (California Department of Public Health · English): [cdph.ca.gov](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CEH/DFDCS/Pages/FDBPrograms/FoodSafetyProgram/CottageFoodOperations.aspx)  
  **What to look for:** the list of allowed foods and how to register in your county.

**Sources for this lesson:** CalGold · FTB, LLC · SBA · CDPH, cottage food · IRS, self-employment.
