# Understand your money and organize your finances

## Is all the money I receive money I earned?

**To read and check**

- **Money Smart for Adults** (FDIC · English and Spanish): [fdic.gov](https://www.fdic.gov/consumer-resource-center/money-smart-adults)  
  **What to look for:** open the module on budgeting and managing money. Use it to practice separating income, expenses and debts.
- **Fake check scams** (FTC · English): [consumer.ftc.gov](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/fake-check-scams)  
  **What to look for:** the signs of a deposit "by mistake" and what to do before you send any money back (Case 3).
- **Check that a bank is insured** (FDIC BankFind · English): [banks.data.fdic.gov](https://banks.data.fdic.gov/bankfind-suite/)  
  **What to look for:** type the name of your bank, or your app's partner bank, and confirm it shows as insured.

**Sources for this lesson:** Federal Reserve, money supply · FTC, fake check scams · FDIC, deposit insurance.

## Why doesn't the same money go as far anymore?

**To read and check**

- **Consumer Price Index** (BLS · English): [bls.gov](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/)  
  **What to look for:** the latest yearly inflation number and the table by category (food, housing, transportation) to compare with your own expenses.
- **Compound interest calculator** (SEC Investor.gov · English): [investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/financial-tools-calculators/calculators/compound-interest-calculator)  
  **What to look for:** enter a starting amount, a monthly deposit and a rate to see how it grows over time.

**Sources for this lesson:** CFPB, compound interest · CFPB, how APY is calculated · BLS, Consumer Price Index.

## What do I need to take care of first?

**To read and check**

- **Your Money, Your Goals** (CFPB · English and Spanish): [consumerfinance.gov](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/your-money-your-goals/)  
  **What to look for:** the goal-setting tool and the "spending priorities" tool; you can download and print them.
- **Financial well-being questionnaire** (CFPB · English and Spanish): [consumerfinance.gov](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/financial-well-being/)  
  **What to look for:** answer the 10 questions to see your starting point; take it again when you finish the course.

**Sources for this lesson:** CFPB, Your Money, Your Goals · CFPB, financial well-being scale.

## Is getting paid every week the same as getting paid every month?

**To read and check**

- **Gig Economy Tax Center** (IRS · English and Spanish): [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/gig-economy-tax-center)  
  **What to look for:** the recordkeeping section, to know what to keep if you get paid through apps, in cash or with tips.

**Sources for this lesson:** IRS, gig economy · CFPB, Your Money, Your Goals.

## Why is my deposit smaller than what I earned?

**To read and check**

- **California Labor Commissioner** (DIR · English and Spanish): [dir.ca.gov](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/)  
  **What to look for:** the minimum wage section and the one on how to file a wage claim, with forms in several languages.
- **California minimum wage** (DIR · English): [dir.ca.gov](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/faq_minimumwage.htm)  
  **What to look for:** the current state minimum wage and the link to the list of cities with their own minimum wage.

**Sources for this lesson:** DIR, know your rights · California Labor Code, section 226 (pay stubs).

## Does filing taxes mean I'll get money back?

**To read and check**

- **IRS: Filing** (IRS · English and Spanish): [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/filing)  
  **What to look for:** who has to file and this year's deadline.
- **How to apply for an ITIN** (IRS · English and Spanish): [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/how-to-apply-for-an-itin)  
  **What to look for:** the list of accepted documents and the section on when an ITIN expires and how to renew it.
- **No-cost tax help in California** (FTB · English): [ftb.ca.gov](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/help/free-tax-help/index.html)  
  **What to look for:** the no-cost filing options and the finder for nearby locations.

**Sources for this lesson:** IRS, ITIN · IRS, dates and extensions · IRS, ITIN expiration.

## What tax benefits are there if I file with an ITIN?

**To read and check**

- **CalEITC** (FTB · English and Spanish): [ftb.ca.gov](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/credits/california-earned-income-tax-credit.html)  
  **What to look for:** this year's income limits table and the calculator to see if you qualify.
- **Young Child Tax Credit** (FTB · English): [ftb.ca.gov](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/credits/young-child-tax-credit.html)  
  **What to look for:** the child's age requirements and how much the credit can be.
- **Acceptance Agent finder** (IRS · English): [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/itin-acceptance-agents)  
  **What to look for:** filter by California and your city to find a nearby agent.
- **No-cost tax return preparation (VITA)** (IRS · English and Spanish): [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/free-tax-return-preparation-for-qualifying-taxpayers)  
  **What to look for:** the site locator by ZIP code and what documents to bring.

**Sources for this lesson:** IRS, ITIN · FTB, ITIN · FTB, no-cost tax help · IRS, VITA · FTB, CalFile.

## How do I choose someone to help me with taxes?

**To read and check**

- **Verify a registered preparer** (CTEC · English): [ctec.org](https://www.ctec.org)  
  **What to look for:** "Verify a Preparer"; type the preparer's name to confirm their registration is current.
- **IRS directory of preparers** (IRS · English): [irs.treasury.gov](https://irs.treasury.gov/rpo/rpo.jsf)  
  **What to look for:** search by ZIP code to find CPAs and enrolled agents (EAs) with verified credentials.

**Sources for this lesson:** FTB, no-cost help · FTB, Publication 982 · CTEC, preparer verification.

## What should I know before I apply for public benefits?

- **Medi-Cal** (California DHCS · English and Spanish): [dhcs.ca.gov](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov)

  **What to look for:** type your ZIP code to see nearby clinics that charge based on your income.

**Sources for this lesson:** USCIS, public charge · Protecting Immigrant Families · DHCS, Medi-Cal · HRSA, health centers.
