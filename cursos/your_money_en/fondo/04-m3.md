# Build your credit and manage your debts

## If they lend me money, does that mean I can pay it back?

**To read and check**

- **Credit reports and scores** (CFPB · English and Spanish): [consumerfinance.gov](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/)  
  **What to look for:** the difference between a report and a score, and what factors lenders use.
- **Auto loans** (CFPB · English and Spanish): [consumerfinance.gov](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/auto-loans/)  
  **What to look for:** the worksheet to calculate the total cost of the car with insurance and maintenance before you go to the dealership.

**Sources for this lesson:** CFPB, credit reports and scores · CFPB, auto loans · California Department of Insurance, auto insurance.

## How do I know what information is used about my debts?

**To read and check**

- **No-cost credit reports** (AnnualCreditReport.com · English and Spanish): [annualcreditreport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com)  
  **What to look for:** "Request your no-cost credit reports" to get all three; if you use an ITIN, the mail option.

**Sources for this lesson:** CFPB, checking your report · CFPB, how long information stays · FTC, disputes.

## How do I start if I have no credit history?

**Sources for this lesson:** CFPB, building or rebuilding credit · Mission Asset Fund · California AB 2747 (rent reporting, Civil Code 1954.07).

## What do I do if I know I won't be able to pay?

**To read and check**

- **Debt collection: your rights** (CFPB · English and Spanish): [consumerfinance.gov](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/debt-collection/)  
  **What to look for:** the sample letters to ask for validation of a debt and to ask them to stop calling you.
- **Legal help in California** (State Bar of California · English and Spanish): [calbar.ca.gov](https://www.calbar.ca.gov)  
  **What to look for:** "Find Legal Help" at no cost or low-cost legal aid near you.
- **Court self-help centers** (California Courts · English and Spanish): [selfhelp.courts.ca.gov](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov)  
  **What to look for:** "Debt lawsuits" to learn how to respond on time.

**Sources for this lesson:** CFPB, debt collectors · State Bar of California · California Code of Civil Procedure, time limits · California Code of Civil Procedure, section 360 · California Civil Code, section 1788.14 · DFPI, debt collectors.

## Can signing to help someone leave me with a debt?

**To read and check**

- **Cosigning a loan FAQs** (FTC · English): [consumer.ftc.gov](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/cosigning-loan-faqs)  
  **What to look for:** what happens if the other person doesn't pay and the text of the cosigner notice they must give you before you sign.
- **Child identity theft** (FTC · English and Spanish): [identitytheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov)  
  **What to look for:** the section for children and how to freeze a minor's credit.

**Sources for this lesson:** FTC, Cosigning a Loan FAQs · CFPB, debt collection.
