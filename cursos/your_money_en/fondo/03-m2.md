# Banks and sending money home

## Who takes care of my money and who provides the service?

**To read and check**

- **Submit a complaint** (CFPB · English and Spanish): [consumerfinance.gov](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/complaint/)  
  **What to look for:** the steps to complain about a bank, a payment app or a money transmitter, and what information to have ready.
- **California DFPI** (DFPI · English and Spanish): [dfpi.ca.gov](https://dfpi.ca.gov)  
  **What to look for:** "Verify a license" to confirm whether a financial company is licensed in California.

**Sources for this lesson:** Federal Reserve · CFPB, consumer complaints · DFPI, licenses.

## Is a money app always a bank?

**To read and check**

- **Find an insured credit union** (NCUA · English): [mapping.ncua.gov](https://mapping.ncua.gov)  
  **What to look for:** type the credit union's name or your ZIP code to see insured ones near you.
- **Deposit insurance and apps** (FDIC · English): [fdic.gov](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance)  
  **What to look for:** the section on apps and non-bank companies ("fintech") and what is and isn't insured.

**Sources for this lesson:** NCUA · FDIC, third-party apps · FDIC BankFind · FDIC, deposit insurance · NCUA, coverage.

## What ID works for financial paperwork?

**To read and check**

- **AB 60 license** (California DMV · English and Spanish): [dmv.ca.gov](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/assembly-bill-ab-60-driver-licenses/)  
  **What to look for:** the list of accepted documents to prove identity and residence, and how to make an appointment.
- **MiConsulado** (Mexico's Foreign Ministry · Spanish): [citas.sre.gob.mx](https://citas.sre.gob.mx)  
  **What to look for:** create your account and book a no-cost appointment for a consular ID or passport.
- **Checklist for opening a bank account** (CFPB · English): [files.consumerfinance.gov](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_checklist_opening_bank_account_web.pdf)  
  **What to look for:** the list of questions to ask the bank before you open the account.

**Sources for this lesson:** Financial institutions, ID requirements · California DMV, AB 60 · CFPB, checklist for opening an account.

## Does "no fee" always mean more money arrives?

**To read and check**

- **Remittance Prices Worldwide** (World Bank · English): [remittanceprices.worldbank.org](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org)  
  **What to look for:** choose "United States" to "Mexico" or "El Salvador" to compare the total cost of sending 200 dollars.
- **Remittance tax** (IRS · English): [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov)  
  **What to look for:** search for "remittance transfer excise tax" to read which transfers the 1% applies to.

**Sources for this lesson:** IRS, remittance tax · CONDUSEF, Remesamex · World Bank, remittance prices.

## What do I check before sending, and what do I do if something goes wrong?

**To read and check**

- **Directory of licensed money transmitters** (DFPI · English): [dfpi.ca.gov](https://dfpi.ca.gov/regulated-industries/money-transmitters/directory-of-money-transmitters/)  
  **What to look for:** the company's exact name in the list of authorized money transmitters.
- **NMLS Consumer Access** (NMLS · English): [nmlsconsumeraccess.org](https://www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org)  
  **What to look for:** type the company's name and check that it's licensed in California ("CA").

**Sources for this lesson:** CFPB, remittance rights · DFPI, license directory · NMLS Consumer Access.
