Module outcome: An 8-week money calendar, a budget that also works in a tough month, a tax folder and a 90-day plan.

Lessons: 14 · Estimated time: 70 to 140 minutes, plus activities and self-assessment.

  1. Is all the money I receive money I earned?
  2. Why doesn't the same money go as far anymore?
  3. What do I need to take care of first?
  4. Is getting paid every week the same as getting paid every month?
  5. Why is my deposit smaller than what I earned?
  6. Can a small purchase add up to a lot?
  7. Why am I short on money if I earn enough this month?
  8. How do I decide what to use my money for?
  9. Does filing taxes mean I'll get money back?
  10. Is everything I earn on my own for spending?
  11. What tax benefits are there if I file with an ITIN?
  12. How do I choose someone to help me with taxes?
  13. What should I know before I apply for public benefits?
  14. How do I turn what I learned into something I can actually do?

In this section: the lesson book, one "What would you do?" activity per lesson and the module self-assessment.
