The essentials

5 min

Filing is not the same as getting money back

Filing your tax return means reporting your information and calculating your tax for the year.

A refund only exists if you paid too much during the year. They're different things.

You paid too much or too little

The calculated tax is 100. During the year you already paid 120, because it was taken out of your wages.

120What you already paid
−
100What you owed
=
20Refund in your favor

If you had only paid 80, you would owe 20.

A deduction and a credit are not the same

Deduction
It reduces the income your tax is calculated on.
It lowers part of your tax.
Tax credit
It reduces your tax directly.
It lowers your tax dollar for dollar.

SSN and ITIN

The SSN is issued by Social Security. The ITIN is issued by the IRS to people who must file and can't get an SSN.

The ITIN doesn't give you permission to work or change your immigration status. Never use someone else's number or a made-up one.

Good to know: if you don't use your ITIN on a federal return for three years in a row, the IRS deactivates it. Renewing it can take months and delay your refund and your state credits, like CalEITC and YCTC.

Filing and paying have dates

There's a date to file your return and another to pay what you owe. They usually both fall in mid-April.

An extension to file usually does not give you more time to pay.

A case in one minute

Daniela is paid by the hour at her job, with withholding, and she also sells cakes, without withholding.

She does her tax return and finds she owes nothing for her job, but she does owe taxes on the cakes.

For next year, she sets aside part of each order in a digital "taxes" envelope. That way, when April comes, the money will already be there.

Do I have to file?

It depends on how much you earned, your filing status and how you earned your money.

If you work for yourself, in many cases you must file even if you earned little. And even if you don't have to, filing can get you credits you're entitled to.

Ask at VITA or a preparer with credentials whether you have to file this year.

Do it this week

  1. Put your W-2 and 1099 forms in a folder.
  2. Check whether your ITIN or SSN and your family's are active.
  3. Write down this year's deadline.

Check your understanding

Does filing your return guarantee a refund?

No. There's only a refund if you paid too much during the year.

Does the ITIN allow you to work?

No. It's only for meeting your tax obligations.

Remember

  • Filing is reporting; a refund only exists if you paid too much.
  • A credit lowers your tax more than a deduction of the same size.
  • Use your ITIN every year so it doesn't get deactivated.
