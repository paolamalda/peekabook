Module outcome: Your goals with amount, date and priority; savings, investment, housing and retirement decisions with full costs; your binational map; and an integrated financial plan.

Lessons: 12 · Estimated time: 60 to 120 minutes, plus activities and self-assessment.

  1. How do I turn a wish into a goal?
  2. Does having more money mean my investment made money?
  3. Does opening an account mean I already invested?
  4. Rent or buy? What rights do I have as a tenant?
  5. Is the advertised price all it costs?
  6. What will I live on when I work less or stop working?
  7. What happens to what I saved in Mexico?
  8. What happens to my family if something happens to me?
  9. How do I plan when my income changes every month?
  10. How do I formalize my business?
  11. What's my financial plan?
  12. What happens if I inherit or leave something across two countries?

In this section: the lesson book, one "What would you do?" activity per lesson and the module self-assessment.
