Module outcome: Your credit path, a reviewed report, two financing options compared, a debt inventory with a strategy and a payment calendar.

Lessons: 10 · Estimated time: 50 to 100 minutes, plus activities and self-assessment.

  1. If they lend me money, does that mean I can pay it back?
  2. How do I know what information is used about my debts?
  3. How do I start if I have no credit history?
  4. Does improving my credit mean erasing everything?
  5. Why can a small payment end up costing more?
  6. Which debt do I start with?
  7. What do I do if I know I won't be able to pay?
  8. Can signing to help someone leave me with a debt?
  9. Does a tanda help me build credit?
  10. What's my next step with credit?

In this section: the lesson book, one "What would you do?" activity per lesson and the module self-assessment.
