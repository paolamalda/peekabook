Connect your statement with your plan

Item Amount
Starting balance 1,000
Interest +20
New purchases +100
Your payment −150
Ending balance 970

You paid 150, but the debt only went down 30, because there was interest and new purchases. Always check all the items before judging your strategy.

Review at 30 and 90 days

At each review write down:

  • the action you took;
  • the answer you got;
  • what it cost you;
  • the obstacle you found;
  • your next step.

Protect what you built

Once you have a history, take care of it:

  • set up automatic minimum payments and pay the rest manually;
  • check your no-cost reports every few months;
  • freeze your credit if you don't plan to borrow soon (M4 U04).

Good to know: negative information doesn't stay on your report forever. Meanwhile, every on-time payment works in your favor.

What's next

In Module 4 you'll learn to protect your money, your identity and your family: scams, identity theft, insurance and a plan for emergencies.

What you achieved in this module

Now you know how to tell approval from ability to pay, read your report, build a history without going into debt, recognize repair scams, compare loans by total cost, organize your debts, negotiate and protect yourself from collectors, sign carefully and make your tanda count.

If you feel overwhelmed

If your debts keep you up at night, you're not alone. A nonprofit credit counseling agency can review your case at no cost or at low cost.

Asking for help in time avoids bigger problems.

Cases: think first, then check

Case 1. Mar's balance

Starting balance 1,000; interest 20; new purchases 100; payment 150.

What is the ending balance?

970.

Why did it go down so little?

Because of the interest and the new purchases. If she stops using the card while she pays, the balance will go down faster.

Case 2. Andrés's error

Andrés disputes the card he didn't open and freezes his credit.

How does he know he made progress?

He has the dispute confirmation number and the freeze confirmation, even if the answer takes 30 days.

Case 3. Daniela's collection account

Daniela has a medical bill in collections.

What is her first step?

Ask for validation of the debt in writing and ask the hospital about financial assistance.
