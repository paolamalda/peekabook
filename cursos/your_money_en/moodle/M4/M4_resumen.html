Module outcome: Your pause rule, a fraud response protocol, a review of your coverage, a staged reserve and your family preparedness plan.

Lessons: 11 · Estimated time: 55 to 110 minutes, plus activities and self-assessment.

  1. Why is someone pushing me to act right now?
  2. Who can really help me with an immigration case?
  3. What protects my accounts besides a password?
  4. What do I do if I already shared information or see something strange?
  5. Is talking about money the same as controlling someone?
  6. What is setting aside a little money for?
  7. Does having insurance mean I won't pay anything?
  8. Is this medical document asking me to pay?
  9. How do I pay for the essentials if I can't work for a few days?
  10. What happens to my family and my money if I can't be there?
  11. How do I know if my protection plan works?

In this section: the lesson book, one "What would you do?" activity per lesson and the module self-assessment.
