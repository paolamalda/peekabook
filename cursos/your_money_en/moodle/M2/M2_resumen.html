Module outcome: A map of institutions, accounts compared by how you use money, three comparable remittance quotes, a sending calendar and a family agreement.

Lessons: 13 · Estimated time: 65 to 130 minutes, plus activities and self-assessment.

  1. Who takes care of my money and who provides the service?
  2. Is a money app always a bank?
  3. What ID works for financial paperwork?
  4. What do I need to open an account, and how much does it cost to keep it?
  5. Is all the balance I see available to spend?
  6. How do I support my family without deciding at the last minute?
  7. How much can I send, and when?
  8. Does "no fee" always mean more money arrives?
  9. What do I check before sending, and what do I do if something goes wrong?
  10. What happens when I borrow to send money?
  11. How do I say how much I can help without promising too much?
  12. How do I save for something that will be paid in pesos?
  13. How do I bring my account and my remittances into one plan?

In this section: the lesson book, one "What would you do?" activity per lesson and the module self-assessment.
