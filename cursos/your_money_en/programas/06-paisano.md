# If you travel to Mexico: Héroes Paisanos

The **Héroes Paisanos** program guides and protects you when you travel to Mexico during holiday seasons. During those periods, by land you can bring up to 500 dollars in goods without paying taxes, besides your personal items. If your family travels together in the same vehicle, your allowances can be combined.

If an official asks you for money, don't pay: report it at the program's help stations.

> **Current fact:** the winter period runs from November 25, 2026 to January 8, 2027. Accessed October 6, 2026 through Mexico's National Customs Agency and news outlets.
