# Dates to keep in mind

Each date disappears on its own once it has passed.

- **October 15, 2026 to October 18, 2026:** **SNEF 2026** in Mexico City, at no cost. Its online platform stays open until March 31, 2027.
- **November 20, 2026:** **«Directo a México» ends**, the Bank of Mexico's transfer service. If you used it, choose another way to send and compare how much your family receives.
- **November 25, 2026 to January 8, 2027:** **Héroes Paisanos, winter:** by land you can bring up to 500 dollars in goods tax-free.
- **January 26, 2027 to April 15, 2027:** **U.S. tax season:** the deadline is April 15, 2027. Look for no-cost help at VITA.
- **March 31, 2027:** **The SNEF 2026 online platform closes.**
