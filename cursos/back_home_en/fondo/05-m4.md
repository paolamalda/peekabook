# What you left in the United States

## Your last paycheck and your account back there

**To read and check**

- **Consular protection** (SRE · Spanish): [consulmex.sre.gob.mx](https://consulmex.sre.gob.mx)  
  **What to look for:** «protección» (protection) and the consulate for the city where you worked.
- **Unclaimed property** (NAUPA · English): [unclaimed.org](https://unclaimed.org)  
  **What to look for:** the state where you lived.

**Sources for this lesson:** SRE, consular protection; NAUPA, unclaimed property; checked October 5, 2026.

## Your U.S. taxes

**To read and check**

- **Refunds** (IRS · English and Spanish): [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov)  
  **What to look for:** «refund» and «taxpayers living abroad».

**Sources for this lesson:** IRS, refunds and the deadline to claim them; checked October 5, 2026.

## Your U.S. retirement: 401(k) and Social Security

**To read and check**

- **Federal Benefits Unit** (U.S. Embassy in Mexico · English and Spanish): [mx.usembassy.gov](https://mx.usembassy.gov)  
  **What to look for:** «Federal Benefits Unit».
- **Social Security** (SSA · English): [ssa.gov](https://www.ssa.gov)  
  **What to look for:** «payments outside the U.S.».

**Sources for this lesson:** IRS, early withdrawals from retirement plans; SSA, payments outside the U.S.; checked October 5, 2026.
