# Your account in Mexico

## Your returnee cards

**To read and check**

- **FINABIEN** (Government of Mexico · Spanish): [gob.mx](https://www.gob.mx/finabien)  
  **What to look for:** «Tarjeta FINABIEN Paisano».

**Sources for this lesson:** SEGOB, México te Abraza; Ministry of Welfare, Tarjeta Bienestar Paisano; FINABIEN, Tarjeta FINABIEN Paisano; checked October 5, 2026.

## Choose an account with no fees

**To read and check**

- **SIPRES** (CONDUSEF · Spanish): [webapps.condusef.gob.mx](https://webapps.condusef.gob.mx/SIPRES)  
  **What to look for:** the institution's name.
- **Basic accounts** (CONDUSEF · Spanish): [condusef.gob.mx](https://www.condusef.gob.mx)  
  **What to look for:** «cuenta básica» (basic account).

**Sources for this lesson:** CONDUSEF, basic accounts and SIPRES; checked October 5, 2026.
