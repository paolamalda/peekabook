# Work or a business with what you know how to do

## What you know how to do has value

**To read and check**

- **Servicio Nacional de Empleo** (STPS · Spanish): [empleo.gob.mx](https://www.empleo.gob.mx)  
  **What to look for:** «repatriados» (repatriated people).
- **Skills certification** (CONOCER · Spanish): [conocer.gob.mx](https://conocer.gob.mx)  
  **What to look for:** «certifícate» (get certified).

**Sources for this lesson:** STPS, Servicio Nacional de Empleo; CONOCER; checked October 5, 2026.
