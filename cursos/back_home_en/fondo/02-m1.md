# Your papers

## Your repatriation record and your CURP: the first two papers

**To read and check**

- **Look up your CURP** (Government of Mexico · Spanish): [gob.mx](https://www.gob.mx/curp)  
  **What to look for:** «consulta e impresión» (look up and print).
- **México te Abraza** (SEGOB · Spanish): [portales.segob.gob.mx](https://portales.segob.gob.mx/es/PoliticaMigratoria/Mexico-te-abraza)  
  **What to look for:** «servicios» (services).

**Sources for this lesson:** SEGOB, México te Abraza; INM, Record of Reception of Repatriated Mexicans; checked October 5, 2026.

## Your birth certificate and your INE

**To read and check**

- **Birth certificate online** (Government of Mexico · Spanish): [gob.mx](https://www.gob.mx/actas)  
  **What to look for:** «copia certificada» (certified copy).
- **ID requirements** (INE · Spanish): [ine.mx](https://www.ine.mx)  
  **What to look for:** «requisitos credencial para votar» (voter ID requirements).

**Sources for this lesson:** INE, requirements for the voter ID; Government of Mexico, birth certificates online; checked October 5, 2026.

## Papers for your children born in the United States

**To read and check**

- **Dual citizenship** (SRE · Spanish): [gob.mx](https://www.gob.mx/sre)  
  **What to look for:** «inscripción de nacimiento doble nacionalidad» (birth registration, dual citizenship).
- **School enrollment** (SEP · Spanish): [gob.mx](https://www.gob.mx/sep)  
  **What to look for:** «inscripción niñas y niños migrantes» (enrollment of migrant children).

**Sources for this lesson:** SRE, dual citizenship; SEP, enrollment rules for migrant children; checked October 5, 2026.
