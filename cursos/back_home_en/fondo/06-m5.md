# Your Afore, your IMSS and your weeks

## Do you have an Afore? Find it

**To read and check**

- **Locate your Afore** (CONSAR · Spanish): [gob.mx](https://www.gob.mx/consar)  
  **What to look for:** «localiza tu Afore» (locate your Afore) and «AforeMóvil». SARTEL 55 1328 5000.

**Sources for this lesson:** CONSAR, account location and AforeMóvil; checked October 5, 2026.

## Your contribution weeks

**To read and check**

- **Contribution weeks certificate** (IMSS · Spanish): [serviciosdigitales.imss.gob.mx](https://serviciosdigitales.imss.gob.mx)  
  **What to look for:** «semanas cotizadas» (contribution weeks).

**Sources for this lesson:** IMSS, contribution weeks certificate and Ley 73 and Ley 97 pension requirements; checked October 5, 2026.

## Save for retirement even if you're self-employed

**To read and check**

- **Self-employed workers** (IMSS · Spanish): [imss.gob.mx](https://www.imss.gob.mx)  
  **What to look for:** «personas trabajadoras independientes» (self-employed workers).

**Sources for this lesson:** CONSAR, voluntary savings; IMSS, enrollment of self-employed workers; checked October 5, 2026.
