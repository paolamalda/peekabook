# Integrated cases

The cases use made-up data. Solve each case before opening the key.

## E1. Don Rafa's first week (Modules 1 and 2)

**Data.** Don Rafa arrived with his repatriation record and the Tarjeta Bienestar Paisano with 2,000 pesos. The bus to his town costs 650, his birth certificate and copies 250, and food for the week 600. Outside the center, a man charges 500 to «get his CURP».

**Tasks.**

1. How much is left of the 2,000?
2. Does he pay the man for the CURP?
3. Which card does his son use to send him money from Dallas?
4. Which account does he ask for at the bank, and what does he check first?

**Key.**

- 2,000 minus 1,500: 500. The support comes only once.
- No: looking up and printing the CURP on gob.mx has no cost.
- The Tarjeta FINABIEN Paisano, which works as an account.
- The basic account; he checks the institution in CONDUSEF's SIPRES and asks for the summary sheet.

## E2. What was left in Texas (Modules 3 and 4)

**Data.** His boss owes Don Rafa a week: 900 dollars. His account there has 1,400 dollars and charges 12 a month. 2,400 dollars in taxes were withheld last year and he owed 1,700. An «advisor» offers him «good credit in Mexico» if he pays 400 pesos for his report.

**Tasks.**

1. What does he do about the week he's owed?
2. How much does he lose in a year if he leaves the account forgotten?
3. How much would his refund be?
4. What does he do about the credit report?

**Key.**

- He asks for it by deposit or by check to someone he trusts; the consulate guides him if he isn't paid.
- 12 times 12: 144 dollars.
- 2,400 minus 1,700: 700 dollars.
- He requests it at no cost on the official website, once a year from each bureau; in Mexico he starts from zero.

## E3. Lupita's weeks (Modules 5 and 6)

**Data.** Lupita worked in Mexico from 2003 to 2007 with IMSS (about 210 weeks). Now 9,000 comes in each month and her basics are 7,600. Her family asks her for 1,500 for a welcome party.

**Tasks.**

1. Which law applies to her, and how many weeks does it require in 2026?
2. How many is she missing?
3. How much is left each month after the basics?
4. What does she say about the party?

**Key.**

- Ley 97, because she started after July 1997: 875 weeks in 2026.
- 875 minus 210: 665.
- 9,000 minus 7,600: 1,400.
- She suggests something simple with a cap that doesn't put her 90 days at risk.

## E4. Memo's job (Modules 7 and 8)

**Data.** Memo is offered 2,400 a week with IMSS or 2,700 in cash with no paperwork. Another «job» asks him for 1,200 for the uniform before starting. He gets a call: «We have your cousin at the border, deposit 8,000».

**Tasks.**

1. What is his minimum aguinaldo at the formal job?
2. What else does the formal job add?
3. What does he do about the job that charges for the uniform?
4. What does he do about the call?

**Key.**

- 2,400 divided by 7 is about 343 a day; times 15 days: about 5,145.
- IMSS, Afore, vacation and Infonavit; and weeks toward his pension.
- He doesn't pay: a real job doesn't charge to hire you.
- He hangs up and calls his cousin with a number he already has; he reports it to 089.
