# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the course words, grouped by module.

## Your papers

- **Apostille:** a seal that recognizes a foreign document.
- **Birth registration:** recording in Mexico a birth that happened abroad.
- **Certified copy:** an official copy that counts as the original.
- **CURP:** Mexico's unique population ID code.
- **Dual citizenship:** being a citizen of two countries at once.
- **INE office (módulo):** the office where you apply for the INE.
- **Proof of address:** a paper that shows where you live.
- **Record (constancia):** an official paper that proves something.
- **Repatriation:** a person's return to their country.

## Your account in Mexico

- **Basic account:** an account with no opening or maintenance fees.
- **CLABE:** the 18-digit number for transfers.
- **Fee:** a charge for a service.
- **One-time support:** money given only once.
- **PIN:** your card's secret number.
- **Reference number (folio):** the number that proves your claim.
- **Remittance:** money a relative sends from another country.
- **SPEI:** the system for transfers between banks.
- **Summary sheet (carátula):** the summary of the contract.

## Credit from scratch

- **CAT:** the credit's total annual cost.
- **Collection harassment:** illegal pressure to make you pay.
- **Credit bureau:** an authorized company that keeps that record.
- **Credit history:** a record of how you've repaid what you borrowed.
- **Credit limit:** the most they'll lend you.
- **Minimum payment:** the least you can pay without being late; it generates interest.
- **Special report:** your full history, at no cost once a year.
- **Term:** the time you have to pay.
- **Total cost:** everything you pay, minus what you borrowed.

## What you left in the United States

- **401(k):** a retirement plan from a U.S. job.
- **Consulate:** Mexico's office in another country.
- **Last paycheck:** wages still owed when a job ends.
- **Penalty:** an extra charge for withdrawing early.
- **Refund:** what you get back if you paid too much.
- **Social Security credit:** a unit that counts the work you paid into.
- **Unclaimed property:** forgotten money the state holds.
- **W-2:** a form with your yearly wages and withholdings.
- **Withholding:** tax taken out of each paycheck.

## Your Afore, your IMSS and your weeks

- **Afore:** the company that manages your retirement savings.
- **Automatic debit:** an automatic charge to your account.
- **Beneficiary:** the person who receives your savings if you pass away.
- **Contribution weeks:** weeks worked with IMSS.
- **Ley 73 and Ley 97:** pension rules based on when you started contributing.
- **NSS:** social security number.
- **Return:** what your savings earn over time.
- **Self-employed worker:** someone who works without a boss.
- **Voluntary contribution:** money you add to your Afore yourself.

## Your first 90 days

- **Budget:** a plan for what comes in and goes out.
- **Cap:** the most you'll spend.
- **Certain income:** money you know will arrive.
- **Cut:** an expense you remove or reduce.
- **Limit:** how far you can go without harming yourself.
- **Little savings (guardadito):** small savings for the unexpected.
- **Obligation:** an expense you feel you're expected to make.
- **Scholarship:** support to study.

## Work or a business with what you know how to do

- **Aguinaldo:** a yearly payment of at least 15 days' wages.
- **Benefits (prestaciones):** what you receive besides the wage.
- **Certification:** an official paper that recognizes what you know.
- **Cost:** what it costs you to make or buy what you sell.
- **IMSS registration (alta):** the record of your job for social security.
- **Job board:** a list of available jobs.
- **Price:** what you charge.
- **Profit:** what you keep after the cost.
- **Skill:** something you know how to do well.

## Don't get scammed when you return

- **Anonymous report:** a report without giving your name.
- **Code word:** a family password to confirm emergencies.
- **Extortion:** a threat to make you hand over money.
- **Fixer (gestor):** a person who does paperwork for others.
- **Next action:** the next concrete step.
- **Original:** the real document, not the copy.
- **Plan:** a list of what you'll do and when.
- **Review:** looking again at how you're doing.
- **Service window (ventanilla):** the official place where you're served.
