# Bienestar pensions

If you or someone in your family meets the age or condition, they can receive one of these pensions. They're paid every two months to a Banco del Bienestar card:

- **Mujeres Bienestar:** women aged 60 to 64, 3,100 pesos.
- **Older Adults:** from age 65, 6,400 pesos.
- **People with Disabilities:** 3,300 pesos. In 24 states it covers ages 0 to 64; in the rest, ages 0 to 29.

Registration opens in periods: ask the Servidores de la Nación or your municipality's office. It's at no cost, and nobody can ask you for part of your pension.

> **Current fact:** 2026 amounts; the last national registration for older adults and Mujeres Bienestar was June 22 to 28, 2026. Accessed October 6, 2026 through Programas para el Bienestar and national news outlets.
