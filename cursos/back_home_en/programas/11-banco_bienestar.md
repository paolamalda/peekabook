# Banco del Bienestar

**Banco del Bienestar** is a government bank. Many pensions, scholarships and support payments are deposited there, and you can also open your own savings account:

- you open it with **50 pesos**;
- it **doesn't charge** to open or keep the account, and there's no minimum balance;
- it has branches and ATMs in rural communities too;
- its app shows your balance and transactions, and in 2026 it adds transfers with no fee.

Bring your ID and your CURP. Nobody from the bank will ask for your PIN or codes by phone.

> **Current fact:** a Banco del Bienestar savings account opens with 50 pesos, with no opening or maintenance fee. Accessed October 6, 2026 through national news outlets; confirm at a branch or on the official site.
