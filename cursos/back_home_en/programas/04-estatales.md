# Support from your state and municipality

Besides federal programs, **your state and your municipality** (or your alcaldía, if you live in Mexico City) have their own support: for women, young people, older adults, small businesses, unemployment, housing or farming. They change every year and many open registration for only a few weeks, so we don't describe them one by one here: we tell you where to look.

**Where to look**

- On your **state's official site** (table below): look for the welfare or social development ministry, the DIF, the women's institute and the employment service.
- In your **municipality or alcaldía:** ask at the town hall, the municipal DIF or the citizen service office. Many post updates on their official social media.

**What to ask before you register**

- Is it support you don't pay back, or a loan you have to repay?
- What are the requirements and registration dates?
- How much do I get in total, and how often?

**Scam warning signs:** being charged to sign up, a fixer who offers to "get" you the support, or being asked for part of what you receive. Official registrations are at no cost.
