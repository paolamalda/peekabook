# Women's Ministry: LIBRE Centers and Line 079

Mexico's **Women's Ministry** (Secretaría de las Mujeres, formerly Inmujeres) offers two kinds of support at no cost:

- **LIBRE Centers:** places in your municipality with emotional support, legal guidance and training so you can have your own income. There are more than a thousand in all 32 states.
- **Women's Line 079, option 1:** legal guidance and emotional support 24 hours a day, with immediate referral if you need it.

You can go even if you're not sure what you're living through is violence. If you're in danger, call 911.

> **Current fact:** in 2026 there are 1,001 LIBRE Centers in all 32 states, in 40% of the country's municipalities. Accessed October 6, 2026 through the Women's Ministry and national news outlets.
