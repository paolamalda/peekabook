# Vivienda para el Bienestar (housing subsidy)

**Vivienda para el Bienestar**, from CONAVI, is a subsidy to get a home. People over 18 can register if they:

- don't own a home;
- don't have an active housing loan (Infonavit, Fovissste or other);
- earn less than 17,800 pesos a month.

Priority goes to women heads of household, older adults, people with disabilities, and Indigenous and Afro-Mexican people. Bring your ID, CURP, proof of address and proof of income. Registration is in person and at no cost: **you don't need a fixer**, and any charge is irregular.

> **Current fact:** in 2026 the call is open in 26 states. Accessed October 6, 2026 through CONAVI and national news outlets.
