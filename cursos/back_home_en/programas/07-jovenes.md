# Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro

If you're 18 to 29 and not studying or working, you can train at a workplace with **Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro**. You get a monthly payment and IMSS health coverage while you learn a trade.

Registration is online and at no cost. Be wary of anyone who charges you for a spot.

> **Current fact:** in 2026 the payment is 9,582.47 pesos a month. A new placement period opened on October 1, 2026. Accessed October 6, 2026 through national news outlets.
