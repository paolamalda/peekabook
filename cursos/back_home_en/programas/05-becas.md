# Student scholarships

If there are public-school students in your home, check these scholarships:

- **Beca Rita Cetina:** for elementary and middle school students. Amounts change by grade level.
- **Beca Benito Juárez:** for high school students, 1,900 pesos every two months.

They're paid to a Banco del Bienestar card. Registration is at no cost, online or at school, on the dates of each call.

> **Current fact:** in 2026, the Beca Benito Juárez pays 1,900 pesos every two months, five times a year. Accessed October 6, 2026 through Programas para el Bienestar.
