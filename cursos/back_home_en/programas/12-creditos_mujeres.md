# Word-of-honor loans for women (FINABIEN)

**Financiera para el Bienestar (FINABIEN)** offers **interest-free loans with no cosigner** for women. There are three types:

- **Self-employment for Women:** 10,000 pesos for your activity or business, paid over 11 months, with one month of grace.
- **Solidarity groups:** for groups of at least 5 women, from 50,000 to 300,000 pesos for a shared project.
- **Crédito Libertad Mujer:** for women who have lived through violence and want their own income. In most places it's 10,000 to 30,000 pesos; in some states, by agreement, it can be more. You can ask about it at a LIBRE Center.

They usually ask for ID, CURP, proof of address and a photo of your business or activity. **They're not open in every state at the same time:** each opens by agreement with the state. Registration is at no cost.

Even with no interest, it's a loan: make your payment plan before you apply, and don't use it for household expenses.

> **Current fact:** in 2026 registration is open in states such as Michoacán, Morelos, the State of Mexico, Querétaro and Tlaxcala. Accessed October 6, 2026 through FINABIEN, state governments and national news outlets.
