# Support for working mothers and fathers

The **Program for the Well-being of Children of Working Mothers** supports mothers, single fathers and guardians who work, study or are looking for work and **don't have childcare** through IMSS or ISSSTE.

- **1,650 pesos every two months** for each child aged 0 to 4.
- Up to **3,720 pesos** every two months if the child has a disability, ages 0 to 6.
- Up to three children per household.

Bring your ID, your CURP and each child's, their birth certificates and proof of address. Registration is at no cost.

> **Current fact:** 2026 amounts; starting this year, single fathers and guardians can also register. Accessed October 6, 2026 through Programas para el Bienestar and national news outlets.
