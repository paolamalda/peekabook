# Where to get no-cost guidance

If you have a problem, these institutions guide you at no cost, and you don't need a lawyer to ask:

- **CONDUSEF:** problems with banks, cards, loans, insurance or your Afore. 55 5340 0999.
- **PROFECO:** purchases, services, pawnshops and contracts. 55 5568 8722.
- **PROFEDET:** dismissals, wages, IMSS and Infonavit. 800 911 7877.
- **PRODECON:** problems with the SAT. 55 1205 9000.
- **Public defender's office:** if you need a lawyer and can't pay for one.
- **Procuraduría Agraria:** questions about ejido and communal land.

Always ask for a case number and keep it.
