# México te Abraza: if you return to Mexico

**México te Abraza** ("Mexico Embraces You") is the Mexican government's program to receive people sent back from the United States. More than 30 government agencies take part. At the care centers and arrival points on the border and at some airports, they can help you with:

- **food, a place to stay and transport** to your state;
- your **CURP and birth certificate**;
- **IMSS enrollment** so you can get medical care;
- the **Tarjeta Bienestar Paisano**, a card with 2,000 pesos for travel and first expenses;
- **jobs**, through the National Employment Service;
- **counseling and legal guidance**.

All these services are **at no cost**. Keep the record the INM gives you when you arrive: you'll need it for your paperwork. If you came back on your own, ask your state's office for migrants which support applies to you.

> **Current fact:** México te Abraza continues in 2026. From January 20, 2025 to August 13, 2026 it received 271,193 people sent back from the U.S. It gave out 180,042 Bienestar Paisano cards, made 98,698 IMSS enrollments and 138,669 CURP and birth certificate procedures, and connected 18,644 people with a formal job. Accessed October 6, 2026 through Mexico's Ministry of the Interior (SEGOB) and national news outlets.
