# Back Home, Your Money, Your Future

Program manual · Version 1.0 · Desarrolla Talento · October 2026

Financial well-being program for people returning from the United States to Mexico, whether deported or by their own decision: 8 modules and 24 short lessons. Hybrid format: one in-person kickoff session and the rest online. Also available in Spanish and as a bilingual group.

# 1. General information

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Program | Back Home, Your Money, Your Future |
| Audience | Mexican people returning from the United States, and their families, who prefer English or are more comfortable in English (for example, people who grew up in the U.S.). |
| Tone | Warm and direct, plain English, short sentences, no jargon or blame |
| Format | Hybrid: 1 in-person session of 3 hours and 2 hours online (5 hours total). |
| Length | 8 modules, 24 lessons of 5 to 10 minutes |

## The problem it solves

- They arrive without their papers at hand, or with U.S. papers that can't be used here to open an account.
- Their U.S. credit history doesn't count in Mexico, and they're targeted by expensive «no credit check» loans.
- They leave wages, accounts, tax refunds and retirement savings in the U.S. without knowing how to recover them.
- They don't know whether they have an Afore or how many weeks they contributed before leaving.
- In the first months little money comes in, and savings go to family obligations and loans.
- Fake fixers, fraud and extortion target people who have just returned.

## Program rules

1. **Educate, don't sell:** lessons don't recommend banks, lenders or products; they teach how to verify any institution with CONDUSEF.
2. **No SOFIPO, SOCAP or SOFOM recommendations.**
3. **Never ask for personal data**, passwords, PINs or real debt or savings amounts.
4. **Financial well-being program:** that's the name used in all materials.
5. **Nothing is free or a gift:** public services are described as «at no cost».
6. **Every figure with a date and source**; where two official sources differ, both are cited.
7. **Names not used for characters:** Jorge, José, Ángeles, David, Luis, Ana, Jonathan, Paola, Ernesto, Marisa, Tomás, Leonardo, Esther, Liliana and Alberto (the generator's check detects them).
8. **No immigration or legal advice is given:** participants are referred to consulates, the Embassy and public offices.

# 3. Lessons

## Module 1. Your papers

| Code | Lesson | What the person achieves |
|---|---|---|
| M1 U01 | Your repatriation record and your CURP: the first two papers | Explain what the repatriation record is for and how to get your CURP to start any paperwork. |
| M1 U02 | Your birth certificate and your INE | Get your birth certificate and apply for your INE voter ID, which the bank and employers will ask for. |
| M1 U03 | Papers for your children born in the United States | Register your U.S.-born children in Mexico so they have a CURP, school and services. |

## Module 2. Your account in Mexico

| Code | Lesson | What the person achieves |
|---|---|---|
| M2 U01 | Your returnee cards | Tell apart the Tarjeta Bienestar Paisano and the Tarjeta FINABIEN Paisano, and use the 2,000 pesos well. |
| M2 U02 | Choose an account with no fees | Compare basic accounts and choose one in your name that doesn't charge you for keeping your money. |
| M2 U03 | Getting paid, paying and disputing | Use your account to get paid and pay safely, and know how to dispute a charge you didn't make. |

## Module 3. Credit from scratch

| Code | Lesson | What the person achieves |
|---|---|---|
| M3 U01 | Your U.S. credit history doesn't count here | Explain that in Mexico you start with no credit history, and check your report at no cost. |
| M3 U02 | Start small and pay on time | Build a credit history in Mexico with a small credit you can pay in full and on time. |
| M3 U03 | «No credit check» loans and apps that overcharge | Recognize the expensive loans that target people with no history, and compare them before signing. |

## Module 4. What you left in the United States

| Code | Lesson | What the person achieves |
|---|---|---|
| M4 U01 | Your last paycheck and your account back there | Recover the wages you're owed and decide what to do with your U.S. bank account. |
| M4 U02 | Your U.S. taxes | Check whether it's worth filing your U.S. tax return to recover taxes that were withheld. |
| M4 U03 | Your U.S. retirement: 401(k) and Social Security | Check whether you left retirement money in the U.S. and who to ask so you don't lose it. |

## Module 5. Your Afore, your IMSS and your weeks

| Code | Lesson | What the person achieves |
|---|---|---|
| M5 U01 | Do you have an Afore? Find it | Check whether you have an Afore account from when you worked in Mexico, and locate it. |
| M5 U02 | Your contribution weeks | Look up your IMSS contribution weeks and calculate how many you're missing for a pension. |
| M5 U03 | Save for retirement even if you're self-employed | Make voluntary contributions to your Afore, and explain how to enroll in IMSS if you're self-employed. |

## Module 6. Your first 90 days

| Code | Lesson | What the person achieves |
|---|---|---|
| M6 U01 | Think in pesos: your return budget | Make a budget in pesos for your first 90 days, with what really comes in and goes out. |
| M6 U02 | Welcome-home obligations | Set kind limits on the spending and loans people ask of you when you return. |
| M6 U03 | When the remittances stop | Reorganize household spending when the relative who sent money comes back or stops sending. |

## Module 7. Work or a business with what you know how to do

| Code | Lesson | What the person achieves |
|---|---|---|
| M7 U01 | What you know how to do has value | List your skills and identify how to prove them to get a job. |
| M7 U02 | Compare offers: wages and benefits | Compare a formal job and an informal one by adding up what you don't see in the wage. |
| M7 U03 | A small business without risking your savings | Test a business idea on a small scale before putting in all your money. |

## Module 8. Don't get scammed when you return

| Code | Lesson | What the person achieves |
|---|---|---|
| M8 U01 | Fake fixers | Recognize people who charge for no-cost procedures or keep your papers. |
| M8 U02 | Fraud and extortion against people who return | Recognize the most common frauds and extortion against people who return, and decide what to do. |
| M8 U03 | Your return plan on one page | Bring together on a single page your papers, your account, your credit history, what you left there, your retirement and your budget. |

# 4. Structure of each lesson

- **Start:** the hook and what you'll learn.
- **The essentials (5 minutes):** steps, cards, a calculation and a one-minute case; it ends with a key idea and common mistakes.
- **Go deeper (5 more minutes):** a table to fill in, an extra topic and three cases.
- **Practice:** «What would you do?» with situations, a review and an action plan.
- **Resources, key words and sources** at the end of each lesson.
