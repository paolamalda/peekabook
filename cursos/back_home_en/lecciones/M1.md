# M1 U01 | Your repatriation record and your CURP: the first two papers
objetivo: Explain what the repatriation record is for and how to get your CURP to start any paperwork.
gancho: Don Rafa arrived in Michoacán with a paper from the National Migration Institute folded in his wallet. His brother told him to throw it away because he «was already in Mexico». In this lesson you'll see why that paper is worth gold.

== esencial

--- paso | fa-file-text-o | The repatriation record
If you were sent back from the United States, the National Migration Institute (INM) gave you a paper. It's the **Record of Reception of Repatriated Mexicans** (Constancia de Recepción de Mexicanos Repatriados). It works as a temporary ID for your first steps. Use it for your CURP, birth certificate, health services, jobs and support from México te Abraza.

--- tarjetas | fa-th-large | What it's for
* fa-id-card | CURP | To get or recover your CURP. | First step. | igual
* fa-heartbeat | Health | Basic medical care and continuing treatment. | IMSS and health institutions. | igual
* fa-briefcase | Jobs | Register with the National Employment Service. | Job board. | igual
* fa-gift | Support | Tarjeta Bienestar Paisano and Tarjeta FINABIEN Paisano. | Module 2. | igual

--- paso | fa-key | The CURP is the key
Almost everything in Mexico asks for your {{CURP|Mexico's unique population ID code. Almost every process asks for it.}}: a bank account, IMSS, Afore, your children's school, a job. If you already had a CURP, you can look it up and print it at gob.mx/curp at no cost. If you never had one, you get it at the Civil Registry or at a help center for returnees.

--- paso | fa-user | A one-minute case
Don Rafa kept his record, looked up his CURP at an internet café and printed it. With those two papers he could ask for his birth certificate and sign up for support.

> **Key idea:** keep your repatriation record and get your CURP first; they open all the other doors.

--- comprueba
1. What is the repatriation record for? || It's a temporary ID for your first steps in Mexico.
2. How much does it cost to look up your CURP at gob.mx? || Nothing: looking it up and printing it has no cost.

--- recuerda
- Keep the record.
- Get your CURP first.
- No CURP process costs money.

Ask for a copy at the INM office or at the help center for returnees in your state. In the meantime, any other paper you have (birth certificate, consular ID, even an expired passport) helps prove who you are.

--- casos
### Case 1. Don Rafa's paper
His brother tells him to throw away the record because he «is already in Mexico».
? What does he do? || He keeps it with his other papers.

### Case 2. Memo's CURP
Memo doesn't know if he has a CURP; he left for the U.S. when he was 6.
? What does he do? || He looks for it at gob.mx/curp with his name and date of birth.

### Case 3. The «fixer» at the plaza
A man offers to get Lupita's CURP for 500 pesos.
? What does she do? || She doesn't pay: looking it up at gob.mx has no cost.

--- errores
* Throwing away the record | You're left without ID | Keep it
* Paying for the CURP | You lose money | It has no cost
* Leaving the CURP for later | Everything else gets stuck | CURP first
* Giving your papers to a stranger | Identity theft | Do it yourself

# M1 U02 | Your birth certificate and your INE
objetivo: Get your birth certificate and apply for your INE voter ID, which the bank and employers will ask for.
gancho: Chayo went with her husband to the bank to open an account. They asked for an INE. He only had his Texas driver's license. They went home without an account. In this lesson you'll see how to avoid that.

== esencial

--- paso | fa-file | The birth certificate
You can get a certified copy of your birth certificate at gob.mx/actas with your CURP. Pay online or at a bank, and print it. It's worth the same as the one from the Civil Registry. The cost depends on the state.

--- paso | fa-id-card | The INE
The {{INE voter ID|Mexico's official photo ID, issued by the National Electoral Institute. It's the ID most often requested.}} is the ID people ask for most. Applying for it has no cost. Bring to the INE office: your birth certificate, a photo ID (the repatriation record or a document with your photo) and proof of address.

--- tarjetas | fa-th-large | If you don't have proof of address
* fa-home | A relative's bill | Electricity, water or property tax in the name of whoever takes you in. | Ask the office what they accept. | igual
* fa-users | Witnesses | Some offices accept two witnesses. | Ask before you go. | igual
* fa-building | Proof of residence | Issued by the municipality or local authority. | Useful in small towns. | igual

--- paso | fa-user | A one-minute case
Chayo's husband got his birth certificate online, asked the municipality for a proof of residence and went to the INE office. Two weeks later he had his ID and opened his account.

> **Key idea:** with your birth certificate and proof of address, you get your INE at no cost. It's the ID that opens a bank account.

--- comprueba
1. Where can you get your birth certificate without going to the Civil Registry? || At gob.mx/actas, with your CURP.
2. How much does the INE cost? || Nothing: applying is free of charge.

--- recuerda
- Birth certificate at gob.mx/actas.
- INE at no cost.
- Proof of address: ask about options.

== profundiza

--- tabla | fa-table | What to bring to the INE office
| Requirement | What you can bring |
|---|---|
| Nationality | Birth certificate |
| Photo ID | Repatriation record, passport or consular ID |
| Address | Utility bill or proof of residence |

--- tema | fa-clock-o | While your INE arrives
Ask the office for your application receipt and keep it. If you need to collect support before then, ask the program office which ID they accept.

--- casos
### Case 1. The Texas license
Chayo's husband wants to open an account with his Texas driver's license.
? What does he do? || He applies for his INE with his birth certificate and proof of address.

### Case 2. No bills in his name
Memo lives with his aunt and no bill is in his name.
? What does he do? || He asks the office if they accept his aunt's bill or witnesses.

### Case 3. The paid appointment
A website asks for 300 pesos to «book an appointment» at the INE.
? What does he do? || He doesn't pay: the appointment and the ID are free of charge.

--- errores
* Arriving without a birth certificate | They send you back | Bring it printed
* Paying for an INE appointment | Fraud | It has no cost
* Not asking about proof of address | Wasted trip | Ask first
* Throwing away the application receipt | You can't pick up your ID | Keep it

# M1 U03 | Papers for your children born in the United States
objetivo: Register your U.S.-born children in Mexico so they have a CURP, school and services.
gancho: Lupita's children were born in Houston. At the school in Guanajuato she was told that without a CURP they couldn't enroll. In this lesson you'll see how dual citizenship works.

== esencial

--- paso | fa-flag | They're Mexican too
If your child was born in the U.S. and you are Mexican, your child is also Mexican by birth. For Mexico to recognize it, you **register the birth** at the Civil Registry or at a consulate. That way your child gets a Mexican birth certificate and a CURP.

--- tarjetas | fa-th-large | What they usually ask for
* fa-file | U.S. birth certificate | The original birth certificate. | Some states ask for an apostille. | igual
* fa-language | Translation | Into Spanish, if they ask for it. | Ask first. | igual
* fa-id-card | Your papers | Your Mexican birth certificate and ID. | Mom's or dad's. | igual
* fa-child | Your child | Must be present, or as the office says. | Ask. | igual

--- paso | fa-graduation-cap | School should not wait
Mexico's Education Ministry (SEP) says children can enroll even if papers are missing, while they're being sorted out. Ask the school to enroll them and tell them the process is underway.

--- paso | fa-user | A one-minute case
Lupita took her children's birth certificates to the Civil Registry. They told her what her state requires. In the meantime, her children started school with provisional enrollment.

> **Key idea:** register your children in Mexico so they have a birth certificate and a CURP; school should not wait for those papers.

--- comprueba
1. Can your U.S.-born child be Mexican? || Yes, if their mom or dad is Mexican; you register the birth.
2. What do you do if the school asks for a CURP and you don't have it yet? || Ask for enrollment while the process is completed.

--- recuerda
- Register the birth.
- Ask about requirements in your state.
- School doesn't wait.

--- tema | fa-passport | Apostille
An {{apostille|A seal that makes a document from another country valid. For a U.S. birth certificate, it's issued by the state where the child was born.}} is a seal that recognizes a document from another country. For a U.S. birth certificate, you request it in the state where the child was born. Some civil registries no longer ask for it: ask before you pay.

--- casos
### Case 1. Lupita's school
She's told there's no spot for her children without a CURP.
? What does she do? || She asks for enrollment while the birth registration is finished.

### Case 2. The original certificate
Lupita only has a plain copy of the birth certificate.
? What does she do? || She asks the Civil Registry if they accept it or how to request a certified one.

### Case 3. The expensive translation
She's offered a translation of the certificates for 2,000 pesos each.
? What does she do? || First she asks whether her state requires a translation and who can do it.

--- errores
* Thinking they aren't Mexican | You don't register them | They are
* Paying for an apostille without asking | Extra cost | Ask first
* Keeping the kids out of school | They lose the year | Provisional enrollment
* Losing the U.S. birth certificate | Hard to replace from Mexico | Keep the original
