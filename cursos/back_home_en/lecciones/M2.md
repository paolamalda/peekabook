# M2 U01 | Your returnee cards
objetivo: Tell apart the Tarjeta Bienestar Paisano and the Tarjeta FINABIEN Paisano, and use the 2,000 pesos well.
gancho: At the reception center, Don Rafa was given a card with 2,000 pesos. A man on the bus told him «more would come every month». In this lesson you'll see what each card is and what it isn't.

== esencial

--- tarjetas | fa-th-large | Two different cards
* fa-gift | Bienestar Paisano | A one-time support of 2,000 pesos. | For the trip home and first expenses. | igual
* fa-credit-card | FINABIEN Paisano | An account to receive and send money. | Remittances and payments. | igual

--- paso | fa-exclamation-circle | The support comes only once
The 2,000 pesos on the Tarjeta Bienestar Paisano are given once and don't repeat every month. Use them to get to your community and for what's most urgent: food, transportation, papers.

--- paso | fa-exchange | FINABIEN is the one to keep
The Tarjeta FINABIEN Paisano is an account: you can receive money from the U.S., save and pay. You request it at FINABIEN branches. Use it while you open another account, or as your main account.

--- paso | fa-user | A one-minute case
Don Rafa used 900 pesos of the support for the bus to his town and kept the rest for his birth certificate and his first days. Then he got his FINABIEN card so his son could send him money from Dallas.

> **Key idea:** the 2,000-peso support comes only once; FINABIEN is an account to keep receiving money.

--- comprueba
1. How often does the Tarjeta Bienestar Paisano support arrive? || Only once.
2. What is the Tarjeta FINABIEN Paisano for? || To receive, save and send money, like an account.

--- recuerda
- 2,000 pesos, once.
- FINABIEN is an account.
- Urgent things first.

== profundiza

--- ecuacion | fa-calculator | Don Rafa's 2,000 pesos
= 2,000 | Support
- 900 | Bus to his town
- 250 | Birth certificate and copies
- 600 | Food for the first week
= 250 | For the unexpected

--- tema | fa-lock | Your PIN is only yours
No one from the program will ask for your PIN or password by phone. If someone asks, it's fraud (Module 8).

--- casos
### Case 1. «More every month»
Don Rafa is told the support repeats every month.
? What does he do? || He plans with 2,000 pesos, one time only.

### Case 2. The son in Dallas
Don Rafa's son wants to send him money every two weeks.
? What does he do? || He uses his FINABIEN card to receive it.

### Case 3. The call «from the program»
Someone calls «from the program» to «activate» the card with his PIN.
? What does he do? || He hangs up: no one from the program asks for the PIN.

--- errores
* Waiting for more deposits | You run out of money | It's only once
* Mixing up the cards | You can't receive remittances | FINABIEN is an account
* Giving out your PIN | They empty your card | Never give it
* Spending it all on day one | No money for papers | Urgent things first

# M2 U02 | Choose an account with no fees
objetivo: Compare basic accounts and choose one in your name that doesn't charge you for keeping your money.
gancho: Lupita opened an account at the first branch she found. Three months later she saw she was being charged 120 pesos a month for «minimum balance». In this lesson you'll see what to check before signing.

== esencial

--- paso | fa-search | Basic accounts
Banks must offer a **{{basic account|An account with no opening fee, no minimum balance and no monthly maintenance fee.}}**: no opening fee, no minimum balance and no monthly maintenance fee. Ask for that account by name.

--- tarjetas | fa-th-large | Before signing, ask
* fa-money | Is there a maintenance fee? | A monthly charge for having the account. | Look for zero. | igual
* fa-balance-scale | Is there a minimum balance? | If you go below an amount, they charge you. | Look for none. | igual
* fa-credit-card | Free withdrawals? | Which ATMs don't charge. | Ask which ones. | igual
* fa-shield | Is it protected? | The institution must be authorized. | Check with CONDUSEF. | igual

--- paso | fa-check-square-o | Check the institution
We don't recommend any bank: we teach you to check. Look up the institution in CONDUSEF's SIPRES registry. If it isn't there, don't deposit there.

--- paso | fa-user | A one-minute case
Lupita went to another branch and asked for the basic account. She pays nothing a month. She closed the old one and stopped losing 120 pesos.

> **Key idea:** ask for the basic account and ask about fees before you sign.

--- comprueba
1. What must a basic account not charge you? || No opening fee, no minimum balance and no monthly maintenance fee.
2. Where do you check that an institution is authorized? || In CONDUSEF's SIPRES registry.

--- recuerda
- Ask for the basic account.
- Ask about fees.
- Check with CONDUSEF.

== profundiza

--- ecuacion | fa-calculator | What Lupita was losing
= 120 | Fee per month
× 12 | Months
= 1,440 | Per year, for nothing

--- tema | fa-file-text-o | Read the contract and the summary sheet
The summary sheet (carátula) sums up the contract. That's where the fees are. Ask for a copy and keep it.

--- casos
### Case 1. The minimum balance
Lupita is offered an account with a gold card and a 3,000-peso minimum balance.
? What does she do? || She asks for the basic account with no minimum balance.

### Case 2. The town savings box
Chayo is offered a place to keep her money that pays 4% a month.
? What does she do? || She looks it up in CONDUSEF's SIPRES before depositing.

### Case 3. No contract
Memo signed and didn't get a copy.
? What does he do? || He asks for his contract and the summary sheet with the fees.

--- errores
* Signing without asking | Hidden fees | Ask first
* Accepting a minimum balance | You're charged if you go below | Basic account
* Depositing in unauthorized places | You can lose it | Check
* Not keeping the contract | You can't complain | Ask for a copy

# M2 U03 | Getting paid, paying and disputing
objetivo: Use your account to get paid and pay safely, and know how to dispute a charge you didn't make.
gancho: Memo got his first payment by transfer and saw a 1,800-peso charge he doesn't recognize. He doesn't know if it's a mistake, fraud or «just how Mexico is». In this lesson you'll see what to do in the first hours.

== esencial

--- tarjetas | fa-th-large | Ways to move your money
* fa-exchange | Transfer (SPEI) | With the 18-digit CLABE. | Arrives in minutes. | igual
* fa-credit-card | Debit card | You pay with your own money. | It isn't credit. | igual
* fa-mobile | Bank app | Check and transfer. | Only the official one. | igual
* fa-money | ATM withdrawal | Prefer ATMs with no fee. | Watch your surroundings. | igual

--- paso | fa-bell | Turn on alerts
Ask for a text message for every transaction. That way you see a strange charge the same day.

--- paso | fa-phone | If you see a charge you didn't make
1. Call the bank at the number on the back of your card and block it.
2. Ask for a reference number (folio) for your claim.
3. If they don't solve it, go to CONDUSEF: 55 5340 0999.

--- paso | fa-user | A one-minute case
Memo called the bank, blocked the card and wrote down his reference number. The bank reviewed it and returned the 1,800 pesos.

> **Key idea:** turn on alerts; if you see a strange charge, block the card, get a reference number and dispute it.

--- comprueba
1. What do you need to receive a transfer? || Your 18-digit CLABE.
2. What do you do first about a charge you didn't make? || Call the bank, block the card and get a reference number.

--- recuerda
- Alerts on.
- Block and get a reference number.
- CONDUSEF if they don't solve it.

Dispute the charge as soon as you see it. The longer you wait, the harder it is to prove it wasn't you.

--- casos
### Case 1. Memo's charge
He sees 1,800 pesos he didn't spend.
? What does he do? || He blocks the card, gets a reference number and disputes it.

### Case 2. The CLABE for the boss
Don Rafa's boss asks for his details to pay him.
? What does he do? || He gives his CLABE, never his PIN or password.

### Case 3. «That's just how Mexico is»
A friend tells Memo not to complain because «they never give it back».
? What does he do? || He disputes it and, if they don't solve it, goes to CONDUSEF.

--- errores
* Not disputing | You lose the money | Dispute it soon
* Giving your PIN to get paid | Fraud | Only the CLABE
* Not getting a reference number | No proof | Always get one
* Unofficial apps | Data theft | Only the bank's app
