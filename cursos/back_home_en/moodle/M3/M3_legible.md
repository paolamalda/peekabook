# Module 3. Credit from scratch

## M3 U01. Your U.S. credit history doesn't count here

**What you will be able to do:** Explain that in Mexico you start with no credit history, and check your report at no cost.

**To start:** Don Rafa had good credit in Texas: he paid off his truck on time for years. In Mexico he asked for a loan and was told he «doesn't show up anywhere». In this lesson you'll see why and what to do.

### The essentials (5 minutes)

#### Two countries, two histories

Your U.S. credit history doesn't carry over to Mexico. Here it's kept by credit bureaus such as **Buró de Crédito** and **Círculo de Crédito**. If you never had credit in Mexico, you show up with no history. That isn't bad: it's starting from zero.



#### What your report says

| Type | Description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Your credit | Who lent to you and how much. | Banks, stores, apps. |
| How you paid | On time or late. | What weighs most. |
| Who checked you | Who looked at your report. | Watch out for many checks. |

#### Check it at no cost

You can request your special report at no cost once every 12 months from each bureau. Do it to confirm no one opened credit in your name while you were away.



#### A one-minute case

Don Rafa requested his report: he had no credit. But his cousin's showed a loan from an app he never asked for. He disputed it in time.

> **Key idea:** in Mexico you start with no history; check your report at no cost to see that no one used your name.



#### Check your understanding

1. Does your U.S. history count in Mexico?
*Answer:* No: here you start from zero.

2. How often can you get your report at no cost?
*Answer:* Once every 12 months from each bureau.


#### Remember

- You start from zero.
- Report at no cost every 12 months.
- Check that no one used your name.



### Go deeper (5 more minutes)

#### Your check

| Bureau | I requested it | Date | All good? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buró de Crédito | | | |
| Círculo de Crédito | | | |



#### If something isn't yours

File a dispute with the bureau (on its website) and notify the institution that gave the credit. If they don't solve it, go to CONDUSEF.



#### Cases


**Case 1. «You don't show up»**

Don Rafa is told he has no history.
- *What does he do?* He understands he's starting from zero and requests his report to check it.


**Case 2. Someone else's loan**

His cousin's report shows an app he never used.
- *What does he do?* He files a dispute with the bureau.


**Case 3. The paid report**

A website charges Memo 400 pesos for his report.
- *What does he do?* He requests it on the official website, at no cost once a year.


#### Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Thinking your U.S. credit works here | You get frustrated | Start from zero |
| Not checking your report | You miss fraud | Request it |
| Paying for the yearly report | You lose money | It's no cost once a year |
| Ignoring someone else's loan | You end up paying | Dispute it |

### Practice

#### Interactive activity

**What would you do? (H5P):** three situations from this lesson with Don Rafa, his cousin and Memo. Choose the best decision in each one; if you miss, you can try again. You earn experience points.



#### Quiz

1. You had good credit in Texas. How do you show up in Mexico? a) With no history · b) With the same score · c) With bad credit
2. How many times a year is your special report at no cost? a) Never · b) Once · c) Every month
3. A loan you never asked for shows up. What do you do? a) Pay it to avoid problems · b) File a dispute · c) Nothing
**Answers:** 1-a: you start from zero. 2-b: once every 12 months. 3-b: you dispute it.



#### Put it into practice

You requested your Buró report in January. From what month can you request it again at no cost?
**Answer:** From January of the next year.



#### Your plan

Request your special report from Buró de Crédito and Círculo de Crédito and write down the date.



### Learn more

- **Reporte de Crédito Especial** (Buró de Crédito · Spanish): https://www.burodecredito.com.mx — **What to look for:** «reporte de crédito especial» (special credit report).
- **Credit report** (Círculo de Crédito · Spanish): https://www.circulodecredito.com.mx — **What to look for:** «reporte de crédito» (credit report).

### Key words

- *Credit history:* a record of how you've repaid what you borrowed.
- *Credit bureau:* an authorized company that keeps that record.
- *Special report:* your full history, at no cost once a year.

## M3 U02. Start small and pay on time

**What you will be able to do:** Build a credit history in Mexico with a small credit you can pay in full and on time.

**To start:** Memo wants a credit card «to have a history» and is offered one with a 20,000-peso limit. He earns 9,000 a month. In this lesson you'll see how to start without sinking.

### The essentials (5 minutes)

#### Start small

To build a history you don't need much credit. You need **one** small credit and to pay it on time for several months. It can be a low-limit card or a small installment purchase you were going to make anyway.



#### The three rules

| Type | Description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| On time | Pay before the due date. | What counts most. |
| Use little | Don't use more than a third of the limit. | Example: of 3,000, use up to 1,000. |
| Pay it all | Pay the full balance, not the minimum. | That way you pay no interest. |

#### The minimum payment is a trap

If you pay only the minimum, the debt lasts years and you pay much more in interest. Use the card only for what you already have in your account to pay.



#### A one-minute case

Memo asked for a card with a 3,000 limit. He uses it for his phone top-up and gas, and pays it in full every month. In a year he already has a history.

> **Key idea:** a small credit, low use and full payment on time build your history.



#### Check your understanding

1. How much of the limit should you use?
*Answer:* No more than a third.

2. Why not pay only the minimum?
*Answer:* Because the debt lasts years and you pay a lot of interest.


#### Remember

- One small credit.
- Use little.
- Pay it all, on time.



### Go deeper (5 more minutes)

#### Memo's limit

- Limit: **3,000**
- One third: **3**
- The most you should use: **1,000**


#### Compare with the CAT

The Costo Anual Total (CAT, total annual cost) adds up interest and fees. Between two similar credits, the one with the lower CAT is cheaper. You see it on the summary sheet and in CONDUSEF's calculators.



#### Cases


**Case 1. The 20,000 limit**

Memo is offered a 20,000 limit; he earns 9,000.
- *What does he do?* He asks for a low limit he can pay in full.


**Case 2. The minimum payment**

Lupita's statement says «minimum payment 250».
- *What does she do?* She pays the full balance to avoid interest.


**Case 3. Three cards at once**

Don Rafa is offered three cards in one week.
- *What does he do?* He starts with just one and pays it on time.


#### Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Asking for a high limit | You get into debt | Start small |
| Paying the minimum | Years of interest | Pay it all |
| Using the whole limit | Hurts your history | One third |
| Many cards at the start | Hard to control | One |

### Practice

#### Interactive activity

**What would you do? (H5P):** three situations from this lesson with Memo, Lupita and Don Rafa. Choose the best decision in each one; if you miss, you can try again. You earn experience points.



#### Quiz

1. What builds your history best? a) Many cards open at the same time · b) A small credit paid on time · c) Paying the minimum
2. Your limit is 6,000. What's the most you should use? a) 2,000 · b) 6,000 · c) 4,500
3. Which indicator compares the cost of two credits? a) The card's color · b) The minimum payment · c) The CAT
**Answers:** 1-b: small and on time. 2-a: one third. 3-c: it adds up interest and fees.



#### Put it into practice

Your limit is 4,500. How much is one third?
**Answer:** 1,500 pesos.



#### Your plan

If you decide to get credit, write down your maximum limit, what you'll use it for and how you'll pay it in full every month.



### Learn more

- **Calculators and comparison tools** (CONDUSEF · Spanish): https://www.condusef.gob.mx — **What to look for:** «simulador tarjeta de crédito» (credit card calculator).

### Key words

- *Credit limit:* the most they'll lend you.
- *Minimum payment:* the least you can pay without being late; it generates interest.
- *CAT:* the credit's total annual cost.

### Sources

CONDUSEF, credit cards and CAT; Banco de México; checked October 5, 2026.

## M3 U03. «No credit check» loans and apps that overcharge

**What you will be able to do:** Recognize the expensive loans that target people with no history, and compare them before signing.

**To start:** Lupita needs 5,000 pesos for her children's school uniforms. An app promises her the money in 10 minutes, «no credit check, no paperwork». In a week she owes 7,800. In this lesson you'll see how it happens.

### The essentials (5 minutes)

#### «No credit check» doesn't mean no cost

People with no history are targeted by fast loans with very high interest and terms of one or two weeks. Some apps also ask for access to your contacts to pressure you.



#### Warning signs

| Type | Description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Very short term | 7 to 15 days. | Hard to pay. |
| They ask for your contacts | Access to your phone book and photos. | To pressure you. |
| They don't show the CAT | They don't show the total cost. | Bad sign. |
| Not registered | It isn't listed with CONDUSEF. | Don't use it. |

#### Do the math first

Ask how much you'll pay **in total** and over how long. If you borrow 5,000 and pay 7,800 in a week, the loan costs you 2,800: more than half.



#### A one-minute case

Lupita deleted the app before accepting. She asked her comadre to wait two weeks and used part of her savings circle (tanda). If she ever needs credit, she'll compare the total cost.

> **Key idea:** before borrowing, ask how much you'll pay in total. If they won't tell you, or they ask for your contacts, don't sign.



#### Check your understanding

1. What do you ask before any loan?
*Answer:* How much I'll pay in total and over how long.

2. Why does an app ask for your contacts?
*Answer:* To pressure you through your family if you fall behind.


#### Remember

- Ask for the total.
- Don't give your contacts.
- Check with CONDUSEF.



### Go deeper (5 more minutes)

#### The app loan

- What you'd pay: **7,800**
- What they lend you: **5,000**
- What it costs, in one week: **2,800**


#### If you're already being harassed

Keep screenshots, don't pay into personal accounts and file a complaint with CONDUSEF. If they threaten you, report it to the Fiscalía (public prosecutor). Module 8 covers this in depth.



#### Cases


**Case 1. The 10 minutes**

An app promises Lupita 5,000 in 10 minutes with no paperwork.
- *What does she do?* She asks for the total; since they won't say, she doesn't use it.


**Case 2. The contacts**

The app asks for access to Memo's phone book.
- *What does he do?* He denies permission and uninstalls it.


**Case 3. The store loan**

The furniture store offers Chayo «tiny» payments.
- *What does she do?* She adds up all the payments and compares with the cash price.


#### Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Not asking for the total | You pay double | Always ask |
| Giving access to contacts | Your family gets harassed | Deny it |
| Using unregistered apps | No one to complain to | Check |
| Looking only at the small payment | Hidden cost | Add it all up |

### Practice

#### Interactive activity

**What would you do? (H5P):** three situations from this lesson with Lupita, Memo and Chayo. Choose the best decision in each one; if you miss, you can try again. You earn experience points.



#### Quiz

1. You borrow 3,000 and pay 4,500 in two weeks. How much does it cost you? a) 4,500 · b) 1,500 · c) Nothing
2. An app asks for your contacts. What do you do? a) Deny it and don't use the app · b) Accept · c) Give only your family's numbers
3. What must any loan tell you before you sign? a) Only the weekly payment · b) The collector's name and phone number · c) How much you'll pay in total
**Answers:** 1-b: 4,500 minus 3,000. 2-a: it's to pressure you. 3-c: the total.



#### Put it into practice

You're offered 12 weekly payments of 450 for a phone that costs 3,900 in cash. How much extra do you pay?
**Answer:** 1,500 pesos (12 times 450 is 5,400).



#### Your plan

Write three ways to get urgent money without expensive loans: savings, selling something, support, more time from the person you owe.



### Learn more

- **Loan apps** (CONDUSEF · Spanish): https://www.condusef.gob.mx — **What to look for:** «apps de préstamos» (loan apps) and «SIPRES».

### Key words

- *Term:* the time you have to pay.
- *Total cost:* everything you pay, minus what you borrowed.
- *Collection harassment:* illegal pressure to make you pay.

### Sources

CONDUSEF, alerts on loan apps and debt collection; checked October 5, 2026.
