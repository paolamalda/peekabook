# Community and channels · Your Business, Your Money, Your Future (U.S., English)

## 1. Your Business Community in Moodle

A separate course, **"Your Business Community · U.S."** (short name `YBMF-US-EN-COM`), that complements this course. It has announcements, question forums by topic, scam alerts, the "Introduce your business" forum, wins, a monthly live session and a separate commercial referrals forum. Its setup is in its own folder.

To enroll the group at once, the site administrator can create a **cohort** "Your Business US EN" and add it with "Cohort sync" in both the course and the community.

## 2. Monthly live session

- One 60-minute video session a month: the month's topic (for example, "Your estimated payments," "Price with margin") and questions.
- Made-up examples only. **Never** open a participant's IRS account, bank or platform in front of the group.
- If an expert is invited (tax preparer, CPA, SBDC adviser), it's made clear that it's general orientation.

## 3. WhatsApp channel

- Name: "Your Business U.S. · Desarrolla Talento".
- Content: course announcements, tax dates (estimated payments, April return), scam alerts against businesses and referrals.
- Only the team posts; followers can't see each other's numbers.
- Avoid WhatsApp group chats: numbers are visible there and scams and "loan" offers circulate.

## 4. Commercial referrals (channels only)

Services with a commercial agreement (for example, payment processors, tax preparers or insurance) are posted **only** in the channel and the referrals forum, never in lessons, activities, self-assessments or the certificate. Every post says: **"Commercial referral: Desarrolla Talento may receive a commission. Compare with at least one other option. Using this service is voluntary and doesn't affect your access, points or certificate."**

## 5. Moderation rules

1. Remove posts with personal or tax data (SSN, ITIN, EIN, passwords, account screenshots, immigration status) and notify privately.
2. Don't allow participants to promote loans, investments, financial products or tax schemes.
3. In "Introduce your business," one post a month per person.
4. Review any scam alert the same day.
5. Refer tax, legal, employment or immigration cases to the IRS, VITA, a preparer, an SBDC or an accredited attorney or organization.
