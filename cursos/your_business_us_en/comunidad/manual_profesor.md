# 1. Your role

## The community and the course

The community is a **companion** to the course, not a separate course with its own content:

| | Course (YBMF-US-EN) | Community (YBMF-US-EN-COM) |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Learn: 9 modules, 43 lessons | Accompany: monthly session, questions, alerts, "Introduce your business" and wins |
| Content | Lessons, H5P, self-assessments | Forums and the *Community guide*; no new topics taught |
| Assessment | Yes: badges and certificate | No |
| Who joins | People enrolled in the program | The same people, through a link in the course |

## Why the community matters here

Entrepreneurs often work alone, with little time and fear of the IRS. The community gives them three things the course doesn't: **rhythm** (a challenge and a session every month), **company** (others with the same problems) and **timely alerts** (scams and tax dates).

## What you do

| Task | What it involves |
|---|---|
| **Monthly session** | A course topic, an exercise with made-up numbers and questions. |
| **Answer** | General questions in the forums, linked to the lesson. |
| **Encourage** | Monthly challenges, wins and "Introduce your business." |
| **Protect** | Remove personal or tax data, financial offers and tax schemes. |
| **Report** | Log and monthly report to coordination. |

## What you never do

- Ask for or listen to anyone's SSN, ITIN, EIN, passwords, account numbers, real amounts or immigration status.
- Log into anyone's IRS account, bank or platform, not even "to help."
- Tell someone whether to open an LLC or how to file: you explain the rules and refer.
- Give immigration advice.
- Recommend specific banks, processors, platforms, preparers or suppliers.
- Promise tax, credit or sales results.

## Your tone

- Friendly and direct, short sentences, real business examples.
- No judgment about informality: "formalizing is a decision; here you see what you gain and what it costs."
- Use the examples of Lupita, Javier, Daniela and Don Ramón.

| Minute | Block | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | Welcome | Rules: no personal or tax data, general questions. |
| 5 to 15 | Key idea | The month's topic with a character. |

| 35 to 50 | Questions | General only; personal ones in private and referred. |

# 6. Forums and tax questions

## How to answer in the forums

1. **Acknowledge:** "Great question; it happens to a lot of entrepreneurs."
2. **Answer the general part** in 3 to 5 lines.
3. **Link to the course** (annex B).
4. **Give the next step:** what to check or which institution to go to.

## Tax questions: the limit

You can explain how self-employment tax works, what a 1099 is or when estimated payments are due. You **don't** decide whether someone opens an LLC or review returns, and you **never** comment on immigration situations. Useful line: "This is the general rule; for your case, VITA, a preparer with a PTIN or a CPA; for immigration matters, an attorney or accredited organization."

## "Introduce your business"

One post a month per person. Reply with a sentence of encouragement and, if it applies, a useful lesson. Remove posts offering loans, investments, tax schemes or miracle products.

# Annex C. Directory and plans

**Plan for a selling scam:** 1) don't hand over more merchandise or send money back; 2) keep evidence; 3) tell your bank or payment processor; 4) report at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and, if it happened online, at ic3.gov.

**Plan for extortion or a fake authority:** 1) hang up without negotiating; 2) verify by going to the official site yourself or calling the official number; 3) never pay with gift cards or transfers; 4) 911 or local police if there's danger; report to the FTC.

| Need | Where |
|---|---|
| Federal taxes, EIN, ITIN | irs.gov; VITA |
| Business advice | SBDC and SCORE (sba.gov) |
| Permits and sales tax in California | CalGold and CDTFA |
| Health insurance | Covered California and Medi-Cal |
| Complaints about banks and payment apps | CFPB (consumerfinance.gov) |
| Scams | ReportFraud.ftc.gov and ic3.gov |
| Identity theft | IdentityTheft.gov |
| Trademark | USPTO |
| Immigration questions | Attorneys or organizations accredited by the Department of Justice |

Confirm phone numbers and hours on the official site before sharing them.
