# Moderation guide · Your Business Community

For the team that moderates the forums and the channel. Version 1.0 · September 2026.

## 1. Principles

1. **Protect personal and tax data** above all else.
2. **Orient, don't advise:** we explain general rules and refer; we don't resolve specific tax, legal, employment or immigration cases.
3. **Never act for them:** we don't log into anyone's IRS account, bank or platform; we explain and the person does the steps.
4. **No sale of financial products**; businesses are introduced only in their forum, and commercial referrals only in theirs, always labeled.
5. **Course tone:** friendly and direct, no judgment about informality.

## 2. Response times

| Type | Target |
|---|---|
| Post with personal or tax data | Remove within 2 business hours |
| Scam alert | Review the same day and pin if relevant |
| Scam or extortion in progress (private message) | Respond immediately with the plan |
| General question | Answer within 48 business hours |

## 3. What to do in each case

| Situation | Action | Suggested private message |
|---|---|---|
| Posts SSN, ITIN, EIN, account, IRS or bank screenshot, or immigration status | Remove the post | "We removed your post because it had personal or tax data. You can ask again with made-up or rounded numbers." |
| Offers loans or investments | Remove and warn | "Financial offers aren't allowed in the community. Check the rules in chapter 2." |
| Offers schemes to "pay less tax" | Remove, warn and tell coordination | "Tax schemes aren't allowed in the community. Handle your taxes with a preparer or CPA (M5 U03)." |
| Promotes their business outside its forum or more than once a month | Move or remove | "Please use **Introduce your business**, once a month. Thanks for sharing!" |
| Recommends a "notario" or a site that charges for the EIN | Remove and explain | "The EIN and seller's permit are free on official sites; for immigration matters, only attorneys or accredited organizations (M5 U02)." |
| Asks us to review their return or whether to open an LLC | Answer generally and refer | "Your case depends on your details. We suggest VITA, a preparer with a PTIN or an SBDC; lesson [lesson] helps you prepare your questions." |
| Selling scam in progress | Respond immediately | "Don't send money back, keep evidence, tell your bank or processor and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov (M4 U03)." |
| Extortion or threat | Respond immediately | "Don't negotiate; hang up. If there's danger, call 911 or local police (M7 U03)." |
| Immigration question | Respond carefully and refer | "We don't give immigration advice. Look for an attorney or an organization accredited by the Department of Justice; never a 'notario.'" |
| Employment conflict with a worker | Answer generally and refer | "Employer obligations are reviewed with the EDD, DIR and a professional (M8 U01)." |
| Conflict between participants | Remind of rules; if it continues, suspend | "Please keep it respectful. It's the community rule." |

## 4. Template answers

**Question already covered in the course:**
"Great question! We cover it in [lesson]. In short: [2-line summary]. If you still have questions after reading it, tell us which part."

**A fact that changes:**
"This fact changes every year. As of [date] it was [fact] according to [source]. Confirm it with the IRS or your state before deciding."

**Request for a recommendation:**
"We don't recommend specific banks, processors, platforms or preparers. Here's how to compare: [lesson]. If you like, share the options you're looking at, without personal data, and we'll help you see what to ask."

## 5. What we never do

- Ask for SSN, ITIN, EIN, passwords, account numbers, real amounts or immigration status.
- Log into a participant's IRS account, bank or platform.
- Recommend an institution, product, preparer or supplier outside the referrals forum.
- Promise tax or credit results.

## 6. Escalation

1. Moderation answers or refers.
2. Coordination reviews repeated cases, tax schemes, complaints about referrals and suspensions.
3. If course content turns out to be wrong or outdated, it's logged for the next version.

## 7. Monthly metrics

| Most-asked topics | Improve lessons and sessions |

| "Introduce your business" posts | Community life |
