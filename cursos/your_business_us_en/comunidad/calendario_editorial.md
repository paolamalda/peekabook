## WhatsApp channel format

- Maximum 5 lines, no abbreviations.
- One link only, always to the course, the forum or an official site.
- Every post ends the same way: "Desarrolla Talento never asks for your SSN, ITIN, passwords or immigration status."

**Alert template:**
"⚠️ Alert: [scam]. How to spot it: [sign]. What to do: [action]. More in [lesson]. Desarrolla Talento never asks for your SSN, ITIN, passwords or immigration status."

**Estimated payment template:**
"📅 Your estimated tax payment is due on [date]. Use what you set aside and pay at irs.gov; never from a link in a message. More in M5 U04."

**Challenge template:**
"🎯 [Month] challenge: [challenge]. It takes [time]. When you finish it, share it in Wins, without personal data."
