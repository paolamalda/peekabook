# How to ask and introduce your business

A clear question gets a useful answer faster.

## Question template

**Module or topic:** (for example, M5 estimated payments)
**My business, without personal data:** (for example, "I sell food, about 3 thousand a month, mostly cash")
**What I already checked:** (for example, "I read lesson M5 U04")
**My specific question:** (one question, not five)

## What the community can and can't solve

| Can | Can't |
|---|---|
| Questions about what the course explains | Review your actual returns or invoices |
| How to calculate price, cash flow or break-even | Tell you which bank, processor or platform to use |
| Which institution to go to | Do IRS or state paperwork for you |
| How to recognize a scam | Log into your IRS account or your bank |

## "Introduce your business" template

**What you do:** (one or two lines)
**Where:** (city or "online")
**Something from the course you already apply:** (one line)
**Where to find you:** (your public social media or business page)

## Answering others

You can share your experience: say it's your experience, not a rule; don't recommend brands, preparers or suppliers by name; if you're not sure, point to the lesson or the official institution. Remember that taxes depend on each case.
