# Your calendar for the year

Dates that matter to entrepreneurs in the United States. We remind you of each one in **Announcements** and on the channel.

| Month | Reminder | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| January 15 | Fourth estimated payment for the previous year | M5 U04 |
| January | Check the new retirement limits, mileage rate and minimum wage | M1 U04, M8 U01, M9 U01 |
| January and February | Your 1099s arrive; send any 1099-NECs you owe | M5 U04 |
| April 15 | Annual return and first estimated payment | M5 U03, M5 U04 |
| April | In California, your LLC's minimum tax, if you have one | M5 U01 |
| May | Check your retirement estimate at ssa.gov | M9 U01 |
| June 15 | Second estimated payment | M5 U04 |
| July | Mid-year dashboard: sales, margin, profit | M8 U03 |
| August | Review your business insurance | M7 U02 |
| September 15 | Third estimated payment | M5 U04 |
| October | Set money aside for the slow season and year-end expenses | M3 U03 |
| November to January | Health insurance open enrollment | M7 U01 |
| December | Contribute to your retirement account and fill in your one-page plan | M9 U01, M9 U03 |

Your sales tax filing dates depend on the frequency the CDTFA or your state assigns you. Official dates can change; confirm them with the IRS and your state.
