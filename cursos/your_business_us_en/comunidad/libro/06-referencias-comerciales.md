# How commercial referrals work

We want you to know exactly what is program content and what is a commercial offer.

## Where they appear

- **Only** in the **Commercial referrals** forum and the WhatsApp channel.
- **Never** in lessons, activities, self-assessments, badges, the certificate or the monthly session.

## What they always say

> **Commercial referral:** Desarrolla Talento may receive a commission if you use this service. Compare with at least one other option. Using it is voluntary and doesn't affect your access, points or certificate.

## Before using any service

- Check the institution's license with your state regulator (M6 U02).
- Compare the total cost with your real numbers (M4 U01 and M6 U03).
- Ask for the proposal in writing and compare with at least one other option.

## What you'll never see

- Merchant cash advances or payday loans.
- "No credit check" loans with an up-front fee.
- Schemes to "pay less tax."
- Pressure to decide.
