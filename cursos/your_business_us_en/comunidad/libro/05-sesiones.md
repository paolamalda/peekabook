# Monthly session and challenges

## Monthly live session

One video session a month. The date and link are posted in **Announcements** and on the channel.

- Length: 60 minutes.
- One topic of the month, an exercise with made-up numbers and questions.
- We never open your IRS account, bank or platform in front of the group.
- If there's a guest (tax preparer, SBDC adviser), it's general orientation.

## Monthly challenges

Each month we post a short challenge in **Announcements**. When you complete it, share it in **Wins**, without personal data or real amounts.

| Month | Challenge |
|---|---|
| 1 | Log sales, expenses and miles 7 days in a row (M1 U04) |
| 2 | Open your business account and your tax account (M1 U02 and U03) |
| 3 | Set your salary and your tax percentage (M1 U03) |
| 4 | Figure the real cost of your main product (M2 U01) |
| 5 | Figure your break-even point and daily target (M2 U03) |
| 6 | Build your cash-flow calendar with your estimated payments (M3 U01) |
| 7 | Turn on alerts and two-step verification (M4 U02) |
| 8 | Check in CalGold or with your city which permits your business needs (M5 U02) |
| 9 | Request your IRS IP PIN (M5 U04) |
| 10 | Get a quote for business insurance (M7 U02) |
| 11 | Review your health insurance options before open enrollment (M7 U01) |
| 12 | Check your estimate at ssa.gov and fill in your one-page plan (M9) |

## Community survey

Twice a year we ask for your opinion in the **Community survey** activity. It's anonymous.
