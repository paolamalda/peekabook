# Welcome to the community

**Your Business Community · U.S.** is the space for people taking *Your Business, Your Money, Your Future* in the United States. It complements the course: here you ask questions, share progress, meet other businesses, learn about tax dates and warn each other about scams.

## What you'll find

| Space | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Announcements** | Tax dates, reminders, the monthly session and alerts. Only the team posts. |
| **Introduce yourself** | Tell us who you are, what business you have and what you want to achieve. |
| **Question forums** | Three forums by topic: money, price and cash flow; taxes and formality; credit, protection and future. |
| **Introduce your business** | One post a month to tell people what you do. No financial offers. |
| **Scam alerts** | Warn about "IRS" calls, government-imitation letters, overpayment checks or fake suppliers. |
| **Wins** | Celebrate progress: you paid yourself a salary, calculated your price, got your EIN. |
| **Monthly live session** | One topic a month and questions. |
| **Commercial referrals** | Services with a commercial agreement, separate and labeled. Optional. |

## What it is and isn't

- **It is** a place to learn, ask and support each other as entrepreneurs.
- **It isn't** personalized tax, legal or accounting advice. For your specific case, we point you to the IRS, VITA, an SBDC or a professional. We don't give immigration advice.
- **It isn't** a place to offer loans, investments or schemes to "pay less tax."

## How to recognize the team

The team **never** asks for your SSN, ITIN, EIN, passwords, account numbers, your business's real amounts or your immigration status. If someone does in the community's name, it's a scam: report it in **Scam alerts**.

## How to start

1. Read the rules (chapter 2).
2. Introduce yourself in **Introduce yourself**.
3. Note the date of the monthly session (chapter 5).
4. When you have a question, use the template in chapter 3.
