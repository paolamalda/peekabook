# Rules and privacy

These rules protect your information and everyone else's.

## Your data

- **Never post** SSN, ITIN, EIN, passwords, account or card numbers, codes, real sales amounts, screenshots of your bank or IRS account, or your or anyone else's immigration status.
- If you want to give an example, use **made-up or rounded numbers**: "I sell about 3 thousand a month."
- Forums don't allow attachments, to avoid sharing documents by mistake.

## Respect

- No mocking or judging: everyone starts somewhere, formal or not.
- No personal arguments or posts about customers, suppliers or others by full name.

## Not allowed

1. Offering loans, investments, referral "businesses" or financial products.
2. Offering schemes to "pay less tax" or lending your tax number.
3. Recommending "notarios" or fixers who "take care of" taxes, papers or credit.
4. Posting shortened links or unofficial sites for official procedures.
5. Promoting your business outside the **Introduce your business** forum.
6. Impersonating the team, an authority or another person.

## Introduce your business

One post a month per person: what you do, where and how to reach you through your public channels. No misleading prices, no financial offers and no one's personal data.

## If something looks like a scam

Post it in **Scam alerts** without personal data, or message the team privately. If it already affected you: keep evidence, tell your bank or payment processor and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov (M4 U03 and M7 U03).

## What the team does

- Removes posts with personal or tax data and lets you know privately.
- Removes posts selling financial products or inviting people to invest.
- Answers general questions and refers personal cases.
