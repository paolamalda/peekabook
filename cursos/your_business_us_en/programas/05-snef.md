# National Financial Education Week (SNEF)

Every year, CONDUSEF holds Mexico's **National Financial Education Week (SNEF)**, with workshops, talks and games about saving, credit, insurance and avoiding scams. **It's at no cost.** You can go in person or use its online platform from anywhere, including the United States.

> **Current fact:** SNEF 2026 runs October 15 to 18 at the Monumento a la Revolución, Mexico City, from 9:00 to 17:00. Its online platform is open from October 15, 2026 to March 31, 2027. Accessed October 6, 2026 through CONDUSEF.
