# Where to get no-cost help in the U.S.

If you have a problem and don't know who to call, these options help at no cost, in English and Spanish:

- **211:** call or text 211 to find local help with food, rent, utilities, health or legal advice.
- **VITA:** no-cost tax preparation for people with low or moderate income. Find a site at 800-906-9887.
- **CFPB:** complaints about banks, cards, loans, debt collectors or money transfers. 855-411-2372.
- **Free legal help:** LawHelp.org lists organizations in your state.

For immigration matters, go only to attorneys or representatives accredited by the Department of Justice. Never to a "notario."
