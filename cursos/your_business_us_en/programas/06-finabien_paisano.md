# FINABIEN Paisano card to send money

The **FINABIEN Paisano card** is from the Mexican government and lets you send money from the United States to Mexico for a low fee. You get it at no cost at Mexican consulates, with a valid official ID.

- A fee of **2.99 dollars** per transfer, from 100 to 2,500 dollars.
- Up to 2,500 dollars a day and 10,000 a month.
- Because the transfer is electronic, **you don't pay the 1% tax** charged on cash transfers.

Always compare how many pesos your family receives, not just the fee.

> **Current fact:** 2026 fee and limits. Accessed October 6, 2026 through FINABIEN and national news outlets.
