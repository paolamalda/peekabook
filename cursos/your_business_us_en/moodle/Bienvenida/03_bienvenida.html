How to post in the forum

1. Open the forumIt is above the course parts.
2. «Add a new discussion topic»Write a short subject: «Question about the credit lesson».
3. Ask and sendUse made-up or rounded numbers. You’ll be notified when someone replies.

Golden rules

  • Your data is yours: Do not share your SSN, ITIN, EIN, passwords, account numbers, your business's real amounts or your immigration status.
  • No one on the team will ask for personal data, passwords or money. If someone does it in our name, it’s fraud.
  • Always respectful: no judging, no selling and no ads.
  • What you share is confidential: the organization offering the course does not receive your personal or financial data.

If you need urgent help

Don’t wait for the forum:
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text, English and Spanish)
  • CFPB: consumerfinance.gov (complaints about financial companies)
  • 211 (local help with food, rent and bills)
  • National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233
  • 911 (emergencies)
