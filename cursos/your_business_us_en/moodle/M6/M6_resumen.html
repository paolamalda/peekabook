Module outcome: Knowing whether you need credit, what it really costs and how not to mix it with personal debt.

Lessons: 5 · Estimated time: 50 to 75 minutes, plus activities and self-assessment.

  1. Do you need credit?
  2. Credit options and how to check them
  3. The total cost: APR, fees and guarantees
  4. Don't mix debts, and be careful about cosigning
  5. Cosigner, guarantor, authorized user and reference: what are you signing?

In this section: the lesson book, one "What would you do?" activity per lesson and the module self-assessment.
