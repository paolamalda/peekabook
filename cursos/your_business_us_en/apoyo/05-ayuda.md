# Where to get help

All options in this table are official or nonprofit. Information checked on September 29, 2026; confirm it on the official site.

| Need | Where | Cost | What to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| EIN, ITIN, estimated payments, 1099s | IRS: irs.gov | Free | Go to the official site yourself |
| Preparing your return | VITA (moderate income); preparers with a PTIN; CPA | Free or paid | That they sign and give you a copy |
| Business advice | SBDC and SCORE (SBA) | Free or low cost | Advisers from the official network |
| Permits in California | CalGold; your city and county | Varies | Official offices |
| Seller's permit and sales tax | CDTFA | Permit at no cost | Your city's rate |
| LLC and business name | Secretary of State; your county (DBA) | Paid | No middlemen |
| Contractor license | CSLB | Paid | $1,000 limit |
| Employers and minimum wage | EDD and DIR | Free | ABC test |
| Loans | Banks, credit unions, CDFIs, SBA microloans | Depends on credit | License with the DFPI |
| Health insurance | Covered California, Medi-Cal, community clinics | Based on income | Certified enrollers |
| Scams | ReportFraud.ftc.gov; FBI IC3 | Free | Keep evidence |
| Identity theft | IdentityTheft.gov | Free | Recovery plan |
| Trademark | USPTO; state registry | Paid | Only uspto.gov |
| Retirement | ssa.gov; CalSavers | Free | Your my Social Security account |
| Immigration questions | Attorneys or organizations accredited by the Department of Justice | Free or paid | Never "notarios" |

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I take the course if I file with an ITIN or don't file yet?** Yes. The course explains how the system works. We never ask about your situation.

**Do I have to give real data about my business?** No. Use made-up numbers.

**Does the course tell me whether to open an LLC?** It explains what it protects and what it costs. You decide with your preparer or an attorney.

**Do you recommend a bank, processor or platform?** No. We teach you how to compare.

**What do I do if someone calls "from the IRS"?** Hang up and go to irs.gov yourself (M5 U04).

**What do I do if I'm overpaid and asked to send back the difference?** Don't send anything back: it's a scam (M4 U03).

**Is the certificate official?** It's educational recognition from the program. It isn't a license.

**What if a fact has changed?** Confirm it on the official site and use the current information.
