# Welcome

**Your Business, Your Money, Your Future · U.S.** is a financial education program for Latino entrepreneurs and self-employed people in the United States.

Here you'll learn to separate business and household money, set prices, manage your cash flow, get paid without losing, formalize and pay your taxes without fear, use credit carefully, protect your business and plan for your future.

You don't need to know about finance or taxes. Each lesson starts from a real situation and ends with an action you can take this week. We use federal rules and California examples; if you live in another state, we tell you what to check.

Version 1.2 · September 2026. Also available in Spanish.

## How each module works

| Part | What you'll find | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Lesson book** | Each lesson starts with a cover page where you choose your path. | 5 or 10 minutes per lesson |
| **What would you do?** | One activity per lesson: three situations to choose the best decision. | 3 minutes |
| **Module self-assessment** | Questions from all the module's lessons. You pass with 70% and can try as many times as you want. | 10 to 15 minutes |

## Choose your path in each lesson

- **Quick path (5 min):** read *The essentials* and go to *Practice*.
- **Full path (10 min):** add *Go deeper*, with cases, common mistakes and more facts.

## The characters

- **Don Ramón** (60): runs a food truck in Sacramento.

## Three program rules

1. **We will never ask for your personal or tax data or your immigration status**: no SSN, ITIN, EIN, passwords or account numbers. If someone asks in the program's name, it's a scam.
2. **We don't sell you anything.** Lessons contain no recommendations of banks, processors, platforms or loans.
3. **Orientation, not advice.** Review your tax situation with a tax preparer or CPA, and immigration questions with an attorney or accredited organization.

## Facts change

Limits, rates and rules change every year. The program's facts were checked on **September 29, 2026**, and each one names its source. Before deciding, check the **IRS**, your state or the institution's official site.

## Points, levels and badges

You earn points for each completed activity. There are six levels (Idea, Launch, Up and running, Formal, Growing and Established) and eight badges. Nobody sees your name on a leaderboard.

## Certificate

When you pass all nine self-assessments you get the **Full plan** badge and your **certificate of completion** with a verification code. It's educational recognition from the program; it's not a license or an official accreditation.
