# Protect your business

## Your health when you work for yourself

**To read and check**

- **Covered California** (English and Spanish): [coveredca.com](https://www.coveredca.com)  
  **What to look for:** "self-employed".
- **Medi-Cal** (DHCS · English): [dhcs.ca.gov](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov)  
  **What to look for:** "Medi-Cal".
- **Health insurance marketplace** (HealthCare.gov · English): [healthcare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov)  
  **What to look for:** enrollment dates and whether you qualify for help with the monthly cost.

**Sources for this lesson:** Covered California · DHCS · HealthCare.gov · EDD, accessed September 29, 2026.
