# M9 U01 | Your retirement when you work for yourself
objetivo: Learn how to save for retirement when you're self-employed: Social Security, IRAs, SEP IRAs and Solo 401(k)s.
gancho: Don Ramón is 60. He thinks "the food truck is his retirement." But he doesn't know how much he'd get from Social Security or whether it's enough. In this lesson you'll see your options.

== esencial

--- paso | fa-question | Your business isn't your retirement
Your business depends on you working. If you get sick or tired, it may stop paying. That's why you need retirement savings **separate** from the business.

--- paso | fa-university | Social Security
The self-employment tax you pay (M5 U03) counts toward your Social Security. To qualify for a retirement benefit, you generally need 40 credits (about 10 years of work). Check your record and estimate in my Social Security (ssa.gov).

> **Current fact:** in 2026 you earn one Social Security credit for every $1,890 in earnings, up to 4 credits a year ($7,560). Accessed September 29, 2026 through the Social Security Administration (SSA).

--- tarjetas | fa-th-large | Retirement accounts for the self-employed
* fa-user | Traditional or Roth IRA | For anyone with earnings. | Up to $7,500 in 2026 ($8,600 at 50 or older). | si
* fa-briefcase | SEP IRA | Easy for owners to open. | Up to 25% of adjusted profit, maximum $72,000. | si
* fa-line-chart | Solo 401(k) | For owners with no employees. | $24,500 as the employee plus the business's contribution, maximum $72,000. | si
* fa-cubes | Only the business | Depends on your health. | Risky. | no

> **Current fact:** for 2026, the IRA contribution limit is $7,500 ($8,600 at age 50 or older). A SEP IRA allows up to 25% of compensation and a maximum of $72,000. A Solo 401(k) allows $24,500 as the employee and $72,000 in total, not counting age-based catch-up contributions. Accessed September 29, 2026 through the IRS.

--- paso | fa-user | A case in one minute
Don Ramón checked his account at ssa.gov: he has 52 credits and an estimate of $1,300 a month at 67. He opened an IRA and contributes $300 a month. He knows that each year he waits to claim, his monthly benefit goes up.

> **Key idea:** keep your retirement separate from your business; check your Social Security and put something into a retirement account every month.

--- comprueba
1. How many credits do you generally need for a Social Security retirement benefit? || 40 credits.
2. Which retirement account can anyone with earnings open? || A traditional or Roth IRA.

--- recuerda
- Retirement separate from the business.
- Check ssa.gov.
- A fixed contribution every month.

== profundiza

--- tema | fa-percent | Tax advantages
Contributions to a traditional IRA, SEP or Solo 401(k) can lower your taxes today. With a Roth you pay taxes today and withdraw without taxes in retirement, if you follow the rules. Withdrawing before age 59½ usually carries a penalty. Ask your preparer which one fits you.

--- tema | fa-id-card-o | If you file with an ITIN
Earnings are recorded with Social Security under a Social Security number. If you currently file with an ITIN, ask the SSA or a professional how your earnings might be recognized in the future. Some institutions open IRAs with an ITIN.

--- tema | fa-university | CalSavers
In California, CalSavers is the state retirement savings program. Employers with employees and no other plan must offer it, and it's also open to self-employed people.

--- casos
### Case 1. Don Ramón's retirement

### Case 3. Daniela's savings
Daniela is 27 and thinks it's too early for retirement.
? What does she do? || She starts with a small, fixed IRA contribution.

--- errores
* The business as a pension | No income if it fails | Separate savings
* Not checking ssa.gov | Surprises | Check your record
* Withdrawing early | Penalties | Let it grow
* Starting late | Less savings | Start small

# M9 U02 | If the business changes or you're not there
objetivo: Prepare to close, pause or hand over your business, and leave your information in order in case you're not there.
gancho: Lupita was sick for three weeks. Her daughter didn't know which supplier to pay, where the accounts were or how to collect on orders. In this lesson you'll see how to prepare your business for changes.

== esencial

--- tarjetas | fa-th-large | Three situations
* fa-pause | Pause | Season, illness, move. | Give notice and get organized. | igual
* fa-times | Close | The business no longer pays. | Formal closing. | igual
* fa-exchange | Hand over | To a relative or someone else. | Written agreement. | igual
* fa-user-times | If you're not there | Nobody knows what to do. | Leave your information in order. | no

--- pasos | fa-folder-open | Your business folder
1. List of suppliers, customers and contacts.
2. Business accounts and who can operate them (no written passwords).
3. Debts, loans and pending payments.
4. Tax numbers, permits, tax preparer and dates.
5. Insurance and beneficiaries.

--- paso | fa-user | A case in one minute
Lupita put together a folder with suppliers, accounts, payment dates and her preparer's contact. She added her daughter as an authorized user on the business account. The next time she was out, orders kept going.

> **Key idea:** put in one folder what someone would need to keep your business running, or to close it, without you.

--- comprueba
1. What goes in your business folder? || Suppliers, accounts, debts, tax information, permits, insurance and contacts.
2. Why not write down passwords? || Because if someone finds the folder, they can steal your accounts.

--- recuerda
- Pause, close or hand over in an orderly way.
- Business folder.
- Never written passwords.

== profundiza

--- tema | fa-times | Closing properly
File your last return and mark it as final, if it applies. Cancel your seller's permit and licenses. If you have an LLC, formally dissolve it: in California, if you don't, the $800 minimum tax keeps running every year.

--- tema | fa-users | Handing over the business
If a relative will continue the business, agree in writing on when, under what conditions and what happens with equipment, inventory and debts. Permits and licenses are almost always in your name and must be applied for again.

--- tema | fa-file-text-o | Will and beneficiaries
The business's equipment, inventory and money are part of your estate. A will or living trust and up-to-date beneficiaries on accounts, IRAs and insurance spare your family long proceedings. There cost nothing or low-cost legal clinics.

> **Before you act, check:** the steps to close with the IRS, your state and your city. Talk to an attorney about a will or transfer.

--- casos
### Case 1. Lupita's illness
Lupita's daughter didn't know how to keep orders going.
? What does Lupita prepare? || A folder with suppliers, accounts, payments and contacts.

### Case 2. Daniela's LLC
Daniela will stop selling and won't use her LLC anymore.
? What does she do? || She formally dissolves it so she doesn't keep paying $800 a year.

### Case 3. Don Ramón's food truck
Don Ramón wants his daughter to take over the food truck in a few years.
? What does he do? || He makes a written agreement and reviews his will or trust.

--- errores
* Everything in your head | Nobody can continue | Folder
* Written passwords | Account theft | Authorizations
* Leaving the LLC open | $800 a year | Dissolve it
* Handing over by word of mouth | Family conflict | In writing

# M9 U03 | Your one-page plan
objetivo: Bring together on one page the decisions from the course: money, price, cash flow, formality, protection and future.
gancho: Daniela learned a lot, but it's in scattered notes. She wants one page she can pin next to her worktable. In this lesson you'll put together your one-page plan.

== esencial

--- paso | fa-file-o | Why one page
One page forces you to choose what matters. You pin it where you can see it and review it every three months.

--- tabla | fa-table | Your one-page plan
| Topic | My decision |
|---|---|
| Separate money | Business account: ___ · Tax account: ___ · My salary: ___ |
| Price | Cost of my main product: ___ · Price: ___ · Margin: ___ |
| Break-even point | ___ a month · ___ a day |
| Cash flow | Reserve goal: ___ · % I set aside: ___ · Credit rules: ___ |
| Getting paid | Payment methods: ___ · Anti-scam plan: yes / no |
| Formality | Structure: ___ · EIN and permits: ___ · Estimated payment dates: ___ |
| Credit | Do I need it? ___ · APR and total repayment: ___ |
| Protection | Health insurance: ___ · Business insurance: ___ · Trademark: ___ |
| Monthly numbers | Review on day ___ |
| Future | Social Security checked: yes / no · Retirement account: ___ · Folder: yes / no |
| My commitment | Goal: ___ · Amount: ___ every ___ · Witness: ___ · Reminder: day ___ |

--- paso | fa-calendar | Review every three months
Set a fixed date, for example alongside your estimated payments. Update the numbers, check whether you followed through and change one decision if needed.

--- paso | fa-user | A case in one minute
Daniela filled in her page in one afternoon. Two things were missing: her retirement account and checking her trademark. She made them goals for next quarter.

--- paso | fa-handshake-o | Your commitment
Choose one goal for the quarter with a name ("two-month reserve for winter"), how much you'll set aside and how often. Tell someone you trust or your community group. Set up an automatic transfer on the day you get paid and a monthly reminder with your goal's name.

> **Key idea:** your plan fits on one page. Review it every three months, improve one thing at a time and keep your commitment with a name, a witness and a reminder.

--- comprueba
1. How often do you review your plan? || Every three months.
2. What do you do if something is missing? || Make it a goal for next quarter.

--- recuerda
- One page.
- Where you can see it.
- Quarterly review.

== profundiza

--- tema | fa-list-ol | Prioritize
If a lot is missing, start with: 1) separating money and setting aside taxes, 2) a price with margin, 3) a reserve, 4) health and business insurance. The rest can wait a few months.

--- tema | fa-users | Share it
Share your plan with someone you trust or in the community (with no personal data or real amounts). A no-cost adviser from an {{SBDC|Small Business Development Centers: no-cost or low-cost business advice.}} or {{SCORE|A network of volunteer business mentors, supported by the SBA.}} can also review it with you.

--- tema | fa-lightbulb-o | Why it works
Studies in several countries found that naming a goal, committing to someone and getting a monthly reminder with that goal helps people save more. Setting money aside automatically, before you see it, also helps.

--- casos
### Case 1. Daniela's page
Daniela is missing her retirement account and her trademark.
? What does she do? || She makes them goals for next quarter.

### Case 2. Javier's priorities
Javier has many pending tasks and doesn't know where to start.
? What does he do? || He starts by separating money, setting aside taxes and his price.

### Case 3. Don Ramón's review
Don Ramón made his plan three months ago and hasn't reviewed it.
? What does he do? || He sets a fixed date and updates his numbers.

--- errores
* Scattered notes | They get forgotten | One page
* Wanting everything at once | You get overwhelmed | Prioritize
* Not reviewing | It gets outdated | Every three months
* Keeping it in a drawer | You don't see it | Pin it up

# M9 U04 | If you're not there: beneficiary accounts, your home, digital access and taxes
objetivo: Complete your succession plan in the U.S.: beneficiary accounts, transfer on death deed, small estates, the business's digital access, taxes when inheriting, and what you own in Mexico.
gancho: Don Ramón has his food truck, a business account, a house in Sacramento and land in Michoacán. His daughter believes that if he dies "the IRS takes half" and the house will spend years in court. In this lesson you'll see what's true and what to prepare.

== esencial

--- tarjetas | fa-th-large | Passing things on without court
* fa-university | Payable-on-death (POD) accounts | Bank and credit union accounts. | They pass directly. | si
* fa-line-chart | Transfer-on-death (TOD) investments | Brokerage accounts and IRAs. | They pass directly. | si
* fa-home | Transfer on death deed | In California, for your home. | Avoids probate. | si
* fa-file-text | Living trust or will | For everything else. | With a lawyer. | si
* fa-laptop | Digital | Accounts, platforms, social media. | Who has access. | igual

--- paso | fa-balance-scale | Taxes when inheriting
Heirs **don't pay federal tax** for receiving an inheritance. The federal estate tax only applies to estates over 15 million dollars per person in 2026, and California has no inheritance tax. If your heirs sell what they inherit, the gain is measured from its value when you died, which usually lowers the tax.

> **Current fact:** the federal estate tax only applies to estates over 15 million dollars per person in 2026; California has no inheritance tax. Accessed September 30, 2026 through the IRS and specialized media.

--- paso | fa-globe | If you own something in Mexico
Receiving an inheritance in Mexico pays no income tax (ISR). But putting land or a house in your name does cost money: state tax and notary. If your heirs live in the U.S. and receive a foreign inheritance of more than 100,000 dollars, they must report it to the IRS. They use Form 3520, even though they owe no tax. The penalty for not filing can reach 25% of the value. Mexican consulates offer notary services, including wills.

--- paso | fa-user | A case in one minute
Don Ramón named his daughter as POD beneficiary on his accounts. He reviewed a transfer on death deed for his home with a lawyer. He made a will at the consulate for the land in Michoacán. And he wrote down who can access the business accounts.

> **Key idea:** with beneficiaries on your accounts, a transfer on death deed for your home and a will or living trust, your family avoids court. Inheriting pays no federal tax, except for huge estates. A large foreign inheritance is reported on Form 3520.

--- comprueba
1. Do heirs pay federal tax? || No; the estate tax only applies to estates over 15 million.
2. What is Form 3520? || A report to the IRS of foreign inheritances or gifts over 100,000 dollars.

--- recuerda
- POD and TOD on your accounts.
- Transfer on death deed for your home.
- Form 3520 if you inherit from abroad.

== profundiza

--- tabla | fa-table | Your succession map
| Asset | How it passes without court |
|---|---|
| Bank or credit union account | POD beneficiary |
| Investment account or IRA | TOD or named beneficiary |
| House in California | Transfer on death deed or living trust |
| Equipment and business | Will or living trust, partner agreement |
| House or land in Mexico | Will (notary or consulate); succession list if ejido land |
| Digital accounts | Access instructions stored safely |

--- tema | fa-file-text | Small estates
If what you leave is small, California has a small estate affidavit that avoids probate. The limit changes every few years: ask the court or a lawyer.

--- tema | fa-home | Your home in Mexico: usufruct
Maybe you own a house or land in Mexico and want to pass it to your children while you're alive, but keep using it. Mexico has a gift with **reserved usufruct** (usufructo), done before a notary. In the U.S., a similar tool is a life estate. Ask your consulate before you sign.

--- casos

### Case 2. The land in Michoacán

### Case 3. Daniela's account

--- errores
* Believing the IRS takes half | Needless fear | Only huge estates
* Not naming beneficiaries | Long probate | POD and TOD
* Not reporting foreign inheritances | High penalties | Form 3520
* Leaving land in Mexico without a will | Family fights | Notary or consulate
