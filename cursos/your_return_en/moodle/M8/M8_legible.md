# Module 8. Don't get scammed when you return

## M8 U01. Fake fixers

**What you will be able to do:** Recognize people who charge for no-cost procedures or keep your papers.

**To start:** Outside the reception center, a man in a vest offered to «speed up» Don Rafa's CURP, birth certificate and support for 1,500 pesos. All he had to do was leave him his original papers. In this lesson you'll see why not.

### The essentials (5 minutes)

#### Who a fake fixer is

Someone who charges you for something that has no cost or that you can do yourself, and who sometimes keeps your papers or your information. They wait outside offices and reception centers, or find you on social media.



#### Signs

| Type | Description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Charges for no-cost things | CURP, INE, support, checking your weeks. | All of that has no cost. |
| Asks for originals | So «he can take them». | Never let them go. |
| Promises speed | «I know someone». | There's no shortcut. |
| Vest or badge | Looks official. | Not enough to believe him. |

#### What to do

Go into the office and ask at the service window. If someone says they're from the government, ask for their name and verify it inside. You can do the procedures in this course yourself.



#### A one-minute case

Don Rafa didn't give him his papers. He went in, asked at the window and was told everything had no cost. He did his paperwork that same day.

> **Key idea:** if they charge you for a no-cost procedure or ask for your originals, it isn't help: it's a fake fixer.



#### Check your understanding

1. How much does it cost to get your CURP or your INE?
*Answer:* Nothing; if they charge you, be wary.

2. What do you do if someone in a vest offers help outside?
*Answer:* You go in and ask at the window.


#### Remember

- No-cost things aren't paid for.
- Keep your originals with you.
- Ask at the window.



### Go deeper (5 more minutes)

#### No-cost procedures in this course

| Procedure | Does it cost anything? |
|---|---|
| Looking up and printing your CURP | No |
| INE voter ID | No |
| Locating your Afore | No |
| Checking your weeks at IMSS | No |
| Special credit report (once a year) | No |



#### Copies, not originals

For most procedures they ask to see the original and you keep it; they make a copy. Don't leave your originals with anyone outside the service window.



#### Cases


**Case 1. The vest**

A man in a vest offers to speed everything up for 1,500.
- *What does he do?* He goes in and asks at the window.


**Case 2. The Facebook group**

In a group for repatriated people, someone offers to «get your support» with your information.
- *What does he do?* He gives no information; he applies at the program's office.


**Case 3. The originals**

Memo is asked to leave his original birth certificate «for the procedure».
- *What does he do?* He hands over a copy and keeps his original.


#### Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Paying for no-cost things | You lose money | Ask at the window |
| Leaving originals | Identity theft | Copies |
| Trusting the vest | Deception | Verify inside |
| Giving information on social media | Fraud | Only at offices |

### Practice

#### Interactive activity

#### Quiz

1. Someone charges you to process your INE. What is it? a) A fake fixer · b) Normal · c) A legal express service
2. Where do you verify whether someone is from the government? a) By their vest · b) Inside the office · c) On their business card
3. What do you leave for a procedure? a) Your originals · b) Your PIN · c) Copies, and you show the original
**Answers:** 1-a: it has no cost. 2-b: ask inside. 3-c: copies.



#### Put it into practice

A fixer charges 1,500 for three no-cost procedures. How much do you save if you do them yourself?
**Answer:** 1,500 pesos.



#### Your plan

Review the table of no-cost procedures and save it on your phone.

### Key words

- *Fixer (gestor):* a person who does paperwork for others.
- *Original:* the real document, not the copy.
- *Service window (ventanilla):* the official place where you're served.

## M8 U02. Fraud and extortion against people who return

**What you will be able to do:** Recognize the most common frauds and extortion against people who return, and decide what to do.

**To start:** Lupita got a call saying they had her brother «detained at the border» and she needed to deposit 8,000 pesos right away. She could hear someone crying. In this lesson you'll see how to act.

### The essentials (5 minutes)

#### The most common

| Type | Description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Relative «detained» | They ask for an urgent deposit. | Extortion. |
| Job with upfront payment | «Pay for the training or uniform». | Fraud. |
| Miracle investment | «Double your dollars». | Fraud. |
| «I'll process your U.S. retirement» | They ask for your information and a payment. | Fraud. |

#### The golden rule

When you get an urgent call: **hang up, breathe and call** your relative or the institution yourself, using a number you already have. Criminals use urgency so you don't think.



#### If you already paid or they threaten you

Keep the numbers and screenshots. Report it to 089 (anonymous) or at the Fiscalía (public prosecutor). If it went through a bank, tell the bank right away.



#### A one-minute case

Lupita hung up and called her brother. He was at work in Dallas. She blocked the number and reported it to 089.

> **Key idea:** when someone rushes you, hang up and call yourself; no serious person asks for urgent deposits by phone.



#### Check your understanding

1. What do you do when an urgent call asks for money?
*Answer:* I hang up and call my relative myself with a number I already have.

2. What number do you call to report extortion?
*Answer:* 089, anonymously.


#### Remember

- Hang up and call yourself.
- No urgent deposits.
- 089 to report.



### Go deeper (5 more minutes)

#### Be careful what you post

Posting that you just returned, who you live with or where you work gives information to extortionists. Check who can see your social media.



#### Real job offers

A real job doesn't charge you to hire you. If they ask for payment for training, a uniform or a test before you start, be wary.



#### Cases


**Case 1. The detained brother**

Lupita is asked for 8,000 pesos to free her brother.
- *What does she do?* She hangs up and calls her brother herself.


**Case 2. The job with a fee**

Don Rafa is offered a job if he pays 1,200 for the uniform.
- *What does he do?* He doesn't pay: a real job doesn't charge to hire you.


**Case 3. Double your dollars**

Memo is offered to double his savings in three months.
- *What does he do?* He doesn't invest: no one doubles money without risk.


#### Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Depositing out of fear | You lose money | Hang up and call yourself |
| Paying for a job | Fraud | Don't pay |
| Believing in quick profits | You lose your savings | Be wary |
| Posting too much | They find you | Protect your social media |

### Practice

#### Interactive activity

**What would you do? (H5P):** three situations from this lesson with Lupita, Don Rafa and Memo. Choose the best decision in each one; if you miss, you can try again. You earn experience points.



#### Quiz

1. You get a call: your relative is detained and they want money now. What do you do? a) Hang up and call your relative yourself · b) Deposit · c) Give your information
2. A job asks you to pay for the uniform before starting. What is it? a) Normal · b) A benefit the employer gives you to start · c) A sign of fraud
3. What number do you call to report anonymously? a) Only 911 · b) 089 · c) The number that called you
**Answers:** 1-a: verify yourself. 2-c: you don't pay to work. 3-b: 089.



#### Put it into practice

You're offered to turn 10,000 into 20,000 in three months. What do you do?
**Answer:** I don't invest; it's a sign of fraud.



#### Your plan

Agree on a code word with your family for emergencies and save 089 on your phone.



### Learn more

- **Anonymous reporting** (Government of Mexico · Spanish): 089.
- **Financial fraud** (CONDUSEF · Spanish): https://www.condusef.gob.mx — **What to look for:** «fraudes» (fraud).

### Key words

- *Extortion:* a threat to make you hand over money.
- *Anonymous report:* a report without giving your name.
- *Code word:* a family password to confirm emergencies.

### Sources

CONDUSEF, fraud alerts; Executive Secretariat of the SNSP, 089 line; checked October 5, 2026.

## M8 U03. Your return plan on one page

**What you will be able to do:** Bring together on a single page your papers, your account, your credit history, what you left there, your retirement and your budget.

**To start:** Don Rafa has notes in three notebooks and photos on his phone. When someone asks him «how's it going?», he doesn't know where to start. In this lesson you'll put it all on one page.

### The essentials (5 minutes)

#### Your page has six parts

| Type | Description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Papers | What you have and what's missing. | M1. |
| Account | Your CLABE and alerts. | M2. |
| Credit | Your report checked. | M3. |
| What's back there | Wages, account, taxes, retirement. | M4. |
| Retirement | Afore and weeks. | M5. |
| 90 days | Budget and work. | M6 and M7. |

#### One action per part

For each part, write the next action and a date. You don't need to solve everything today; you need to know what's next.



#### A one-minute case

Don Rafa made his page. He still needs his INE (appointment on Monday), to ask for his W-2s (this week) and to locate his Afore (already done). Now he knows where to go next.

> **Key idea:** put everything on one page with the next action and a date for each part.



#### Check your understanding

1. How many parts does your plan have?
*Answer:* Six: papers, account, credit, what's back there, retirement and 90 days.

2. What do you write in each part?
*Answer:* The next action and a date.


#### Remember

- Six parts.
- One action per part.
- With a date.



### Go deeper (5 more minutes)

#### Your return plan

| Part | How I'm doing | Next action | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Papers | | | |
| Account | | | |
| Credit | | | |
| What's back there | | | |
| Retirement | | | |
| 90 days | | | |



#### Review it every month

Your situation changes fast in the first months. Review your page every month and cross off what you've done.



#### Cases


**Case 1. Three notebooks**

Don Rafa has scattered notes.
- *What does he do?* He brings everything together in his one-page plan.


**Case 2. Everything today**

Lupita wants to solve all six parts in one day.
- *What does she do?* She writes the next action for each part with a date.


**Case 3. The plan in a drawer**

Memo made his plan and forgot it in a drawer.
- *What does he do?* He reviews it every month and crosses off his progress.


#### Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Scattered notes | You don't see progress | One page |
| Wanting everything today | You get overwhelmed | One action per part |
| No dates | It stays an idea | Add a date |
| Not reviewing it | It's forgotten | Every month |

**Answer:** Example: «Go to the INE office on Monday the 12th with my birth certificate and proof of address».
