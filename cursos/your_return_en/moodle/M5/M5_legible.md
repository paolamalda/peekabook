# Module 5. Your Afore, your IMSS and your weeks

## M5 U01. Do you have an Afore? Find it

**What you will be able to do:** Check whether you have an Afore account from when you worked in Mexico, and locate it.

**To start:** Before leaving, Don Rafa worked six years at a factory in Morelia with IMSS. He doesn't know whether that money «got lost». In this lesson you'll see how to find it.

### The essentials (5 minutes)

#### Your Afore is waiting for you

If you worked in Mexico with IMSS after 1997, you have an Afore (retirement savings) account in your name. The money isn't lost because you left: it's still there and earning returns.



#### How to locate it

| Type | Description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| AforeMóvil app | With your CURP. | Tells you which Afore you're in. |
| e-SAR | CONSAR's website. | Locates your Afore. |
| SARTEL | 55 1328 5000. | No cost. |

#### If you never worked formally

If you don't have an Afore, you can open one with your CURP in the AforeMóvil app, even if you're self-employed. That way you start saving for retirement (U03).



#### A one-minute case

Don Rafa downloaded AforeMóvil, entered his CURP and his account appeared with the savings from his six years at the factory. He updated his phone number and address.

> **Key idea:** if you worked formally in Mexico, your Afore is still there; locate it with your CURP.



#### Check your understanding

1. Is your Afore lost if you went to the U.S.?
*Answer:* No: it's still there in your name.

2. What do you need to locate it?
*Answer:* Your CURP.


#### Remember

- Your Afore isn't lost.
- Locate it with your CURP.
- Update your details.

#### Register your beneficiaries

They're the people who would receive your savings if you pass away. Check them at your Afore; if you named them years ago, they may no longer be who you want.



#### Cases


**Case 1. «It got lost»**

Don Rafa thinks he lost his factory savings.
- *What does he do?* He looks for it with his CURP in AforeMóvil.


**Case 2. Never formal**

**Case 3. The Afore fixer**

- *What does she do?* She doesn't pay: the lookup is no cost and he does it with his CURP.

## M5 U02. Your contribution weeks

**What you will be able to do:** Look up your IMSS contribution weeks and calculate how many you're missing for a pension.

**To start:** Chayo heard that her husband «can't get a pension anymore because he was gone for many years». No one has checked how many weeks he has. In this lesson you'll see how to do it.

### The essentials (5 minutes)

#### Weeks aren't erased

Every week you worked with IMSS in Mexico stays on record. When you left, the count stopped, but it wasn't lost. You can check it at no cost on the IMSS website with your CURP and your social security number.



#### Which law applies to you?

| Type | Description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Ley 73 | You started contributing before July 1, 1997. | Requires 500 weeks. |
| Ley 97 | You started after that. | In 2026 it requires 875 weeks. |

#### Ley 97 goes up every year

Under Ley 97, the requirement goes up 25 weeks every year until it reaches 1,000 in 2031. If you're short of weeks, you can contribute again with a formal job or on your own (U03).



#### A one-minute case

Chayo's husband checked: he has 610 weeks and started in 1993, so he's under Ley 73. He's already over 500. When he reaches the age, he'll be able to review his pension.

> **Key idea:** your weeks aren't erased; check them and find out which law applies to you.



#### Check your understanding

1. What happens to your weeks when you go to the U.S.?
*Answer:* They stay on record; the count stops, but they aren't erased.

2. How many weeks does Ley 97 require in 2026?
*Answer:* 875.


#### Remember

- Weeks aren't erased.
- Ley 73: 500.
- Ley 97: 875 in 2026.



### Go deeper (5 more minutes)

#### How many Don Rafa is missing (Ley 97)

- Requirement in 2026: **875**
- Weeks he has: **312**
- Weeks he's missing: **563**


#### About 52 weeks per year

A full year of formal work adds about 52 weeks. 563 weeks takes almost 11 years; that's why it's best to start soon.



#### Cases


**Case 1. «He can't get it anymore»**

Chayo is told her husband lost everything.
- *What does she do?* They check his weeks at IMSS with his CURP.


**Case 2. Which law?**

Don Rafa started formal work in 2001.
- *What does he do?* He knows Ley 97 applies and calculates how many weeks he's missing.


**Case 3. Missing weeks**

Don Rafa is missing more than 500 weeks.
- *What does he do?* He looks for a formal job or contributes on his own.


#### Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Thinking they were erased | You don't collect | Check |
| Mixing up the law | You count wrong | Check your start date |
| Waiting until 60 | No time left | Start soon |
| Paying for the lookup | Fraud | It's no cost |

### Practice

#### Interactive activity

**What would you do? (H5P):** three situations from this lesson with Chayo and Don Rafa. Choose the best decision in each one; if you miss, you can try again. You earn experience points.



#### Quiz

1. You started contributing in 1990. Which law applies? a) Ley 97 · b) Ley 73 · c) Neither
2. How many weeks does Ley 97 require in 2026? a) 500 · b) 1,250 · c) 875
3. How many weeks does a full year of formal work add? a) About 52 · b) 12 · c) 365, one per day of the year
**Answers:** 1-b: before July 1997. 2-c: 875. 3-a: about 52.



#### Put it into practice

You have 700 weeks and you're under Ley 97. How many are you missing in 2026?
**Answer:** 175.



#### Your plan

Check your weeks on the IMSS website and write down how many you have and which law applies to you.



### Learn more

- **Contribution weeks certificate** (IMSS · Spanish): https://serviciosdigitales.imss.gob.mx — **What to look for:** «semanas cotizadas» (contribution weeks).

### Key words

- *Contribution weeks:* weeks worked with IMSS.
- *Ley 73 and Ley 97:* pension rules based on when you started contributing.
- *NSS:* social security number.

### Sources

## M5 U03. Save for retirement even if you're self-employed

**What you will be able to do:** Make voluntary contributions to your Afore, and explain how to enroll in IMSS if you're self-employed.

**To start:** Don Rafa now does masonry jobs on his own. He has no boss and no IMSS. He thinks retirement «isn't for him anymore». In this lesson you'll see he can still do something.

### The essentials (5 minutes)

#### Voluntary contributions

You can deposit into your Afore from 50 pesos at convenience stores, in the AforeMóvil app or by automatic debit. That money is yours and adds to your retirement.



#### Ways to save

| Type | Description | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| At the store | From 50 pesos. | With your CURP. |
| In the app | AforeMóvil. | Whenever you want. |
| Automatic debit | A fixed amount from your account. | Automatic. |

#### IMSS if you're self-employed

Self-employed workers can enroll in IMSS voluntarily and pay their contribution. It gives them medical care and counts weeks. Check the requirements and cost on the IMSS website.



#### A one-minute case

Don Rafa decided to set aside 200 pesos every two weeks for his Afore at the store. In a year he'll have saved 4,800 pesos plus returns.

> **Key idea:** even if you're self-employed, you can save in your Afore from 50 pesos.



#### Check your understanding

1. From how much can you make a voluntary contribution at a store?
*Answer:* From 50 pesos.

2. What does enrolling in IMSS as self-employed give you?
*Answer:* Medical care and contribution weeks.


#### Remember

- From 50 pesos.
- Store, app or automatic debit.
- Voluntary IMSS exists.



### Go deeper (5 more minutes)

#### Don Rafa's savings

- Every two weeks: **200**
- Pay periods a year: **24**
- In a year, plus returns: **4,800**


#### Long-term contributions

Voluntary contributions can have tax benefits if you leave them until retirement. Ask your Afore which type of contribution suits you.



#### Cases


**Case 1. «It's not for me»**

Don Rafa thinks that without a boss he can't save for retirement.
- *What does he do?* He contributes to his Afore every two weeks at the store.


**Case 2. No medical care**

Lupita sells food and doesn't have IMSS.
- *What does she do?* She checks the requirements to enroll as self-employed.


**Case 3. Saving with an acquaintance**

An acquaintance offers to keep Memo's retirement savings «with good interest».
- *What does he do?* He saves in his Afore, which is in his name.


#### Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Thinking you can't | You don't save | From 50 pesos |
| Waiting to have a boss | You lose years | Start now |
| Saving with acquaintances | Risk of losing it | In your Afore |
| Not checking voluntary IMSS | No medical care | Get informed |

### Practice

#### Interactive activity
