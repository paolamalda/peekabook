# Welcome

**Your Return, Your Money, Your Future** — Financial well-being program for people returning from the United States to Mexico, whether deported or by their own decision: 8 modules and 24 short lessons. Hybrid format: one in-person kickoff session and the rest online. Also available in Spanish and as a bilingual group.

Version 0.1 · October 2026.

## What you'll achieve

- **Your papers:** Your list of papers in order: repatriation record, CURP, birth certificate, INE and your children's papers.
- **Your account in Mexico:** An account in your name to receive support and money, and your returnee cards used well.
- **Credit from scratch:** Your first credit history in Mexico, starting small and with no traps.
- **What you left in the United States:** Your list of what was left there: last paycheck, accounts, taxes and retirement.
- **Your Afore, your IMSS and your weeks:** Your contribution weeks checked and your Afore located.
- **Your first 90 days:** Your return budget for the first three months.
- **Work or a business with what you know how to do:** Your plan to use what you know how to do: a job, a trade or a small business.
- **Don't get scammed when you return:** Your protocol against fake fixers, fraud and extortion targeting people who return.

## How each lesson works

| Part | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Start** | A short situation and what you'll achieve. |
| **The essentials** | What you must know, with the most common mistakes. |
| **Go deeper** | Math, cases and more facts, if you want to know more. |
| **Practice** | «What would you do?», three questions and your plan for this week. |

Each lesson takes **8 to 8 minutes** including practice. There are 24 lessons in 8 parts. Each part ends with a self-assessment: you pass with 70% and can try as many times as you want.

## Program rules

1. **We'll never ask for personal data**, passwords, PINs, your CURP or real amounts.
2. **We don't sell you anything** or recommend institutions: we teach you to verify them.
3. **Your information is yours.** If an organization offers you the course, it doesn't receive your personal or financial data.
4. **No immigration or legal advice is given:** participants are referred to consulates, the Embassy and public offices.

## Facts change

The program's facts were checked on **October 5, 2026** and each lesson lists its sources. Before deciding, check the current information on the official site.

## Points, badges and certificate

You earn points for each activity and there are 8 badges. When you pass all the self-assessments you get the **Complete plan** badge and your **certificate of completion**. It's an educational recognition, not a license or official accreditation.
