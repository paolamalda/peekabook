# Practice exercises

Solve each exercise. The answers are at the end.

| Key | Lesson | Exercise |
|---|---|---|
| P01 | M1 U01 | You have your record and your CURP, but no birth certificate or INE. Which two papers are missing from the basic list? |
| P02 | M1 U02 | You have your birth certificate and your repatriation record. You live with your mom and the electric bill is in her name. What do you need to confirm before going to the office? |
| P03 | M1 U03 | You have two U.S.-born children with birth certificates. Which two Mexican papers will they have when the process is done? |
| P04 | M2 U01 | You received 2,000 pesos. The bus costs 650 and your papers 300. How much is left? |
| P05 | M2 U02 | An account charges 85 pesos a month. How much do you pay in a year? |
| P06 | M2 U03 | How many digits does a CLABE have? |
| P07 | M3 U01 | You requested your Buró report in January. From what month can you request it again at no cost? |
| P08 | M3 U02 | Your limit is 4,500. How much is one third? |
| P09 | M3 U03 | You're offered 12 weekly payments of 450 for a phone that costs 3,900 in cash. How much extra do you pay? |
| P10 | M4 U01 | Your account back there charges 12 dollars a month and has 150. In how many months will it be empty? |
| P11 | M4 U02 | 2,400 dollars were withheld and what you owed in taxes was 1,700. How much would your refund be? |
| P12 | M4 U03 | You have 8,000 dollars in your 401(k). If 30% in total is withheld and penalized, how much would you receive? |
| P13 | M5 U01 | Besides the balance, what two things do you check when you find your Afore? |
| P14 | M5 U02 | You have 700 weeks and you're under Ley 97. How many are you missing in 2026? |
| P15 | M5 U03 | You contribute 300 pesos a month. How much in two years, not counting returns? |
| P16 | M6 U01 | 9,000 comes in a month and you spend 7,200 on the basics. How much is left? |
| P17 | M6 U02 | You have 9,000 in savings and need 2,500 a month to cover your expenses. How much can you give away over three months without falling short? |
| P18 | M6 U03 | 10,500 comes in and the basics cost 9,200. How much is left for everything else? |
| P19 | M7 U01 | Write one sentence about your experience with your trade, years and what you coordinated. |
| P20 | M7 U02 | You earn 2,100 a week. What is your daily wage? |
| P21 | M7 U03 | You sell 120 pieces a week and keep 12 pesos per piece. How much do you keep per week? |
| P22 | M8 U01 | A fixer charges 1,500 for three no-cost procedures. How much do you save if you do them yourself? |
| P23 | M8 U02 | You're offered to turn 10,000 into 20,000 in three months. What do you do? |
| P24 | M8 U03 | You still need your INE, your W-2s and your credit report. Write the next action for one of them with a date. |

## Answers

- **P01:** The birth certificate and the INE.
- **P02:** Whether they accept your mom's bill as proof of address.
- **P03:** A Mexican birth certificate and a CURP.
- **P04:** 1,050 pesos.
- **P05:** 1,020 pesos.
- **P06:** 18.
- **P07:** From January of the next year.
- **P08:** 1,500 pesos.
- **P09:** 1,500 pesos (12 times 450 is 5,400).
- **P10:** In 13 months (12.5 rounded up).
- **P11:** 700 dollars.
- **P12:** 5,600 dollars.
- **P13:** Your contact details and your beneficiaries.
- **P14:** 175.
- **P15:** 7,200 pesos.
- **P16:** 1,800 pesos.
- **P17:** 1,500 pesos.
- **P18:** 1,300 pesos.
- **P19:** Example: «8 years in a restaurant kitchen, I prepared 200 dishes per shift and trained two helpers».
- **P20:** 300 pesos.
- **P21:** 1,440 pesos.
- **P22:** 1,500 pesos.
- **P23:** I don't invest; it's a sign of fraud.
- **P24:** Example: «Go to the INE office on Monday the 12th with my birth certificate and proof of address».
