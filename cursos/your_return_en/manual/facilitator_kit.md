# Facilitator kit · Your Return, Your Money, Your Future

One 60-minute session per module, in person or online, for groups of 8 to 20 people. Everything comes from the module's lessons: key ideas, cases and practice. Use it after the group has worked through the module or as a review.

## Rules for every session

- No one shares personal data, account numbers or real amounts: we use made-up examples.
- We don't recommend institutions or products: we teach how to verify and compare.
- We don't give personalized tax, legal or immigration advice: we point to where to go.
- Talking about money is hard: thank every contribution and don't judge.

## Stress signs and where to refer

Watch for someone who misses sessions often, withdraws, mentions skipping food or medicine, borrowing from lenders or apps for basic expenses, threats over a debt, or not sleeping because of money. Talk privately, without pressure, and share these options:

- Línea de la Vida: 800 911 2000 (mental health and addictions, 24 hours, Spanish)
- CONDUSEF: 55 5340 0999 (banks, credit, insurance)
- 088, Guardia Nacional (fraud and online crime)
- 089 (anonymous reports, including extortion)
- 911 (emergencies and violence)

## Module 1. Your papers

**Outcome:** Your list of papers in order: repatriation record, CURP, birth certificate, INE and your children's papers.

**Materials:** phone or projector with the module book, printed pages of the support book, case cards, pens.

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Welcome and opening question: “What step did you take with your money this week?” |
| 5–15 | Key ideas of the module (below). Ask someone to explain one in their own words. |
| 15–35 | Cases in groups of 3 or 4 (below). Each group decides and explains why. |
| 35–50 | Practice: solve the exercises (below) and compare. |
| 50–57 | My commitment: each person writes one step with a date and tells their group partner. |
| 57–60 | Close: remind them of the module self-assessment and the next meeting. |

**Key ideas**

- M1 U01 · Your repatriation record and your CURP: the first two papers: keep your repatriation record and get your CURP first; they open all the other doors.
- M1 U02 · Your birth certificate and your INE: with your birth certificate and proof of address you get your INE at no cost; it's the ID that opens a bank account.
- M1 U03 · Papers for your children born in the United States: register your children in Mexico so they have a birth certificate and a CURP; school should not wait for those papers.

**Cases**

- **M1 U01 · Don Rafa's paper.** His brother tells him to throw away the record because he «is already in Mexico». *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He keeps it with his other papers.
- **M1 U02 · The Texas license.** Chayo's husband wants to open an account with his Texas driver's license. *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He applies for his INE with his birth certificate and proof of address.
- **M1 U03 · Lupita's school.** She's told there's no spot for her children without a CURP. *What does she do?* — Expected answer: She asks for enrollment while the birth registration is finished.

**Practice**

- **M1 U01.** You have your record and your CURP, but no birth certificate or INE. Which two papers are missing from the basic list? — Answer: The birth certificate and the INE.
- **M1 U02.** You have your birth certificate and your repatriation record. You live with your mom and the electric bill is in her name. What do you need to confirm before going to the office? — Answer: Whether they accept your mom's bill as proof of address.

**Key ideas**

- M2 U02 · Choose an account with no fees: ask for the basic account and ask about fees before you sign.
- M2 U03 · Getting paid, paying and disputing: turn on alerts; if you see a strange charge, block the card, get a reference number and dispute it.

**Cases**

- **M2 U01 · «More every month».** Don Rafa is told the support repeats every month. *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He plans with 2,000 pesos, one time only.
- **M2 U02 · The minimum balance.** Lupita is offered an account with a gold card and a 3,000-peso minimum balance. *What does she do?* — Expected answer: She asks for the basic account with no minimum balance.
- **M2 U03 · Memo's charge.** He sees 1,800 pesos he didn't spend. *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He blocks the card, gets a reference number and disputes it.

**Practice**

- **M2 U01.** You received 2,000 pesos. The bus costs 650 and your papers 300. How much is left? — Answer: 1,050 pesos.
- **M2 U02.** An account charges 85 pesos a month. How much do you pay in a year? — Answer: 1,020 pesos.

**Key ideas**

- M3 U01 · Your U.S. credit history doesn't count here: in Mexico you start with no history; check your report at no cost to see that no one used your name.
- M3 U02 · Start small and pay on time: a small credit, low use and full payment on time build your history.
- M3 U03 · «No credit check» loans and apps that overcharge: before borrowing, ask how much you'll pay in total; if they won't tell you or they ask for your contacts, don't sign.

**Cases**

- **M3 U01 · «You don't show up».** Don Rafa is told he has no history. *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He understands he's starting from zero and requests his report to check it.
- **M3 U02 · The 20,000 limit.** Memo is offered a 20,000 limit; he earns 9,000. *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He asks for a low limit he can pay in full.
- **M3 U03 · The 10 minutes.** An app promises Lupita 5,000 in 10 minutes with no paperwork. *What does she do?* — Expected answer: She asks for the total; since they won't say, she doesn't use it.

**Practice**

- **M3 U01.** You requested your Buró report in January. From what month can you request it again at no cost? — Answer: From January of the next year.
- **M3 U02.** Your limit is 4,500. How much is one third? — Answer: 1,500 pesos.

- M4 U02 · Your U.S. taxes: if taxes were withheld, file your return; there may be a refund for you.

**Practice**

- **M4 U01.** Your account back there charges 12 dollars a month and has 150. In how many months will it be empty? — Answer: In 13 months (12.5 rounded up).
- **M4 U02.** 2,400 dollars were withheld and what you owed in taxes was 1,700. How much would your refund be? — Answer: 700 dollars.

**Key ideas**

- M5 U02 · Your contribution weeks: your weeks aren't erased; check them and find out which law applies to you.
- M5 U03 · Save for retirement even if you're self-employed: even if you're self-employed, you can save in your Afore from 50 pesos.

**Cases**

- **M5 U01 · «It got lost».** Don Rafa thinks he lost his factory savings. *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He looks for it with his CURP in AforeMóvil.
- **M5 U02 · «He can't get it anymore».** Chayo is told her husband lost everything. *What does she do?* — Expected answer: They check his weeks at IMSS with his CURP.
- **M5 U03 · «It's not for me».** Don Rafa thinks that without a boss he can't save for retirement. *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He contributes to his Afore every two weeks at the store.

**Practice**

- **M5 U01.** Besides the balance, what two things do you check when you find your Afore? — Answer: Your contact details and your beneficiaries.
- **M5 U02.** You have 700 weeks and you're under Ley 97. How many are you missing in 2026? — Answer: 175.

**Practice**

- **M6 U01.** 9,000 comes in a month and you spend 7,200 on the basics. How much is left? — Answer: 1,800 pesos.
- **M6 U02.** You have 9,000 in savings and need 2,500 a month to cover your expenses. How much can you give away over three months without falling short? — Answer: 1,500 pesos.

**Key ideas**

- M8 U01 · Fake fixers: if they charge you for a no-cost procedure or ask for your originals, it isn't help: it's a fake fixer.
- M8 U02 · Fraud and extortion against people who return: when someone rushes you, hang up and call yourself; no serious person asks for urgent deposits by phone.
- M8 U03 · Your return plan on one page: put everything on one page with the next action and a date for each part.

**Cases**

- **M8 U01 · The vest.** A man in a vest offers to speed everything up for 1,500. *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He goes in and asks at the window.
- **M8 U02 · The detained brother.** Lupita is asked for 8,000 pesos to free her brother. *What does she do?* — Expected answer: She hangs up and calls her brother herself.
- **M8 U03 · Three notebooks.** Don Rafa has scattered notes. *What does he do?* — Expected answer: He brings everything together in his one-page plan.

**Practice**

- **M8 U01.** A fixer charges 1,500 for three no-cost procedures. How much do you save if you do them yourself? — Answer: 1,500 pesos.
- **M8 U02.** You're offered to turn 10,000 into 20,000 in three months. What do you do? — Answer: I don't invest; it's a sign of fraud.
