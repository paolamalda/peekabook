# Your Return · In-person session guide

A 3-hour kickoff session, at a reception center for repatriated people or in the participants' home municipality. The rest of the program (about 2 hours) is taken online, at each person's pace.

**Group:** up to 20 people. **Facilitator:** someone from Desarrolla Talento; ideally, someone who has also returned.
**Materials:** printed papers checklist (M1 U01), «What was left there» table (M4 U01), one-page plan (M8 U03), pens and one phone with internet per table.
**No personal data is requested:** each person fills in their own sheets and takes them home.

## Session goal

Each person leaves with their papers checklist, knows what to do with their two cards, has written down what they left in the U.S., and has taken one concrete first step: looking up their CURP, locating their Afore or activating their account.

## Agenda (180 minutes)

| Minute | Block | Supporting lessons | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–15 | Welcome | — | Introductions without judgment: «no one here asks how you came back». Group agreements. |
| 15–45 | Your papers | M1 U01, U02, U03 | Each person fills in their papers checklist. The CURP is looked up live on one phone per table. |
| 45–75 | Your cards and your account | M2 U01, U02 | The difference between Bienestar Paisano and FINABIEN Paisano. The 2,000-peso exercise. The four questions before opening an account. |
| 75–90 | Break | — | — |
| 90–120 | What was left there | M4 U01, U02, U03 | «What was left there» table: last paycheck, account, W-2 and 401(k). Who helps with each (consulate, Embassy). |
| 120–145 | Your Afore and your weeks | M5 U01, U02 | Locating the Afore with the CURP in AforeMóvil, by table. Which law applies to each person. |
| 145–165 | Don't get scammed | M8 U01, U02 | Table of no-cost procedures. Practicing the «hang up and call yourself» rule. |
| 165–180 | Your plan and what's next online | M8 U03 | Each person writes their next action in three parts of the plan. How to log in to the platform for the rest. |

## What's taken online afterward

Modules 3 (credit from scratch), 6 (first 90 days) and 7 (work or a business), plus review of the others.

## Measurement

- A short, anonymous survey at the start of the session and at the end of the program (well-being and stress index).
- Anonymous follow-up at 30 and 90 days: do they have an account in their name? did they check their credit report? did they locate their Afore? did they recover anything they left there?
- Results are reported only by group, with 5 or more people.

## Care for the facilitator

- Many people arrive with grief, fear or anger. Don't ask why they returned.
- No immigration or legal advice is given: refer people to the consulate, the Embassy or the relevant public office.
- Don't recommend banks or institutions: teach people to verify them in CONDUSEF's SIPRES.
- If someone describes an extortion in progress, guide them to hang up, call their relative and report it to 089.
- Mexican offices serve in Spanish: if someone isn't comfortable in Spanish, help them prepare their questions in writing, in Spanish, before going.
