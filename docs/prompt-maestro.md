# Peekabook: prompt maestro

Todo lo que has dado (la idea de la app, las opciones de personajes, tus 17 imágenes de referencia, tus comentarios, las mascotas, las escenas y las zonas de cielo, agua y espacio) en un solo lugar.

**Cómo usarlo:**
1. Pega el **Prompt maestro** una vez al inicio de la conversación con tu herramienta de imágenes (ChatGPT, Gemini, Ideogram, etc.) o mándaselo a tu ilustrador(a).
2. Después pide cada imagen con una de las **plantillas** de abajo, llenando lo que va entre `{ }`.
3. En Midjourney, que acepta prompts cortos, usa la **versión corta** y sube 2 o 3 de tus referencias como imagen de estilo.

Los prompts están en inglés porque las herramientas de imágenes dan mejores resultados así.

---

## Prompt maestro

```
PROJECT: "Peekabook", a seek-and-find picture-book app for kids (like Where's Waldo).
Kids look for their real friends, family members and pets, drawn as cute chibi
characters hidden in busy illustrated scenes. Every character is built from swappable
layers so families can customize them. Remember everything below for all the images
I ask for.

ART STYLE (for everything):
- Super-cute chibi kawaii style for a children's app, clean vector look.
- The head is about HALF of the total height: big round soft head with small ears,
  tiny rounded body, short stubby arms and legs.
- HUGE glossy eyes set low on the face: thick dark upper lash line with a small wing
  at the outer corner, iris darker at the top and lighter at the bottom, a big white
  highlight at the top and a small one at the bottom. Friendly, never angry.
- Tiny mouth (small smile, ":3", or open "D" smile with a pink tongue), tiny nose dot,
  round pink blush with 3 tiny diagonal lines.
- BIG voluminous hair, much wider than the head, made of large soft locks with
  pointed tips that flick outward; a few thin strand lines inside the hair; one
  lighter shine band across the top of the head shaped like a ring with a jagged
  lower edge; a small curly hair sprout on top. Bangs end ABOVE the eyes.
- Smooth dark-brown outline (#4A3228), a bit thicker on the outer silhouette.
- Flat colors with ONE soft shadow tone. No gradients (except optional ombré hair
  that fades to a lighter color at the tips), no texture.
- Warm, soft, slightly muted pastel palette.
- Natural, relaxed, lively poses: weight on one leg, head slightly tilted, arms doing
  something (waving with an open hand, hand on the hip, holding something). Never
  stiff, never symmetrical, never a T-pose.
- Hands always visible: round and cute, with a thumb and short fingers.
- Shoes big, chunky and clearly visible, with soles and details, feet turned a
  little outward.
- Close-ups (character creator, "found you!" screen, medals, stories) may add soft
  gradients, glossy hair highlights and a few eyelashes, keeping the same shapes.

CHARACTER OPTIONS (each one is a separate layer, or a color the app tints):
- Body type: slim, regular, round.
- Size: mini (baby, ~1.8 heads tall), kid (~2.1), mid (~2.4, teens and grandparents),
  adult (~2.8), tall (~3.1). The head is the SAME size on every body; only the body
  below the head changes.
- Skin: any color. Freckles: optional.
- Eyes: any color. Glasses: none, round, square, sunglasses, with a frame color.
- Hair: any color (optional ombré tip color); type straight, wavy, curly, coily;
  length bald, balding, buzz, short, chin, shoulder, long, very long; style loose,
  ponytail, pigtails, bun, braids; bangs fringe, side-swept, middle part, none.
- Beard: none, stubble, short, full, goatee. Mustache: none, classic, handlebar,
  thin, walrus. Both use the hair color and are separate layers.
- Tops: t-shirt, long sleeve, tank top, button shirt, hoodie, sweater, open jacket,
  lab coat, suit jacket with shirt and tie, dress. Any color.
- Bottoms: jeans, pants, suit pants, shorts, skirt, leggings. Any color.
- Shoes: sneakers, boots, sandals, flats, dress shoes, barefoot. Any color.
- Accessories: cap, beanie, sun hat, crown, bow, headband, superhero cape, earrings,
  scarf, binky (pacifier), backpack, purse, necklace, bracelet. Any color.
- Seasonal looks and costumes kids earn with medals (Halloween, Christmas,
  astronaut, princess, superhero...). Generic designs, never famous characters.

LAYERS (bottom to top): gear back, cape, backpack, hood back, hair back (+ ponytail,
pigtails, bun), legs, bottoms, shoes, arms, sleeves, torso, top, hands + bracelet,
scarf / necklace / straps / purse, braids, head + ears, face (eyes, mouth, blush) +
freckles, iris, beard, mustache, binky, hood front, hair front (bangs, side locks,
sprout), glasses, earrings, hat, item in hand, gear front.
- Layers the app colors are drawn in flat light grey so they can be tinted.
- Hair, hoods, capes, backpacks, boats and balloon baskets are split into a back part
  and a front part.
- The outline and the colors are kept separate (the outline alone becomes a
  coloring page).
- Every layer uses the same canvas, the same pose and the same anchor points.
- Poses: standing with arms slightly out (main dress-up pose), waving, walking,
  sitting, kneeling, peeking (only head and hands), plus floating, swimming and
  sitting in a vehicle for sky, water and space.

PETS: same style and outline. Oversized round head, tiny chubby body, cream belly and
muzzle, big glossy eyes with highlights, blush. One shared body template with two
poses: SITTING and STANDING ON FOUR LEGS. Swappable ears, tail, markings (spots,
patches, stripes, tuxedo, socks, eye mask), collar / bandana / bow / little sweater /
harness, and gear (life vest, swim ring, astronaut helmet, balloon). Start with dog,
cat, bunny and hamster. Fur colors are tinted by the app.

SCENES: wide, busy, cheerful seek-and-find spreads (16:9, high 3/4 bird's-eye view),
with NO people and NO animals (they are added later), lots of small details, clear
open areas all over the image, slightly muted colors and thinner outlines than the
characters so hidden friends can be found. Examples: park, beach, school, zoo, farm,
winter village, birthday party, supermarket, airport, museum, outer space,
underwater city, cloud kingdom, hot-air balloon festival, pirate ships, city
rooftops. Each scene also has a foreground layer (bushes, fences, tables, railings)
that characters can hide behind.

WHERE CHARACTERS CAN GO (a placement map for each scene: same size, flat colors only):
- green = ground (standing)          - blue = seats (sitting)
- yellow = peeking spots
- magenta = sky or space: only with flying gear (balloons, kite, umbrella, broom,
  cape, small plane, hot-air balloon, rocket, jetpack, astronaut suit)
- cyan = water surface: only with swim ring, boat or surfboard
- navy = underwater: only with snorkel or submarine
- red = never (walls, rooftops, tree tops, roads, fire, cliffs, and the top and bottom
  strips where the app's buttons go)
- black = everything else (never)
Each scene decides which zones are open and can give gear to everyone (for example,
in space everyone wears an astronaut suit).

AVOID: realistic, 3D, photo, painterly, sketch, texture, stiff pose, T-pose,
symmetrical pose, arms straight down, hidden hands, stump hands, tiny feet, flat hair,
helmet hair, dashed lines in the hair, dull eyes without highlights, bangs covering the
eyes, angry look, text, watermark, logo, famous or copyrighted characters, scary,
adult proportions.
```

## Plantillas (después del prompt maestro)

### 1. Un personaje completo (para ver cómo queda)
```
Using the Peekabook style, draw ONE full-body character: {size}, {body type} body,
{skin} skin{, freckles}, {eye color} eyes{, glasses}, {hair color} {hair type}
{hair length} hair in {style} with {bangs}{, beard}{, mustache}, wearing a
{top color} {top}, {bottom color} {bottom} and {shoe color} {shoes}{, accessories}.
Pose: {waving / hand on hip / sitting / walking / peeking}. Expression: {smile /
big smile / :3 / happy closed eyes}. Transparent background, centered, feet on the
bottom line, no ground shadow, no sticker border.
```

### 2. Una capa suelta
```
Using the Peekabook style and the attached base body, draw ONLY the {LAYER} for the
{size} {body type} base body in the {pose} pose. Flat light grey with one darker grey
shadow tone so the app can tint it, dark-brown outline, transparent background,
perfectly aligned to the base body. {If it has a back and a front part, output TWO
images: BACK and FRONT.}
```

### 3. Una mascota
```
Using the Peekabook pet style, draw a cute chibi {dog / cat / bunny / hamster},
{sitting / standing on four legs}, 3/4 front view, {fur color} with {markings},
{ears}, {tail}{, collar / bandana / bow}. Transparent background, no ground shadow.
```

### 4. Una escena
```
Using the Peekabook scene style, draw a wide 16:9 seek-and-find scene of {LOCATION},
NO people and NO animals, lots of details, open ground areas all over, {open sky /
water / space areas with only a few clouds, waves or stars}, perspective with far
areas small at the top and near areas large at the bottom, no text.
```

### 5. El mapa de zonas de esa escena
```
Using the exact same scene and composition, make a flat placement map with only solid
colors and no details: green = ground, blue = seats, yellow = peeking spots,
magenta = sky/space, cyan = water surface, navy = underwater, red = forbidden (walls,
rooftops, tree tops, roads, fire, cliffs, top 10% and bottom 8% of the image),
black = everything else. Same size, perfectly aligned.
```
Los mapas que hace la IA salen imprecisos: corrígelos a mano.

## Versión corta (Midjourney y otros con prompts cortos)
```
super-cute chibi kawaii character for a kids app, head half of body height, huge
glossy eyes with big white highlights, pink blush with tiny lines, big voluminous
hair with pointed flicked locks, shine band and a curly hair sprout, dark-brown
outline, flat colors with one soft shadow, warm muted pastels, natural relaxed pose,
visible cute hands, chunky shoes, full body, transparent background
--no realistic, 3d, stiff pose, text, watermark
```
Agrega al final lo que quieras del personaje (pelo, ropa, pose) y sube 2 o 3 de tus imágenes de referencia como referencia de estilo (`--sref`).

Detalles de cada capa, orden, rigs, reglas de colocación y el archivo de datos por escena: [`art-prompts.md`](art-prompts.md).
