Script to ask for information

"I live in California and I need help with [topic]. Do you handle this kind of case in Spanish? What credential does the person who will help me have? What's the total cost and what doesn't it include? What documents do I need and how do I send them securely? Is there a free or low-cost option?"

Frequently asked questions

Can I study without an SSN or ITIN?

Yes. We use a program ID and made-up cases. Whether you can use a financial product is a different question.

Do I have to open an account to pass?

No. We assess how you compare and decide; waiting can be the right decision.

What if I can't save?

Identify your gap, your priorities and the support available. We don't require an amount or blame everything on your habits.

Should I stop sending remittances?

No. The goal is to fit together your family support, your ability to pay and your future.

Does this course give me immigration advice?

No. Only an attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative can give it. We help you find reliable help.

When will my score improve?

Nobody can promise a date or a number of points. We work with correct information, sustainable payments and follow-up.

Can I use made-up information?

Yes. We assess your reasoning; you don't need to reveal your identity, your health or your finances.

What do I do if a source changed?

Mark it, check the current version and don't use an expired fee as if it were current.

Is the certificate officially accredited?

It's the program's own certificate, verifiable online. It isn't a license or an official accreditation.

Do I have to leave a review?

No. Your feedback and follow-up are voluntary and don't affect your access or your certificate.

Can I ask for a video call?

We offer question sessions according to the published availability. Cases that need a professional are referred.

How do I send a question?

Include the module, the situation without sensitive information, what you tried and your specific question. If there's a fraud in progress or a medical emergency, first use the appropriate emergency channel.
