# H5P Activity Scripts

This document says what to build in H5P for each lesson: which content type, which items to load, the correct answer and the feedback to show. All names, amounts and messages are examples.

## How to use this document

1. In the Moodle course, add an **H5P** activity in the lesson's section.
2. Choose the **content type** indicated (install it from the H5P Hub the first time).
3. Copy the items from the script. Check spelling in the preview.
4. Set **Activity completion**: "View" for ungraded activities and "Receive a passing grade" (70%) for graded ones.
5. Level Up awards points when completion is met (see the Level Up and badges guide).

**About simulators and calculators.** H5P has no content type that calculates with the numbers each person types. For those lessons, the script proposes a version that works in H5P today (scenarios with precalculated results) and leaves the calculator for the companion app. The precalculated results in this document were checked with formulas.

**Rules for every activity:**

- Never ask for SSN, ITIN, immigration status, passwords or real amounts.
- Short, blame-free feedback: explain why and what to do.
- Plain language: short sentences, one idea per screen.
- Course characters: Alex and Mar.

# Module 1. Understand your money and organize your finances

## M1 U01. Drag and sort

**Type:** Drag and Drop. **Graded:** yes (70%).

**Instruction:** "Drag each transaction to its column."

**Zones:** Earned income · Loan · Transfer between my accounts · Refund.

| Item | Correct zone | Feedback if wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly wages: 600 | Earned income | It is payment for your work. |
| Advance from the employer: 400 | Loan | It will be deducted later; it is not new income. |
| Tamale sales, profit 350 | Earned income | Profit is what remains after ingredients. |
| Loan from a friend: 200 | Loan | It must be paid back. |
| I move 100 from savings to checking | Transfer between my accounts | It only changed places. |
| I withdraw 80 at the ATM | Transfer between my accounts | It went from your account to your pocket. |
| 40 refund for returning shoes | Refund | It reverses a purchase; it is not income. |
| Tips: 90 | Earned income | They are part of your pay for working. |
| Paycheck advance from an app: 150 | Loan | It comes out of your next paycheck and sometimes has a cost. |
| Charge corrected by the bank: 12 | Refund | The bank returned a charge. |

## M1 U02. Interest simulator

**Type:** Course Presentation (5 slides). **Graded:** yes.

1. **Slide 1:** "You save 1,000 at 10% a year for 5 years. Which type of interest gives you more?"
2. **Slide 2:** precalculated table.

| Year | Simple interest | Compound interest |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1,100.00 | 1,100.00 |
| 3 | 1,300.00 | 1,331.00 |
| 5 | 1,500.00 | 1,610.51 |

3. **Slide 3:** single-choice question: "Why does compound interest give 110.51 more?"
   - Correct answer: "Because interest also earns interest."
4. **Slide 4:** same calculation with credit card debt. "If it is a debt instead of savings, compounding works against you."
5. **Slide 5:** question: "Your savings grow 3% and prices grow 5%. Can you buy more or less?"
   - Correct answer: "Less."
   - Feedback: "1,030 ÷ 1.05 = 980.95 in last year's money."

**For the app:** calculator with free rate, term and interest type.

## M1 U03. Choose your path

**Type:** Branching Scenario. **Graded:** no (completion on finish).

- **Start:** Alex sees a 900 phone "today only," with payments of 25 a week. Rent is due in 10 days.
- **Option A: "I'll buy it now".**
  - Scene: in week 3 a doctor's bill arrives and Alex falls behind on rent with a 50 late fee.
  - Ending: "The rush cost 50 and stress. The offer came back the next month."
- **Option B: "I'll wait 48 hours and compare".**
  - Scene: Alex finds the same model refurbished for 450 cash.
  - Ending: "Waiting revealed a third option."
- **Option C: "I won't buy it this month".**
  - Scene: Alex pays rent and sets aside 25 a week.
  - Ending: "In 5 weeks Alex has 125 for a down payment with no debt."
- **Closing message:** "No option is 'bad'. The 48-hour rule gives you time to see the cost of each one."

## M1 U04. Payday calendar

**Type:** Drag the Words over a yearly calendar. **Graded:** yes.

- **Data:** paid every two weeks, on Fridays. First payday: January 8, 2027.
- **Task:** drag "3 paychecks" to the months that get three paychecks.
- **Answer:** April (2, 16 and 30) and October (1, 15 and 29). There are 26 paychecks in the year.
- **Feedback:** "Three-paycheck months are a good time to boost your emergency fund or pay down debt. Do not count on them for fixed expenses."

## M1 U11. The ITIN path

**Type:** Sort the Paragraphs. **Graded:** yes.

**Correct order:**

1. Confirm you need an ITIN and cannot get an SSN.
2. Gather identity and foreign status documents (a passport alone is enough).
3. Fill out Form W-7, usually together with your tax return.
4. Choose how to submit it: by mail, with an acceptance agent or at an IRS office by appointment.
5. Keep a copy and wait for the IRS letter.
6. Use your ITIN only for taxes and services that accept it.

**Final feedback:** links to the IRS ITIN page and the acceptance agent locator.

## M1 U12. Would you hire this preparer?

**Type:** Branching Scenario. **Graded:** no.

- **Scene 1:** ad: "I guarantee the biggest refund. I charge 20% of what you get back."
  - Options: Hire / Ask more / Look for someone else.
  - Red flag: percentage fee and guarantee.
- **Scene 2:** another preparer wants the refund sent to *their* account. Red flag.
- **Scene 3:** a preparer who signs the return, has a PTIN and CTEC registration, and gives you a copy. Sign of trust.
- **Ending:** checklist.
  - Has a PTIN.
  - Is registered with CTEC or is a CPA, attorney or enrolled agent.
  - Signs the return.
  - The refund goes to your account.
  - The free VITA option also exists.

## M1 U13. Myth or fact?

**Type:** Dialog Cards (10 cards). **Graded:** no.

> **Mandatory review:** counsel must approve these cards before publishing, because the public charge rule can change.

**Front → Back:**

1. "If my citizen children get Medi-Cal, it affects me." → Check against a current source and counsel. Generally, benefits received by other family members are not counted against you.
2. "Public charge applies to every application." → No. Many applications and categories are exempt.
3. "Getting help from a food bank counts." → Generally, no.

Complete the remaining 7 cards with counsel, based on the Protecting Immigrant Families page (link in "Learn more").

## M2 U07. Capacity calculator

**Type:** Course Presentation. **Graded:** yes.

- **Data:** balance today (Tuesday) 620. Before Friday you must pay phone 60 and food 120. On Friday you get paid 700. Rent of 900 is due the following Monday.
- **Today:** 620 − 60 − 120 = 440, minus a minimum cushion of 100. You could send up to **340**, but on Monday you would have 100 + 700 = 800 for a rent of 900.
- **Question:** "When is it better to send?"
- **Answer:** Friday, after payday. 440 + 700 − 900 − 100 cushion = **140** available safely.
- **Feedback:** "Sending 340 today would leave rent to be covered by Friday's paycheck. If anything goes wrong, it will not be enough."

## M2 U09. Receipt detective

## M2 U10. Remittance debt simulator

**Type:** Course Presentation. **Graded:** no.

**Example:** you borrow 300 with a paycheck advance that costs 15. 315 is deducted from your next 700 paycheck.

| Week | Without the loan | With the loan |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send 150 now and 150 on Friday | Send 300 now |
| 2 | Paycheck 700 | Paycheck 700 − 315 = 385 |
| 3 | Rent covered | 115 short for rent: the loan repeats |

## M2 U11. Real or fake?

**Type:** Branching Scenario. **Graded:** no.

- **Message 1:** WhatsApp from a new number: "It's your cousin, I changed my number, send me 500 urgently."
  - Best response: call the old number or ask a question only he would know.
- **Message 2:** phone call: "Your son had an accident, deposit to this account."
  - Best response: hang up and call your family directly.
- **Message 3:** your mother, from her usual number, asks for the usual amount. It is real.
- **Closing:** "Urgency and a new number are warning signs. Agree on a family code word."

## M2 U13. Build the route

# Module 3. Build your credit and manage your debts

## M3 U01. Can I afford it?

**Type:** Course Presentation. **Graded:** yes.

- **Example:** free monthly margin of 350. Loan payment: 180.

| Income drop | Margin | Does the payment fit? |
|---|---|---|
| 0% (income 2,400) | 350 | Yes, 170 left over |
| 10% (−240) | 110 | No |
| 20% (−480) | −130 | No |

- **Message:** "Lenders lend based on your good month. You decide based on your hard month."

## M3 U02. Sample report

**Type:** Find Multiple Hotspots plus questions. **Graded:** yes.

| Item that needs action | Correct path |
|---|---|
| Address you do not recognize | Dispute with the credit bureau |
| Account you did not open | Identity theft: IdentityTheft.gov and freeze your credit |
| Payment marked late that you paid on time | Dispute with evidence |
| Collection debt from 8 years ago | Check the date; do not acknowledge it without reviewing |

## M3 U03. Choose your first step

**Type:** Branching Scenario. **Graded:** no.

- **Question 1:** "Do you have an SSN or ITIN?"
  - Yes: question 2.
  - No: lending circles or designated credit unions; ask about options without a number.
- **Question 2:** "Do you have 200 in savings you can leave as a deposit?"
  - Yes: secured card.
  - No: 0% lending circle or credit builder loan.
- **Question 3:** "Do you have a credit history in Mexico?" If yes, show that some issuers accept it through services like Nova Credit.
- **Ending:** "Check the course comparison matrix."

## M3 U04. Legitimate or scam?

**Type:** Flashcards (8 cards). **Graded:** yes.

| Ad | Answer |
|---|---|
| "We erase legitimate debts from your report." | Scam |
| "Pay before we do the work." | Scam (the law prohibits it) |
| "We give you a new number to start over." | Scam and a crime |
| "You can dispute errors yourself for free." | Legitimate |
| "Nonprofit credit counseling, free session." | Legitimate (verify) |
| "Don't talk to the bureaus, we'll do it." | Red flag |
| "Check your report free at AnnualCreditReport.com." | Legitimate |
| "We guarantee 100 more points in 30 days." | Scam |

## M3 U05. Amortization table

**Type:** Course Presentation. **Graded:** yes.

**Sample loan:** 3,000 at 24% a year.

| Term | Monthly payment | Total interest |
|---|---|---|
| 12 months | 283.68 | 404.15 |
| 24 months | 158.61 | 806.72 |
| 36 months | 117.70 | 1,237.15 |

- **Question:** "What happens to total interest if you extend the term?"
- **Answer:** it goes up. At 36 months you pay three times the interest you would pay at 12.

## M3 U06. Avalanche vs. snowball

**Type:** Course Presentation. **Graded:** yes.

**Sample debts:** Card A 2,500 at 27% (minimum 75) · Card B 600 at 18% (minimum 25) · Loan 1,500 at 12% (minimum 50). Budget: 300 a month.

| Strategy | Order | Months | Total interest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Avalanche (highest rate first) | A, B, Loan | 18 | 715.28 |
| Snowball (smallest balance first) | B, Loan, A | 19 | 911.71 |

**Feedback:** "The avalanche saves 196.43. The snowball gives quick wins. The best one is the one you can stick with."

## M3 U07. Call with a debt collector

**Type:** Branching Scenario. **Graded:** no.

- **Scene 1:** "If you don't pay today we'll report you to immigration."
  - Correct response: "That is not legal. Please send me the debt information in writing."
  - Add a note: threats like this can be reported to the CFPB.
- **Scene 2:** "Give me your bank account number so we can collect."
  - Correct response: do not give it; pay only through a verified method.
- **Scene 3:** "Can you pay 50 a month?"
  - Correct response: accept only what fits and ask for the agreement in writing.

## M3 U08. Do I sign or not?

**Type:** Branching Scenario. **Graded:** no.

- **Case 1:** your brother asks you to cosign a car loan. Consequence: if he does not pay, the debt and the late payments are yours.
- **Case 2:** your cousin wants to add you as an authorized user on her card. Consequence: her history can help or hurt you; it does not oblige you to pay.
- **Case 3:** a friend wants a loan in your name. Consequence: it is your debt.
- **Closing:** "Help without signing: you can help with money you don't need back."

## M3 U09. Tanda simulator

**Type:** Course Presentation. **Graded:** no.

**Sample tanda:** 10 people, 100 a month, each turn receives 1,000.

| Your turn | You pay in before receiving | You pay in after |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100 | 900 (works like a 0% loan) |
| 5 | 500 | 500 |
| 10 | 1,000 | 0 (works like savings) |

- **Risk slide:** if someone stops paying in month 6, turns 7 to 10 receive less.
- **Closing:** "An informal tanda does not build credit. Lending circles that report to the bureaus do."

# Module 4. Protect your money, your identity and your family

## M4 U01. Scam or not?

**Type:** Single Choice Set (12 questions with the options "Scam" and "Legitimate"). **Graded:** yes.

| Message | Answer | Sign |
|---|---|---|
| "Your account will be locked, confirm your password here." | Scam | Asks for a password |
| "You won a green card, pay a 200 processing fee." | Scam | Nobody sells the visa lottery |
| "IRS: pay today with gift cards." | Scam | The IRS does not collect with gift cards |
| Your bank alerts you to a purchase and asks you to call the number on your card. | Legitimate | You call the official number yourself |
| "Work from home, receive checks and forward the money." | Scam | Money mule |
| "Your package was not delivered, pay 1.99." | Scam | Strange link |
| Appointment reminder from your clinic with no links or payments. | Legitimate | Asks for no data |
| "I'm an immigration lawyer, I guarantee your case via WhatsApp." | Scam | Nobody can guarantee |
| "Zelle: I sent you too much by mistake, send it back." | Scam | Fake payment |
| DMV email with the appointment number you booked. | Legitimate | Matches your own request |
| "Crypto investment with a guaranteed 20% a month." | Scam | Guaranteed return |
| "The consulate is calling: pay a fine today." | Scam | Pressure and immediate payment |

## M4 U02. Can they help me?

**Type:** Dialog Cards. **Graded:** no.

- **Notary public (U.S.):** can certify signatures. Cannot give immigration advice.
- **Immigration consultant:** can fill out forms with your answers, with a bond and registration in California. Cannot give legal advice.
- **Attorney:** can advise and represent you. Verify with the State Bar.
- **Accredited representative (DOJ):** can advise and represent you if they work at a recognized organization. Verify on the EOIR roster.
- **Social media "gestor":** none of the above; it is a red flag.

## M4 U03. Security check

**Type:** Documentation Tool with checkboxes. **Graded:** no.

**Checkboxes:**

- Two-step verification on email and bank
- Different passwords
- Transaction alerts
- Screen lock
- Updates installed
- Credit frozen
- Family code word

**Output:** the first three controls the person did not check, in list order.

## M4 U04. Put the response in order

**Type:** Sort the Paragraphs (3 exercises). **Graded:** yes.

- **Incident 1: I gave away my bank password.**
  1. Change the password.
  2. Call the bank at the official number.
  3. Review transactions.
  4. Turn on alerts.
- **Incident 2: a charge I don't recognize.**
  1. Lock the card in the app.
  2. Call the bank.
  3. Dispute the charge.
  4. Save the report number.
- **Incident 3: someone opened an account in my name.**
  1. IdentityTheft.gov.
  2. Freeze credit at all three bureaus.
  3. Notify the company.
  4. Review your reports.

## M4 U05. Agreement or control?

**Type:** Single Choice Set. **Graded:** no. Do not show a score; this is sensitive.

| Situation | Answer |
|---|---|
| "We decide together how much to send to our families." | Agreement |
| "My partner keeps my passport so I don't lose it." | Control |
| "We review the budget every Sunday." | Agreement |
| "I'm not allowed to have my own account." | Control |
