# Calculation practice

Solve each practice showing the method, the result, the period and the limits of the calculation. The answers are at the end.

| Code | Lesson | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| P01 | M1 U02 | Calculate 7% of 600 and explain the decimal conversion. |
| P02 | M1 U02 | A rate goes from 2% to 3%. Tell apart percentage points and relative increase. |
| P03 | M1 U02 | Principal 1,500, simple annual interest 8%, nine months, no fees. Calculate interest and balance. |
| P04 | M1 U02 | Principal 1,000 at 5% a year, compounded yearly, three years, no other activity. Calculate the balance. |
| P05 | M1 U02 | A 12% nominal annual rate compounded monthly. Calculate the monthly rate and the effective annual rate. |
| P06 | M1 U02 | A price index goes from 250 to 265. Calculate inflation for the period. |
| P07 | M1 U02 | Inflation drops from 6% to 4%. Did prices go back to their earlier level? |
| P08 | M1 U09 | Made-up schedule: first 1,000 at 10%, next 500 at 20%. Tax and effective rate on 1,500. |
| P09 | M2 U08 | 3,600 pesos are needed. Exchange rate 18 and fee 5, no other charges. How much do you pay? |
| P10 | M3 U01 | Debt payments 600 and gross income 3,000 a month. Calculate the ratio and explain what it does not prove. |

| P12 | M4 U06 | Reserve 900 and basic expenses 1,800 a month. How much does it cover? |
| P13 | M5 U02 | Principal 1,000, ending value 1,050 and a 30 cash payment not reinvested. Gross total return. |
| P14 | M5 U02 | Same case, with fees of 20 and an exercise tax of 10. Net return. |
| P15 | M5 U02 | Net return of 5% and inflation of 4%. Exact real return. |

| P18 | M5 U02 | The balance goes from 2,000 to 2,300 with a 250 contribution. Gain and missing information for the rate. |
| P19 | M5 U02 | 1,000 grows to 1,440 in two years with no activity. Cumulative rate and compounded annual rate. |
| P20 | M5 U06 | You contribute 40 a month for 10 years with no return. Total contributed; is it compound interest? |
| P21 | M3 U09 | A tanda of 10 people, 80 a week, turn 4. How much do you receive and how much did you contribute before receiving? |
| P22 | M5 U10 | You sell 900 a month, spend 380 on supplies and work 26 hours. Profit and profit per hour. |

## Answers

- **P01:** 42. 7% = 0.07; 600 × 0.07 = 42.
- **P02:** it rose 1 percentage point; the relative increase is 50%.
- **P03:** interest 90; balance 1,590 (1,500 × 0.08 × 9/12).
- **P04:** 1,157.63 (1,000 × 1.05³). With simple interest it would be 1,150.
- **P05:** 1% a month; 12.68% effective annual rate.
- **P06:** 6% ((265 − 250) ÷ 250).
- **P07:** no. Prices keep rising, but more slowly.
- **P08:** tax 200; effective rate 13.33%; marginal rate on the last bracket 20%.
- **P09:** 200 transfer plus 5 fee = 205 dollars.
- **P10:** 20%. It does not prove you can pay after your basic expenses or that you will be approved.
- **P11:** interest 16; principal paid 84; balance 716.
- **P12:** 0.5 months. Do not include locked-up money.
- **P13:** gain 80; return 8% (3% from the payment and 5% from the price change).
- **P14:** net gain 50; net return 5%.
- **P15:** 1.05 ÷ 1.04 − 1 = 0.96%.
- **P16:** 990; a 1% loss even though the average is zero.
- **P17:** −5%. Receiving cash did not prevent the loss.
- **P18:** gain 50. You need to know when the contribution was made to calculate a comparable rate.
- **P19:** 44% cumulative and 20% compounded annually (1.2 × 1.2 = 1.44).
- **P20:** 4,800 contributed. There is no interest: contributions are not returns.
- **P21:** you receive 800; before receiving you contributed 240 (3 weeks × 80), and in total you will contribute 800.
- **P22:** profit 520; 20 an hour before taxes.
