# Glossary

Plain-language definitions. A contract or a law can use a word with a more specific meaning. The Spanish term you may find in documents or conversations appears in parentheses.

## Money and budgeting

- **Asset** (*activo*): something of value you own.
- **Liability** (*pasivo*): a debt or pending obligation.
- **Net worth** (*patrimonio neto*): assets minus liabilities. It is not the same as available cash.
- **Gross income** (*ingreso bruto*): what you earn before deductions.
- **Net income** (*ingreso neto*): what you receive after deductions.
- **Cash flow** (*flujo de efectivo*): money in and out over time.
- **Liquidity** (*liquidez*): how quickly you can use a resource without losing value.
- **Available balance** (*saldo disponible*): what the bank lets you use today; it may not include future payments.
- **Budget** (*presupuesto*): a plan for where your money will go.
- **Small recurring expense** (*gasto hormiga*): a small expense that repeats and adds up; not necessarily bad.
- **Periodic expense** (*gasto periódico*): a payment that repeats but is not monthly.
- **Sinking fund** (*reserva para gastos previstos*): money set aside for a known payment.
- **Emergency fund** (*fondo de emergencia*): accessible money for the unexpected.
- **Opportunity cost** (*costo de oportunidad*): what you give up when you choose.
- **Priorities** (*prioridades*): what you decide to take care of first.
- **Biweekly** (*cada dos semanas*): every 14 days; 26 payments a year.
- **Semimonthly** (*dos veces al mes*): 24 payments a year.
- **Scenarios** (*escenarios*): possible versions of the future to test your plan.
- **Double counting** (*doble conteo*): using the same money for two things.

## Percentages, interest and inflation

- **Percentage** (*porcentaje*): part of each hundred; 4% = 0.04.
- **Percentage point** (*punto porcentual*): the direct difference between percentages; from 4% to 6% is 2 points.
- **Principal** (*capital*): the base amount of a loan or investment.
- **Interest** (*interés*): the cost of borrowing money or the earnings on certain balances.
- **Simple interest** (*interés simple*): always calculated on the original principal.
- **Compound interest** (*interés compuesto*): also calculated on earlier interest.
- **Effective rate** (*tasa efectiva*): the real change over a period including compounding.
- **APR** (*tasa de porcentaje anual*): the yearly cost of credit under official rules.
- **APY** (*rendimiento porcentual anual*): the yearly return on savings with compounding.
- **Fixed rate** (*tasa fija*): does not change during the agreed term.
- **Variable rate** (*tasa variable*): can change according to the contract.
- **Inflation** (*inflación*): a general rise in prices.
- **Disinflation** (*desinflación*): prices rise more slowly.
- **Deflation** (*deflación*): prices fall.
- **Purchasing power** (*poder de compra*): what your money can buy.
- **Exchange rate** (*tipo de cambio*): the price of one currency in another; always state the direction.

## Banks, payments and remittances

- **Central bank** (*banco central*): a public institution; it does not handle personal accounts.
- **Credit union** (*cooperativa de ahorro y crédito*): a financial institution owned by its members.
- **Fintech:** a company that uses technology for financial services; it does not prove it is a bank.
- **Neobank** (*neobanco*): a mainly digital service; check the bank behind it.
- **Responsible entity** (*entidad responsable*): the company responsible for the service.
- **Deposit insurance** (*seguro de depósitos*): protects eligible deposits if the institution fails (FDIC or NCUA).
- **Checking account** (*cuenta de cheques*): for frequent use.
- **Savings account** (*cuenta de ahorro*): for keeping money.
- **Fee** (*comisión*): a charge for a service.
- **Maintenance fee** (*comisión de mantenimiento*): monthly charge for having the account.
- **Bank On:** national standard for low-cost accounts.
- **Overdraft** (*sobregiro*): you spend more than you have and the bank covers it for a fee.
- **ACH:** electronic transfer network between banks.
- **Dispute** (*disputa*): a request to review an incorrect charge or item.
- **Remittance** (*remesa*): money sent to another person or country.
- **Remittance receipt** (*recibo de remesa*): proof of the transfer.
- **Reference number** (*folio*): tracking number.
- **Exchange-rate markup** (*margen cambiario*): the difference between the reference rate and the offered rate.
- **Quote** (*cotización*): terms offered for a given moment and amount.
- **DFPI:** California's financial regulator; it licenses remittance companies.
- **NMLS:** national registry of financial company licenses.

## ID and taxes

- **SSN** (*número de Seguro Social*): Social Security number.
- **ITIN** (*número de identificación personal del contribuyente*): IRS tax number for people who cannot get an SSN; it does not authorize work.
- **Consular ID** (*matrícula consular*): identification from the Mexican consulate.
- **AB 60 license** (*licencia AB 60*): California driver's license for people who cannot prove lawful presence; not a REAL ID.
- **Tax return** (*declaración de impuestos*): reports your information and calculates your tax.
- **Refund** (*reembolso*): money returned if you paid too much.
- **Withholding** (*retención*): tax prepayment taken from your pay.
- **Deduction** (*deducción*): lowers the base of the tax.
- **Tax credit** (*crédito fiscal*): lowers the tax; some are refundable.
- **CalEITC:** California's earned income tax credit; it can be claimed with an ITIN.
- **VITA:** free IRS help to prepare taxes.
- **CTEC:** California's registry of tax preparers.
- **Estimated payments** (*pagos estimados*): tax prepayments during the year.
- **Marginal rate** (*tasa marginal*): the rate on the last bracket of income.
- **Effective tax rate** (*tasa efectiva de impuesto*): tax divided by income.
- **Public charge** (*carga pública*): an assessment in certain immigration processes about dependence on government support.

## Credit and debt

- **Credit history** (*historial crediticio*): record of your credit experiences.
- **Credit report** (*reporte de crédito*): a document with your accounts and payments.
- **Score** (*puntaje*): a number calculated with a model.
- **Affordability** (*capacidad de pago*): being able to pay with your resources.
- **Utilization** (*utilización*): balance divided by limit.
- **Secured card** (*tarjeta garantizada*): backed by a deposit.
- **Credit builder loan** (*préstamo constructor de crédito*): you make payments and receive the money at the end.
- **Tanda:** rotating savings among people.
- **Lending circle** (*círculo de préstamo*): an organized social loan that can report to the bureaus.
- **Co-signer** (*cofirmante o aval*): answers for another person's debt.
- **Authorized user** (*usuario autorizado*): uses someone else's card.
- **Minimum payment** (*pago mínimo*): the least required; it does not pay off the debt.
- **Grace period** (*periodo de gracia*): time to avoid interest if you pay in full.
- **Statement closing date** (*fecha de corte*): end of the statement period.
- **Amortization** (*amortización*): how principal goes down with payments.
- **Delinquency** (*mora o atraso*): not paying on time.
- **Debt collection** (*cobranza*): efforts to collect a debt.
- **Consolidation** (*consolidación*): combining debts into one.
- **Refinancing** (*refinanciamiento*): replacing a debt with another under new terms.
- **Debt-to-income ratio** (*relación deuda-ingreso*): debt payments divided by income.

## Protection

- **Scam** (*estafa*): deception to get money or information.
- **Phishing:** fake messages or pages that ask for information.
- **Identity theft** (*robo de identidad*): using your information to impersonate you.
- **Credit freeze** (*congelamiento de crédito*): stops new accounts from being opened in your name.
- **Multifactor authentication** (*autenticación de varios pasos*): a second check besides the password.
- **Notary public** (*notario público*): in the U.S. only certifies signatures; not a lawyer.
- **Accredited representative** (*representante acreditado*): authorized by the DOJ to give immigration advice at a recognized organization.
- **Economic abuse** (*abuso económico*): control over another person's resources or economic decisions.
- **Premium** (*prima*): what you pay for insurance.
- **Deductible** (*deducible*): what you pay before the insurance pays.
- **Copayment** (*copago*): a fixed amount for a service.
- **Coinsurance** (*coaseguro*): the percentage you pay after the deductible.
- **Exclusion** (*exclusión*): what the insurance does not cover.
- **Explanation of benefits** (*explicación de beneficios*): summary of how a visit was processed; not a bill.
- **Contingency plan** (*plan de contingencia*): steps prepared to respond to a problem.
- **Family preparedness plan** (*plan de preparación familiar*): instructions so your family can carry on if you cannot be there.
- **Caregiver's Authorization Affidavit** (*declaración jurada de autorización del cuidador*): a California document for a minor's school and certain medical matters; it does not change custody.

## Wealth, housing and the future

- **Contribution** (*aportación*): money you add to an account or goal; not a gain.
- **Return** (*rendimiento*): gain or loss on an investment.
- **Real return** (*rendimiento real*): adjusted for inflation.
- **Diversification** (*diversificación*): spreading risk; it does not eliminate losses.
- **Volatility** (*volatilidad*): how much a value goes up and down.
- **ETF:** a fund bought and sold on the stock exchange.
- **Retirement account** (*cuenta de retiro*): a structure with tax and withdrawal rules.
- **Employer match** (*aportación patronal*): what your employer puts in.
- **Vesting** (*adquisición de derechos*): conditions for the employer contribution to become yours.
- **CalSavers:** California's retirement program.
- **AFORE:** retirement fund administrator in Mexico.
- **Down payment** (*enganche*): the upfront payment when buying.
- **Closing costs** (*costos de cierre*): expenses of buying a home.
- **Rent cap** (*tope de renta*): the maximum allowed increase.
- **Beneficiary** (*beneficiario*): the person who receives a benefit.
- **Power of attorney** (*poder notarial*): authorizes someone to act for you.
- **EIN:** a business's tax number.
- **Total cost of ownership** (*costo total de uso*): everything it costs to have and use something.
