# Where to find help

Each option has a different role: a regulator does not replace a tax preparer or an attorney. Before sharing documents, confirm language, cost, scope and credentials. This information was verified as of September 27, 2026; confirm that it is still current.

| Need | Where | Cost | What to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tax preparation | IRS VITA and TCE | Free for those who qualify | Site, language, whether they help with ITINs |
| Tax help in California | FTB free help and CalFile | Free depending on program | That year's requirements |
| Verify a preparer | CTEC (California) and the IRS directory | Free | Current registration |
| Immigration advice | Licensed attorneys and DOJ-accredited representatives | Varies; free options exist | License with the State Bar or accreditation on the DOJ (EOIR) list |
| General legal help | State Bar of California certified lawyer referral services | The consultation may have a cost | Service certification and license |
| Report notario fraud | State Bar, California Attorney General, FTC | Free | Keep contracts and receipts |
| Identity theft | FTC IdentityTheft.gov | Free | Go directly; avoid imitators |
| Financial complaints | CFPB and DFPI | Free | Your complaint reference number |
| Remittance company licenses | DFPI and NMLS Consumer Access | Free | Exact legal name |
| Banks and credit unions | FDIC BankFind and NCUA locator | Free | Legal name and insurance |
| Build credit with tandas | Mission Asset Fund and other lending circles | Free | Which agencies they report to |
| Housing | HUD-certified housing counselors (CFPB locator) | Free or low cost | Agency and service area |
| Insurance | California Department of Insurance (CDI) | Free | Insurer's and agent's license |
| Health plans | DMHC Help Center | Free | Whether your plan is under its oversight |
| Wages and worker rights | Department of Industrial Relations (DIR) | Free | Keep hours and pay stubs |
| Your own business | Small Business Development Centers (SBDC) | Free or low cost | Advice in Spanish |
| Retirement | CalSavers | Free | Participation requirements |
| Consular financial guidance | Financial Advice Windows at Mexican consulates | Free | Hours and appointments |

**Private preparers.** You can compare them using the same approach. One example in San Diego is ATAX Chula Vista Downtown, which advertises tax services, including ITIN help; confirm the full fees, the credential of the person who will help you and the scope. Appearing on this list is not a recommendation or a partnership.

## Script to ask for information

"I live in California and need help with [topic]. Do you handle this case in Spanish? What credential does the person who will help me have? What is the total cost and what does it not include? What documents do I need and how do I send them safely? Is there a free or low-cost option?"

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I study without an SSN or ITIN?** Yes. We use a program ID and made-up cases. Whether you can use a financial product is a separate question.

**Do I have to open an account to pass?** No. We assess whether you compare and decide; waiting can be the right decision.

**What if I can't save?** Identify your gap, your priorities and the available support. We do not require an amount or blame your habits for everything.

**Should I stop sending remittances?** No. The goal is to bring together your family support, your capacity and your future.

**Does this course give me immigration advice?** No. Only an attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative can give it. We help you find reliable help.

**When will my score improve?** No one can promise a date or a number of points. We work with accurate information, sustainable payments and follow-up.

**Can I use made-up information?** Yes. We assess your reasoning; you do not need to reveal your identity, health or finances.

**What do I do if a source changed?** Flag it, check the current version and do not use an outdated fee as if it were current.

**Is the certificate officially accredited?** It is the program's own certificate, verifiable online. It is not a license or an official accreditation.

**Do I have to leave a review?** No. Your feedback and follow-up are voluntary and do not affect your access or your certificate.

**Can I request a video call?** We offer question sessions according to the published availability. Cases that need a professional are referred.

**How do I send a question?** Include the module, the situation without sensitive data, what you tried and your specific question. If there is fraud in progress or a medical emergency, use the right emergency channel first.
