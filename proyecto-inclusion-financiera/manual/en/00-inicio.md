# Welcome

**Your Money, Your Family, Your Future** is a free personal finance program for immigrants living in California. You will learn to organize your money, send remittances at a lower cost, build your credit, protect yourself from fraud and prepare your future, here and in your home country.

You do not need to know anything about finance to begin. Each lesson starts with an everyday situation, explains technical words when they appear and ends with an action you can take this week.

Version 2.1 · English edition · September 2026. A Spanish edition with the same content is available.

# How to use this manual

## Every lesson has the same structure

| Part | What it is for | Approximate time |
|---|---|---|
| **The essentials** | The main idea with an example and short steps. If you only have a few minutes, read this part. | 3 to 5 minutes |
| **Go deeper** | The full explanation, with calculations and more complex situations. | 8 to 12 minutes |
| **Cases** | Realistic situations to apply what you learned. | 5 minutes |
| **Interactive activity** | A game or simulator on the platform (drag and drop, choose a path, calculate). | 5 minutes |
| **Quiz** | Three quick questions with answers and explanations. | 3 minutes |
| **Practice** | An exercise to solve before you see the answer. | 5 minutes |
| **Before you act, check** | What you should confirm before making a real decision. | 1 minute |
| **For your plan** | A concrete action for your life. | Varies |

At the end of each lesson you will find the **key words** (with their meaning) and the **sources**.

## Three program rules

1. **You can use made-up data.** We will never ask for your Social Security number (SSN), your ITIN, your immigration status, passwords or account numbers.
2. **You do not have to buy anything.** You do not need to open accounts, sign up for products or write a review to learn or to receive your certificate.
3. **Waiting can be a good decision.** Comparing and deciding not to sign up is also learning.

## How to move forward

- Follow the modules in order, or start with what is most urgent for you. If you start with Module 3 because you have a debt, you can come back to Module 1 later.
- Try the exercise before reading the answer.
- Write down one decision in your plan at the end of each lesson.
- At the end of each module, solve the **integrative case**. We use it to issue your certificate.

## What is assessed

We assess whether you understand, calculate, make reasoned decisions and document your next step. We do **not** assess how much money you have, your immigration status or whether you signed up for a product.

## Certificate

When you pass each module you receive a **verifiable digital certificate** from the program, and when you pass all five, a program diploma. It is the program's own educational recognition: it is not a professional license or an official accreditation.

## Information changes

Fees, requirements and rules change. Every piece of information about products or institutions has a **verification date**. Before you act, confirm the current information with the institution or in the updated comparison matrix on the platform. Examples with numbers are simulations for learning; they are not offers or personal recommendations.

## When to get professional help

This program teaches you to understand, compare and prepare questions. For a tax, legal, immigration, insurance or investment situation that needs a personal recommendation, look for an authorized professional. The **Where to find help** section lists free and low-cost options.

**Important:** this program **never** gives immigration advice. Only an attorney or a representative accredited by the Department of Justice can give it.

## Meet Alex and Mar

Throughout the program you will follow **Alex and Mar**, a couple who live in California with their two children. Alex works in construction and is paid every week. Mar cleans houses, is paid in cash and sometimes through an app. They send money to Alex's mother in Michoacán. Their decisions, mistakes and successes will help you practice. They are fictional characters: any resemblance to a real family is a coincidence.
